Welcome to Medbase Frauenfeld

Our versatile medical center with its own ultrasound and X-ray is centrally located in the Thurgau cantonal capital and is well connected to public transport. Around 20 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine, gynecology or dermatology, physiotherapy or manual medicine work closely together interprofessionally. After consultation, we are also happy to carry out home visits. Our flexible working models and deputy solutions enable a good work-life balance.

You can move that

In your training for the MPA EFZ, you will get to know all facets of practical work and become part of a supportive team. Medbase accompanies you in your education and gives you space to develop your strengths.

  • Reception and care of patients and preparation of examinations
  • Support of doctors during the consultation and during interventions
  • Independent performance of laboratory tests and X-rays
  • Administrative tasks and appointment coordination
  • Self-employment of various tasks

What you bring

  • Good academic achievements in secondary school A/level E
  • Interest in medicine and pleasure in contact with people
  • Empathy and reliability
  • Careful appearance and communicative personality
  • Willingness to work independently
  • Good knowledge of German (minimum B2 level)

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can concentrate fully on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, Dental Center.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
