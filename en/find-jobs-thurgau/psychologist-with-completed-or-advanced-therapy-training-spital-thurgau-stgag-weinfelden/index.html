Full description

For our team outpatient family therapy we are looking for from 1 January 2027 or by appointment a

Psychologist with completed or advanced therapy training

80%

If you are interested in systemic thinking and resource-oriented working with children, adolescents and their families, then we offer a unique working environment at the KJPD in Weinfelden.

They shall take over

  • Independent case management and implementation of outpatient family therapy under present supervision by the therapeutic director
  • Implementation of outpatient family-based treatment of eating disorders (Anorexia Nervosa and Bulimia Nervosa), also under fallup vision

They bring with them

  • Clinical experience in child and adolescent psychiatry would be desirable
  • Completed or advanced psychotherapy training desirable
  • Low-frequency home visits are often part of the treatment, driving license (category B) is required
  • Independence, flexibility and resilience
  • A certain curiosity for working with patients with eating disorders

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau (PDT) consists of the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen, the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry and the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service. PDT is part of the successful Spital Thurgau AG with more than 4'100 employees. Company locations are located in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.

The Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) Thurgau offers excellent conditions for classical child and adolescent psychiatric as well as comprehensive psychotherapeutic training. Every year, we treat more than 3,000 patients with high quality standards in family-friendly, outpatient, visiting and day-clinical settings, if possible with evidence-based therapy procedures (Multisystemic Therapy MST, Parent Child Interaction Therapy PCIT, ESDM for early intervention in autism). An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the KJPD a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.

The Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) together with the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. With over 280 beds and specialized offerings in the areas of psychotherapy, general and acute psychiatry, addiction disorders, age psychiatry and forensics, we offer evidence-based and qualitatively optimal therapies for the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases. An appreciative culture combined with excellent education and training make PKM a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.

The Ambulante Adult Psychiatry (AEP) together with the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. Every year, we treat more than 7,500 patients and offer specialized outpatient, outreach and daily clinical services in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the AEP as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau a highly attractive employer.
