Full description

Back HR specialist (wage) Permanent employment / Frauenfeld We are looking for an HR specialist for a successful and modern company that attaches great importance to reliable and exact wage administration. To strengthen the existing team, we are looking for an experienced and committed specialist in the field of HR Payroll. They take over the independent implementation of the monthly wage runs in SAP HCM and ensure compliance with all legal and internal requirements in the area of wages and social insurance. You maintain and control the wage-relevant master data and mutations, create and check evaluations, statistics and certificates and actively participate in the quality assurance and the continuous optimization of the payroll processes. In addition, you support the team in financial statements, audits and internal controls.

What you should bring

  • Commercial basic education with further training in human resources or payroll
  • (e.g. personal assistant, payroll specialist or HR specialist with Federal FA)
  • Several years of experience in contract processing, ideally with SAP HCM
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss social security and withholding tax law
  • Strong affinity for numbers, structured and exact working methods
  • Service-oriented personality with pleasure in working together in a team
