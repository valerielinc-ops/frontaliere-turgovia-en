Full description

Are you excited about technology? You keep heating and water running. As a service technician: in heating & sanitary you are in daily use with our customers. You ensure that heating and sanitary facilities function reliably, repair faults independently and advise our customers competently on site. Thanks to your expertise, our customers quickly return to heat, comfort and security of supply.

You can move that with us.

  • Maintenance, inspection and maintenance of heating and sanitary facilities
  • Analysis and correction of disturbances to heat generation and sanitation systems
  • Repair and servicing services of heat pumps, heating systems, water treatment and sanitation installations
  • Implementation of placing in service and functional and security checks
  • Replacement of components and implementation of minor conversions
  • Advising and supporting our private, commercial and industrial customers
  • Digital reporting of the work carried out
  • Participation in the Pikettdienst according to operational planning

That's what makes you.

  • Completed training as heating installer EFZ, sanitary installer EFZ or comparable training in building technology
  • Ideally experience in the service and maintenance sector
  • Knowledge of modern heating systems such as heat pumps, district heating and heating systems
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Customer-oriented appearance and pleasure in direct customer contact
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B driving licence

That's what we offer you.

  • Modernly equipped service vehicle
  • Self-responsible work with a lot of freedom
  • Exciting and varied assignments in your region
  • Attractive employment conditions and very good social benefits
  • Support for training and specialist courses
  • Collegial team with strong cohesion
  • Long-term perspective in a successful building technology company

You are looking for more than just a service job. You want to take responsibility, inspire customers and make an important contribution with your know-how. With us, modern work equipment, exciting challenges and a team that holds together await you. Become part of our team. We look forward to meeting you.
