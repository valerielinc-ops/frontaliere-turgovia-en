Description of post

As a Maintenance Officer, you ensure the good condition of the installations, equipment and interior and exterior spaces. Thanks to your work, the restaurant remains functional, secure and welcoming – for both guests and the team.

Key responsibilities

  • Perform daily checks and minor repairs
  • Repair or replace simple components (bulbs, seats, handles, small failures)
  • Maintain outdoor spaces: entrances, bins, parking
  • Monitor equipment and report any anomalies
  • Support the cleaning team for certain technical tasks
  • Meet hygiene, safety and maintenance standards
  • Coordinate with external technicians if necessary

Qualifications required

  • Basic maintenance interest or skills
  • Responsible, autonomous and proactive attitude
  • Good organization and focus
  • Basic knowledge of safety rules
  • No experience required — complete training provided
  • An experience in maintenance or DIY is an asset
