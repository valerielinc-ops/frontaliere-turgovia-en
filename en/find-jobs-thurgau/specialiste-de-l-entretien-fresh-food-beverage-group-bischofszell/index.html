Maintenance Specialist — Fresh Food & Beverage Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We produce favorite products that inspire – in Switzerland and for Switzerland. In this varied activity, you combine your passion for food with your technical know-how. Become part of our team and ensure that our machines and equipment for potato and frozen products always function reliably and efficiently.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Bischofszell, Industriestrasse 1, 9220 Bischofszell

Mansioni

  • Maintenance, maintenance and repair work on our production facilities
  • Disturbance diagnosis and troubleshooting
  • Preventive maintenance and operation of equipment
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable guidelines and legal requirements
  • Cooperation in new installations and renovation work
  • Participation in the optimization of processes

Requirements

  • 2-3 years: in industrial maintenance, of advantage in the food industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in a mechanical field
  • Readiness for 2-shift work as well as cooking at night and weekends
  • Teamwork and independent, exact working method
  • Analytical way of thinking and acting
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Employee offers: Employee Points Cumulus, Discount Mobile Subscriptions, Higher Interest Rates Migros Bank, Club School/Fitness contributions and more.
  • Du culture: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are per you!

Contatto

Application and contact David Biedermann Recruiter / HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 100 %
