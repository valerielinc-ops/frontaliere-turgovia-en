Full description

In this exciting job, you will combine your enjoyment of dealing with food and your technical understanding. If you enjoy taking on the challenges of the food industry on a daily basis, we look forward to receiving your application.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Bischofszell, Industriestrasse 1, 9220 Bischofszell

Important notes

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Filling of jams, fruit ingredients, marmalades and sauces
  • Labeling and labeling of manufactured items
  • Palletizing of finished products
  • Operating, monitoring and cleaning the various systems and machines
  • Implementation of quality controls
  • Compliance with production planning and hygiene guidelines
  • General assistance in production

Props

  • Several years of industry experience: Food production
  • Eidg. Certificate: Food
  • Completed apprenticeship in the food sector (cook, butcher, cheesemaker, etc.) or equivalent knowledge through several years of industrial experience in the food sector
  • Willingness to work shifts and Saturdays
  • SUVA recognized forklift test
  • Good technical understanding
  • Reliable and precise working methods as well as a high level of hygiene and quality awareness
  • PC Knowledge
  • German (business fluent)

Cosa offriamo

  • Employee offers: Cumulus employee points, discounted mobile subscriptions, higher interest rates Migros Bank, club school/fitness fees and more.
  • Occupational pensions: Comprehensive protection in the Migros Pension Fund – with first-class and attractive benefits.
  • Cumulus points: 3x points on food, 6x points on all other products.
  • Holidays: 5 weeks holiday for everyone, 6 weeks holiday from 50. Age year, 7 weeks holiday from 60. Age year, purchase of additional vacation days possible.
  • Du-Kultur: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are on a first-name basis!

Contatto

Application & Contact Silvana Bickel Business Partner HR

Recruitment Process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We review incoming applications on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release it to the manager for viewing. The decision on whether to advance or leave the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or e-mail. Duration until re-registration: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with HR & specialist department (FFB Group): The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. The main aim is to learn more about personnel and professional skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit of the company and team.
  • Trial work: A trial day or half-day gives us the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Issuance (FFB-Group): After our verbal job offer, the most important points of the contract are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post.

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Grade of occupancy: 100%
