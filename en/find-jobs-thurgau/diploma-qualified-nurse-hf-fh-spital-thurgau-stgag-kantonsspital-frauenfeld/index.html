You take over

In coronary angiography

  • Monitoring and care of cardiological patients with acute coronary syndrome before, during and after coronary angiography / coronary dilation / stent insert and various structural interventions such as PFO, ASD defect / closure
  • Independent work on and with hemodynamics and securing patient data
  • Monitoring critical patients in a technically demanding environment
  • Administration of div i.v. medicines, including emergency medicines
  • Documentation during interventions
  • Active participation in resuscitations

On the Medical Bed Station

  • completed vocational training as dipl. Nursing professional and professional experience in an acute hospital in Switzerland

  • Commitment to the care, advice and support of patients

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
