  • Completed training at university level with a focus on electrical engineering or physics
  • Knowledge / interest in electrical drive technology or rail vehicle technology
  • Initial experience with topics in the area of EMC (interference currents and network effects, electromagnetic interference emissions)
  • Experience in the field of measurement technology
  • Interest in practical measurements on our vehicles
  • Good knowledge of German and English at level B2, both spoken and written TASKS
  • Conception and integration of the electrical drive systems (pantograph, Power electronics, transformer, motor, energy storage) in rail vehicles -Technical coordination with other disciplines (software creation, installation planning, mechanical engineering, purchasing) -Collaboration with customers, suppliers and, if necessary, experts -Creation of EMC concepts, EMC requirement specifications and EMC test specifications -Accompaniment of type tests and EMC acceptance measurements of our vehicles -Support and analysis of technical problems in operation
