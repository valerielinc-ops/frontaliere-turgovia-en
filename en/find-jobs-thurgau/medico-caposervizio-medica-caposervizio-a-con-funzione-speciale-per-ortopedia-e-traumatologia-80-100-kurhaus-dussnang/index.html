Senior physician (a) mbF for orthopaedics and traumatology 80–100 % Location: Dussnang rehabilitation clinic Where your job does rehab. State-of-the-art robotic therapy for musculoskeletal and geriatric rehabilitation, surrounded by beautiful parkland. The first gerontotraumatological rehabilitation department in Switzerland. The environment at the Dussnang rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 270 employees. Here, advanced medicine meets flat hierarchies and a lot of room for manoeuvre. Through close exchange with care and therapy, you and your ideas advance. Here you have the time, resources and team to achieve great therapeutic success. Develop your field of activity and research in a friendly climate

You can look forward to this

  • Assuming medical treatment responsibility and comprehensive medical care for our inpatients.
  • Active participation in and further development of our interdisciplinary treatment programs with focus on individual rehabilitation goals and modern care concepts.
  • Access to a certified continuing education institution for geriatrics and related disciplines, structured continuing education programmes and recognised certifications, including in FLS and as an osteoporosis centre.
  • Freedom for own research projects, diagnostic and therapeutic projects as well as strategic participation in innovative developments, especially in the field of robotics-assisted therapies.
  • An attractive salary and bonus system, generous additional benefits and flexible working time models.

We are pleased about this

  • Swiss recognised specialist title Orthopaedics and Traumatology
  • Strong interest in general medical and rehabilitative issues as well as internistic and geriatric diseases
  • Team player with quick comprehension, independent working methods and a sense of responsibility
  • Experience in rehabilitation medicine and interest in supervision and further training of assistant doctors
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least B2)
