TASKS After a training period at our main office in Bussnang, you will take over the site management in Basel for the delivery and warranty phase of rail vehicles. During your training period, you will be trained and trained in the testing and commissioning of electrical and electromechanical groups (hardware and software). After acquiring the know-how in Bussnang, your tasks at the site will be as follows:
  • Site management
  • Organization, planning, and responsibility for the site
  • Troubleshooting and processing of warranty and maintenance points, as well as the collection of required measurement data on the vehicles
  • Conducting test runs
  • Recording of operational data of the vehicles (RAM)
  • Responsibility for the complete documentation of test and measurement results, as well as assistance in optimizing the vehicles
  • Close cooperation and coordination with our customers, technical departments, suppliers, and employees
  • Material disposition and warehouse management
