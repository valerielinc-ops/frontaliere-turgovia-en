Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with almost 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Development of a production planning and control system (MES) for the Smart Factory
  • Mapping the entire manufacturing process and material flow through the factory
  • Creation of dashboards for real-time visualization of machine and process data
  • Creation, visualization and implementation of software architecture proposals
  • Joint development of the backend in .NET/C#, creation of the user interface in modern web technologies and subsequent implementation of exploratory and automated tests
  • Active participation in an agile Scrum team
  • Regular customer and user contact

What we are looking for

  • Successfully completed study of computer science, software engineering or a comparable degree program
  • Ideally, professional or project experience in the development of modern web applications, experience in the use of front- and back-end technologies and interest in architecture
  • Good programming skills in C# and .NET
  • Ideally experience in front-end development with Angular/HTML5
  • First experience in dealing with Microsoft Azure cloud technology is an advantage
  • Agile mindset and enthusiasm for agile development methods
  • Enthusiasm for Industry 4.0, IoT, Smart Factory
  • Initiative, analytical skills and a structured and independent working method
  • Strength of communication, teamwork and very good English skills
  • Innovative thinking, openness and willingness to learn about new technologies and willingness to take responsibility
  • Interest in AI-supported SW development, prior knowledge here is an advantage

What we offer

  • Access to the latest technologies and cutting-edge development environments
  • Early assumption of responsibility, independent work and varied tasks
  • Working Where Others Vacation
  • Respectful and appreciative corporate culture

Did we convince you?
