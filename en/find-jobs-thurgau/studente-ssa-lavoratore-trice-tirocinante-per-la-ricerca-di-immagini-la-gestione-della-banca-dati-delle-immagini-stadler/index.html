Full description

PROFILE

  • Affinity to visual material, visual communication and technical products
  • Structured and independent working methods
  • Communication strength in dealing with internal contacts
  • Interest in photography
  • First experience with image databases (e.g. Eyebase) is advantageous
  • Basic knowledge of Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom etc. desirable
  • Interest in mobility, technology and marketing

TASKS

  • Support of the Stadler Group marketing team
  • Development of a strategy for the procurement and provision of image material of our vehicles & products
  • Analysis of image requirements in the marketing context
  • Comparison with our image database (eyebase) to identify existing material
  • Determination of missing or particularly sought-after image motifs
  • Contacting internal stakeholders to request image material
  • Organisation and implementation of photo shoots
  • Participation in the development of a campaign for a photo competition to generate additional images from the user's point of view
  • Viewing, selection and simple retouching of the supplied image material (Adobe Photoshop/Lightroom)
  • Tagging and uploading the final images to the image database
  • Documentation of image acquisition and maintenance of the image database structure
