Full description

Sales and people are your passion? Do you want to give your full commitment every day to offer our customers a unique shopping experience? Do you also want a career and internal development opportunities? Then we are looking for you! Together with us, tackle the exciting and versatile tasks in the dynamic environment of the retail trade. Your tasks as an Area Manager

  • Overall corporate responsibility for a region of 3-5 branches in the area BL / AG / SO / LU (with optional extension to Ticino)
  • Recruitment, leadership and development of up to 100 employees
  • Shared responsibility for branch openings
  • Evaluation of key figures and initiation of coordinated measures
  • Ensuring and continuously reviewing branch operations

Do you already have experience as an Area / District Manager? You enter directly into leadership responsibility. After targeted, individual training, you immediately take over the management of your region and actively contribute your experience to our processes.

Are you joining as a trainee? We prepare you intensively for the role in a structured 3-step program:

  • Basic phase: You will be trained in an apprenticeship branch and get to know all sales-relevant tasks from scratch in order to take over the overall responsibility of two branches for 5 weeks.
  • Construction phase: In this phase, you will get to know all departments of our goods distribution center.
  • Deepening phase: You accompany Area Manager to acquire all the necessary knowledge and skills. Afterwards, you take on the overall responsibility for a region for 4 weeks as a representative.

  • High motivation, independence and pleasure in entrepreneurial activity
  • Communicative skills and an eye for the essentials
  • Very good knowledge of German and Italian (min. B2)

Your professional experience – suitable for your start

You bring several years of professional and managerial experience in the retail trade – ideally you have already worked successfully as an area manager or in a comparable role

  • For entry as a trainee:

You are at the beginning of your career after graduation. First practical professional experience in the retail trade is an advantage
