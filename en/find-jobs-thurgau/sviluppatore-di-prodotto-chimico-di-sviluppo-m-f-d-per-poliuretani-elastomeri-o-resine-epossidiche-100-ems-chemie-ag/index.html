Full description

Where do you see yourself?

  • In the independent processing of development projects
  • In the planning and production of new innovative formulations
  • In the examination of the formulations according to customer specifications
  • When supporting operational tests both internally and externally
  • As a point of contact for sales, purchasing and production
  • As a know-how carrier for our customers and suppliers

Who are you and what makes you?

  • You are a chemist or chemical engineer
  • You have at least 2 years of professional experience
  • You are full of ideas and want to realize them
  • You are interested in adhesives and sealants
  • You are familiar with polyurethanes, elastomers or epoxy
  • You already have experience with the automotive industry or comparable industry
  • You have a quick understanding.
  • You recognize the overall context
  • You have a high willingness to perform.
  • You are flexible
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • You use the common IT tools easily
  • You tackle challenges creatively

You can look forward to that.

  • Exciting tasks in an international environment with short decision paths
  • Lots of personal responsibility and attractive management positions
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and own pension fund with good benefits
  • Free parking spaces

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

Under the name EFTEC, we are the world’s leading supplier of adhesives, sealing materials, coatings and the associated application systems for the automotive industry.
