This is what awaits you

With us, the focus is on people and teamwork is important. You are at work with heart and enthusiasm and appreciate the open exchange in the team. In this role, you work closely with our insurance and pension advisors and contribute to a positive customer experience and the satisfaction of our customers.

This is what you do with us

In this function, you coordinate and support the insurance and pension advisors in all sales processes. You take on an active role in this service- and sales-oriented cooperation and ensure optimal support and advice for our customers.

In addition, you are jointly responsible for the cooperative cooperation with our professional brokers and intermediaries.

Create with organizational skill and high awareness of service quality You independently offer, process applications, arrange appointments and are part of the administrative hub of the Kreuzlingen General Agency. You take over the advice and support of the customers at the reception and on the phone and help with the training of the learners.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed commercial training
  • Professional experience in the insurance industry
  • Enjoying direct customer contact and close cooperation in the team
  • Independent and pragmatic working methods
  • Open, empathetic and loyal personality
  • Flowing knowledge of German

Working with us

A young and dynamic team is waiting for you. Together we shape the regional anchorage and maintain an open, collegial exchange. Together we create trust in Mobiliar as the most personal insurance company in Switzerland.

We offer a versatile and varied activity in a motivated and ambitious team. You can expect a modern working environment, excellent working conditions and comprehensive training opportunities. You benefit from a careful training phase and a varied activity in an exciting environment with development potential.

Your place of work is Kreuzlingen and the workload is 60% - 100%. Job as of now or by agreement.
