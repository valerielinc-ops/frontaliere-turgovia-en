Full description

Do you enjoy getting stuck in, like physical work, and mostly prefer working under one roof? Then training in logistics is exactly the right thing for you! With the two-year training program, we enable you to make an easier start in the world of work.

Your training

  • During your training, you will work in one of our modern mail or parcel centers or in a distribution area and take on all kinds of logistics tasks.
  • You operate sorting systems or sort the shipments by hand.
  • Loading and unloading trucks and trains is also part of your duties.
  • You will also learn how to operate a forklift.
  • We complete your broad logistics training with a placement in delivery and a warehouse operation, as well as individual support measures according to your wishes.
  • We also offer you the prospect of pursuing the Passerelle EFZ if you achieve good academic and practical results.

What you bring

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should meet the following requirements:

  • You are physically fit.
  • You enjoy working in a team, performing tasks on machines, as well as manual work.
  • You are reliable, honest and discreet.
  • Irregular working hours may occur from time to time and do not worry you.
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • At the start of your apprenticeship, you have German language proficiency at level B1.
