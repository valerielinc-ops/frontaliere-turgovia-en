Full description

To complement the medical team in our clinic for children and adolescents, we offer from spring 2027 a

Fellow/Training Centre for Developmental Paediatrics

50-100%

The Kinderklinik Münsterlingen offers clarifications, advice and therapy in its developmental paediatric center (EPZ) and provides contacts with other specialists. Children are treated from birth to adulthood.

Medical diagnostic and therapeutic services that we offer

  • Assessment and long-term care of children with developmental disabilities
  • Neuropsychological and psychological diagnostics
  • Parental counselling: crisis intervention in cooperation with parents, school, kindergarten and therapists
  • Short-term behavioral therapy treatments, such as concentration training in AD(H)S children

Good cooperation with other services, therapies and schools is very important. The EPZ Thurgau is integrated into the Kinderklinik Münsterlingen and works closely with the KJPD Thurgau.

  • We offer you 12 months of recognized, structured further education incl. Supervision at the Developmental Paediatric Center
  • You do not have to participate in the schedule of the clinic for children and adolescents and can fully concentrate on training in the developmental pediatric center
  • Continued employment after obtaining the specialist/priority title as senior physician is possible

They take over

  • They look after children and their relatives in the developmental paediatric center and carry out investigations, always in collegial exchange with the team of the clinic for children and adolescents & other bodies

They bring with them

  • You have a specialist title in paediatric medicine or are in the last year of training
  • You enjoy clinical activity in a collegial and motivated team
  • You have a strong communication and team skills
  • You like to work independently

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4’100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
