Full description

The technology group Georg Fischer (GF) in Schaffhausen looks back on more than 200 years of history. The Group Archive of GF is one of the most traditional business archives and stands as an archive of national importance in the Swiss cultural property protection inventory. The archive is supervised by the Eisenbibliothek, a foundation of GF. The iron library is one of the world's largest private libraries in the field of technology history.

We offer an internship for initial practical experience after study or to prepare for an I+D degree. You will get to know the versatile activities in a business archive and a scientific special library.

Internship in GF Group Archive and Iron Library (m/f/d, 100%, 9-12 months)

From 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Collaboration in all archival and library areas
  • Assistance in digitalisation
  • Research, user support, answering requests
  • Assistance with activities and events of Eisenbibliothek and Corporate Archive

Your profile

  • Completed Matura or Bachelor's degree
  • Independent and exact working methods
  • Stylistic German, very good knowledge of English
  • Enjoyment of historical contexts and broad general knowledge

The archive and library together maintain the historical and cultural heritage of the GF Group and are part of the economic and social history of Switzerland. Both are based in the Klostergut Paradies in Schlatt (TG), near Schaffhausen. Here is the main workplace

  • Another place of work is the headquarters of GF in Schaffhausen.

For information or questions you can contact Franziska Eggimann, Head of the Eisenbibliothek and GF Corporate Archive, please contact: Tel. +41 52 547 12 99 / www.eisenbibliothek.ch
