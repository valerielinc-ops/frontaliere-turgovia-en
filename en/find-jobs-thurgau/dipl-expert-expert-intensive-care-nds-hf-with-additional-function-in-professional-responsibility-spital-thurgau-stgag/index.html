Full description

For the interdisciplinary intensive care unit with 10 beds, we are looking for an

Dipl. Expert / Expert Intensive Care NDS HF with additional function in professional responsibility

80-100%

The specialist responsibility IPS acts as a link between clinical practice, specialist development and leadership. It contributes to ensuring high-quality, evidence-based intensive care and supports the further development of structures, processes and nursing skills.

You take on

  • Holistic, evidence-based and high-quality care and care of intensive care patients
  • Professional support and advice for relatives in demanding situations
  • Performing the function as shift management and active participation in three shift operations
  • Professional support of the care team in the nursing treatment process of patients and their relatives
  • Supporting the implementation, implementation and continuous development of tools to ensure nursing and medical quality
  • Support for the introduction and evaluation of care concepts and standards
  • Active participation in clinical improvement and innovation processes within the framework of projects
  • Engaged, appreciative and interprofessional cooperation in the treatment team

You bring with you

  • Diploma recognized as an expert / expert intensive care NDS HF or equivalent
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH or equivalent degree is a plus
  • Several years of professional experience in an intensive care unit of a Swiss acute care hospital
  • Strong professional, communicative and social competence
  • Great interest in innovation, quality development and clinical process design
  • Good digital skills and safe handling of clinical information systems
  • High personal responsibility, resilience and decision-making capacity

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
