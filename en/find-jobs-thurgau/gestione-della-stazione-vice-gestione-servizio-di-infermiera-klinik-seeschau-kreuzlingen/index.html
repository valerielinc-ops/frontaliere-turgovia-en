Station management / Deputy management Nursing service

Do you appreciate the familiarity of a small acute care hospital with 56 beds and around 120 employees?

Would you like to actively participate in our care team in a beautiful location and help shape this area? Then we may soon welcome you to Kreuzlingen.

Their tasks

They work in an interdisciplinary nursing department and, as part of the nursing squad, are jointly responsible for the organization and coordination of work processes and personnel planning. Together with the management care you lead a team of about 30 nurses as well as the cleaning team of about 10 people. As a competent contact person, you work alongside your management tasks directly in the care and carry out a professional pre- and postoperative basic and treatment care.

Your profile

We want a socially competent, team-oriented and positioned personality with leadership experience, ideal with several years of professional experience in our specialist areas as well as in the recovery room (IMCU) / day clinic. They act responsibly and are skilful in communication, lead in a straightforward, appreciative and socially competent manner. They like to work across interfaces, think long-term and are open to change processes and their participation.

Our offer

With us you will find a family atmosphere in a meaningful environment. She expects a very varied and demanding field of activity with a well-established and motivated team as well as a working atmosphere characterized by collegiality and teamwork.

About us

Klinik Seeschau AG is a modern private clinic in the document doctor system with a cantonal service mandate. Our areas of expertise are in gynecology, orthopaedics, visceral surgery, hand surgery, plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery, urology and anesthesiology/pain therapy. We are a member of the renowned association “SLH, The Swiss Leading Hospitals”, independently and privately managed in the third generation.
