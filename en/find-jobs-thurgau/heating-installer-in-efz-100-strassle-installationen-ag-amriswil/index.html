Full description

You like to work independently, are solution-oriented and want to help shape varied projects? We look forward to meeting you.

For our major projects, industrial plants as well as new and conversions, we are looking for a heating installer in EFZ 100% to strengthen our team.

Your tasks

  • Install, install and commission heating systems and systems
  • Execution of heating installations for new constructions and conversions
  • Laying and connecting lines and mounting heat generators and radiators
  • Cooperation with project and construction management and other craftsmen
  • Service, maintenance and repair work
  • Collaboration in the Pikettdienst
  • Accompany and support learners in everyday work

This is what you bring with you

  • Heating installer training
  • Professional experience of several years
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Independent, reliable and careful working method
  • Team spirit, flexibility and responsibility
  • Friendly and professional approach towards customers and partners

This is what we offer

  • Varied projects in a future-oriented company
  • Collegial and uncomplicated team
  • Modern equipment and high-quality workwear
  • 40-hour week, Fridays until 14:00
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Own company vehicle (depending on function)
  • Training support
  • Above-average pension fund solution
  • Good transport connections and employee events

Ready for the next step? Then we look forward to your application documents preferably by e-mail to: bewerbung@straessle.com.

Andreas Schmidt, Managing Director, is at your disposal for any questions (tel. +41 71 414 07 70).
