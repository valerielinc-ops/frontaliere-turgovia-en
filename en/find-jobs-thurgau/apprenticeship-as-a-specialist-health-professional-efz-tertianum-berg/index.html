About us

Start with us in a varied and meaningful apprenticeship! Your future is close to our hearts. Our experienced vocational trainer accompanies and supports you throughout your training.

The family-run residential and care centre Tertianum Schloss Berg with 45 care beds offers people in the 4th stage of life a loving home. The castle is located in the middle of a large park with a chapel, walkways and pond. The train station can be reached in 10 minutes on foot.

We at Berg Castle are looking for you as of August 1, 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as a health specialist EFZ

What you'll learn with us

  • Care, support and accompaniment of nursing guests in everyday and demanding situations
  • Support with personal hygiene, getting up, dressing and undressing, eating and drinking
  • Medical technical tasks such as measuring blood pressure, pulse and temperature, blood sampling, dressing changes and much more
  • Everyday accompaniment of the guests with games, handicrafts, singing, etc.
  • Electronic care documentation
  • Housekeeping, administrative and logistical tasks
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and safety regulations

What you bring to the table

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our nursing guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of a variety of challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of written and spoken German

Your benefits

We offer a wide range of different benefits, e.g.:

  • SBB Half-Fare travelcard
  • Sunrise discounts
  • Alpamare discounts
  • Fitness perks
  • Insurance benefits
  • Regular team events

Your apprenticeship starts with an exciting introductory day. In the second year of training, our apprentices take part in the Tertianum Summer Camp. We celebrate the completion of the apprenticeship with a fitting graduation ceremony. After the apprenticeship, there are numerous opportunities to work within our company in different companies and language regions.

For more benefits and insights, visit our website.
