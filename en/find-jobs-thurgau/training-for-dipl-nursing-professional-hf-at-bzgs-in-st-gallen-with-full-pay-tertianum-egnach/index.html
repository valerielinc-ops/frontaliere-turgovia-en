Original description

Start your nursing career with us – at Tertianum Seerose in Egnach

In our house in Egnach, you will find a warm team that meets on an equal footing, lives an open 'you' culture, and shapes everyday life together with joy and commitment.

Your path to becoming a qualified nurse HF – with a study place at BZGS St. Gallen with full salary

We offer training positions for the following study models

  • Full-time study starting in March 2027
  • Shortened study for FaGe EFZ starting in September 2026
  • Part-time study (80%) for career changers starting in August 2026

Further information can be found at this link

The training structure for the study can be found at the following link

What you will learn with us

  • Holistic care and support of our residents
  • Taking on the role of a reference person
  • Managing and documenting the nursing process
  • Carrying out therapeutic nursing measures
  • Leading case conferences and nursing teams
  • Planning and accompanying doctor's visits

What you bring

  • A completed three-year basic training (for the shortened study: FaGe EFZ)
  • Joy in dealing with people and practical work
  • Teamwork skills, willingness to learn, and reflective ability
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • Very good German language skills, both spoken and written

Your advantage: funding for your training

Thanks to the Thurgau support program for nursing training HF and FH 22plus, you will receive financial support during your training. All information can be found at: www.odags-thurgau.ch → Support program for nursing training HF and FH 22plus

What awaits you with us

  • A motivated, humorous team that supports each other
  • Space for your personal development and participation
  • Attractive benefits: free half-fare card, discounted fitness subscription, shopping advantages, free snacks and drinks, and much more
  • Additional free day in the month of your birthday

Ready for the next step?

Then we look forward to getting to know you!
