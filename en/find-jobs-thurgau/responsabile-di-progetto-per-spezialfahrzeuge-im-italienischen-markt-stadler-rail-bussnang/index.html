PROFIL You are a maker: with a passion for technology and international projects

  • Completed higher technical training (FH, HF, Uni), ideally complemented by business know-how
  • Several years of experience in project management in vehicle or plant construction
  • Convincing appearance and pronounced negotiating strength in the international environment
