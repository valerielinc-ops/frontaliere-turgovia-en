Full description

The main task is to set up and operate CNC grinding machines. The company offers you a diverse working environment with versatile activities. For our client we are looking for a Activities In this function, you are responsible for setting up and operating CNC grinding machines as well as grinding and polishing workpieces. The maintenance and cleaning of machines and the grinding water plant is also part of your task.

Requirements

  • Completed mechanical training
  • Several years of professional experience on CNC cylindrical grinding machines
  • Experience in CNC loop on Kellenberger - KelVaria is an advantage
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Good PC knowledge
  • German in Word and Writing
  • Independent, reliable and capable of teamwork

Offer You can expect a dynamic environment, a versatile field of activity, a very good hourly wage and the chance of permanent employment.
