Full description

In addition to extended basic internal care, the Spital Thurgau AG Medical Clinic offers a very large range of specialist medical services with broad diagnostic and therapeutic options. For the Medical Clinic at the Münsterlingen Cantonal Hospital, we are looking for a Medical Site Manager / Medical Site Manager Gastroenterology 80-100% immediately or by appointment. You will take over management of gastroenterology at the Münsterlingen Cantonal Hospital with technical and organizational responsibility for all gastroenterological processes as well as the gastroenterological outpatient clinic. Collaboration on the future-oriented further development of gastroenterology at Spital Thurgau AG in close cooperation with the cross-site overall management of gastroenterology STGAG. Co-responsibility for the gastroenterology training center at Münsterlingen Cantonal Hospital (currently Further training center B), as well as commitment to gastroenterological/hepatological teaching of the entire medical profession at the KSM Medical Clinic Active participation in the gastroenterological consultation hours, the care of inpatients with gastroenterological/hepatological diseases Federal or Swiss specialist title and several years of clinical experience in gastroenterology. An additional specialist title in general internal medicine is desired, but not required. Intervention experience and appropriate independent work is desirable, but not a requirement. Enjoys leadership tasks and taking on responsibility Communicative, integrative, consensus and solution-oriented personality who enjoys interdisciplinary and interprofessional collaboration
