Your job with us As part of our leadership team and nursing team, you will combine leadership, organization and nursing practice:

  • You bring your experience to the management team, shape and take responsibility.
  • You will perform the administrative and organizational tasks of your cadre function.
  • You actively develop processes, quality and structures.
  • You work regularly with our clients and stay on the pulse of care and close to the team.

This is you You are a seasoned Spitex personality with qualified nursing training (Dipl. nursing specialist), profound Spitex experience and ideally leadership or cadre experience. Because not everything is set in stone. Our management team is re-creating. You get the chance to contribute your experience, your ideas and your personality and to actively shape our Spitex. We are not simply looking for someone for a function. We are looking for someone who wants to lead, think and tackle – in the management team, in the nursing staff and directly with our clients.
