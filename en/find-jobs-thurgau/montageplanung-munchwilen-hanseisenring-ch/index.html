Companies … Job Portal Assembly planning Assembly planning In this function, you ensure that assembly capacities are cleanly planned, coordinated and complemented in the event of bottlenecks. They support the team in day-to-day business, keep an eye on appointments and help to coordinate construction processes reliably. You work closely with project leaders and check whether stages, stage sizes and schedules fit together. The maintenance of the subcontractor pool and property allocation are also part of your tasks. For this you bring organizational talent, experience in a coordinating function and a careful working method. The most important in short Place of work Münchwilen (TG) Pensum 80 – 100 %

Responsibilities

  • Monitoring and control of the assembly capacities incl. Management of external capacity in the event of bottlenecks
  • Operational support in the day-to-day business of the team
  • Support of project managers in scheduling the construction process
  • Master data maintenance of the subcontractor pool
  • Subcontracting
  • Checking of stages, stage sizes and schedules

What you bring

  • Completed training in construction or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in a coordinating capacity
  • High operational readiness
  • Good IT User Knowledge (MS Office, Outlook)
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
