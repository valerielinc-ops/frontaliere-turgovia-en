Construction manager:in civil engineering and road construction Your contribution with us

  • Working as a construction guide: in the areas of civil engineering, dismantling, earth construction and road construction in the East Switzerland region
  • Independent handling and execution of the construction projects, as well as in the acquisition, processing of offers and preparation of offers
  • Management of the undertakings involved in the construction from the start of construction until the completion of the guarantee work
  • Preparation of work calculation and site results control
  • Responsibility regarding construction site organization, mass production and billing as well as maintenance of work calculation
  • Control of the construction process and the construction site results
  • Implementation and co-responsibility of work preparation and construction site organisation
  • Correct and timely massage and settlement
  • Responsibility for cost, deadline and quality topics
  • Collaboration and participation in innovation topics in construction (e.g. BIM)
