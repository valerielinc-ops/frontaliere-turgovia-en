Full description

For our emergency team in Frauenfeld or Münsterlingen we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a /-n

Assistant doctor emergency

60-100%

The position is ideal for colleagues who want to start or expand their further education with a broad basic education in all surgical subjects. On our interdisciplinary emergency, you can expect a varied everyday life with a lot of personal responsibility. You work in a motivated team with modern infrastructure and closely with surgery, orthopaedics, hand and plastic surgery as well as urology.

They take over

  • Initial assessment, diagnosis and treatment of surgical emergency patients
  • Wound care, fracture treatment, reposition and small surgical procedures
  • Triage and prioritization in collaboration with care
  • Close consultation with the cadre doctors of the specialist disciplines
  • Documentation and organisation of further treatment (inpatient or outpatient)
  • Collaboration in shift and service operations

They bring with them

  • Completed medical studies (Federal medical diploma or MEBEKO-approved)
  • You want to start or expand your further education with a broad basic surgical training
  • Interest in emergency medicine and surgical specialties
  • First clinical experience beneficial, career entrants welcome
  • Very good knowledge of German (minor level C1)
  • Resilience, determination and calm in hectic situations
  • Team-oriented, flexible and patient-centered workflow

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 3,500 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.

We prefer direct applications, job agents please refrain from contacting.
