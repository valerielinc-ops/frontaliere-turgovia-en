Role overview

TASKS

  • Design and integration of the electrical components of on-board systems such as HVAC, boarding systems (doors, stepways) and fire protection systems in railway vehicles
  • Technical coordination and interface management with internal specialist areas (software, installation planning, mechanics, vehicle control technology, purchasing) and external suppliers
  • Creation of system concepts, electrical requirements specifications and integration and test specifications
  • Definition and coordination of electrical interfaces (supply, signals, communication) between subsystems and vehicle
  • Accompaniment of commissionings and type tests with focus on electrical system integration
  • Analysis and correction of technical problems in operation with focus on electrical causes and interfaces
  • Coordination and technical evaluation of suppliers with regard to electrical integration and system behaviour
  • Ensuring the correct integration of the systems into vehicle control technology and energy supply PROFILE
  • Completed studies in electrical engineering, mechatronics or a comparable discipline
  • Knowledge of electrical system integration or rail vehicle technology
  • Experience in dealing with electrical systems, interfaces and their integration into complex overall systems
  • Understanding electrical components, energy supply and vehicle control technology
  • Experience in dealing with interfaces (e.g. signals, bus systems, I/O) is advantageous
  • Readiness to support commissioning and testing directly on the vehicle
  • Structured and system-oriented working methods and strong communication skills
  • Good knowledge of German and English in words and writing (at least level B2)
