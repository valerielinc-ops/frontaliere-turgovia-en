Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Seerücken, a successful and locally anchored bank with offices in Pfyn, Müllheim and Weiningen, we are looking for a responsible, committed and trustworthy personality as Compliance Officer & Team Leader Backoffice.

What do you expect?

Their many tasks include

  • Personnel and professional leadership of the back office team as well as promotion and training of employees and learners
  • Ensure efficient back-office processes, high quality of master data and effective key banking controls
  • Support, advice and reporting to the bank management in the areas of compliance, regulation, governance and corporate governance
  • Responsibility for compliance, data protection and regulatory issues (e.g. regulatory system, crossborder, FATCA and AIA)
  • Independent processing of complex legal special cases such as inheritances, deaths, KESB cases, edition orders, seizures and bankruptcy proceedings
  • Contact person for internal and external audits, authorities and specialist offices in the relevant subject areas

What do we offer you?

  • A responsible and varied key position
  • A motivated and collaborative team with short decision paths
  • Modern working environment and attractive employment conditions
  • Training and education support
  • An open corporate culture with room for initiative and participation
  • The opportunity to actively shape the future of a successful, locally anchored Raiffeisenbank

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training, preferably supplemented by further training in the field of compliance, banking, law or management
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of compliance, anti-money laundering and regulatory requirements in the banking environment
  • Several years of experience in the back office or in a comparable specialist or management function
  • Leadership experience of advantage or willingness to assume leadership responsibility
  • High independence, reliability and flexibility
  • Professionally sound, economic and solution-oriented as well as exact working methods
  • Enjoy working with different stakeholders

In this versatile key position, you will take over the personnel and professional leadership of our back office team and assume responsibility for central compliance, governance and support processes. With your expertise and organizational talent, you make an important contribution to compliance with regulatory requirements and to the efficient handling of our internal processes.
