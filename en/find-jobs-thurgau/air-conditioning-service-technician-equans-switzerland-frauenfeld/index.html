Full description

You don’t just look for mistakes – you find solutions. To strengthen our team Klimaklima, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys technology, independent work and varied tasks. Our team supports refrigeration, air conditioning and heat pump systems in various performance areas.

You can move that with us.

  • Service, maintenance and repair of split and multi-split air conditioning systems
  • Recirculation cooling work
  • Cold water tank service and troubleshooting services
  • Heat pump work
  • Systematic troubleshooting and fault diagnosis
  • Work on refrigeration, electrical, hydraulic and control components
  • Commissioning and optimisation of plants
  • Support of the team for larger refrigeration systems
  • If necessary, cooperation on construction sites in the installation and commissioning of new, modern air conditioning systems

That's what makes you.

  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Ideal is a completed training in refrigeration technology or corresponding practical experience in refrigeration and air conditioning technology, pleasure in troubleshooting and problem solving
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • Interest in refrigeration, air conditioning and heat pump technology
  • Willingness to learn new things and develop professionally
  • Team spirit and mutual support
  • Friendly and professional approach to the customer
  • Cross-starters in electrical engineering are also welcome.
  • Category B driving licence

That's what we offer you.

  • Exciting and varied tasks in a dynamic environment
  • High personal responsibility and scope for design
  • Modern equipment and equipment
  • Attractive training and development opportunities
  • Collegial and supportive teams with an appreciative culture
  • Progressive employment conditions with very attractive social benefits
