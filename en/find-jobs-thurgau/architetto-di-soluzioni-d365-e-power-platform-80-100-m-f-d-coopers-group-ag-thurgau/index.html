Full description

For an established Swiss company with a demanding digital environment, we are looking for an experienced professional as Solution Architect D365 & Power Platform .

In this position, you will shape the further development of a central CRM and Power Platform environment. You combine sound architectural expertise with concrete technical implementation and ensure that requirements are transformed into scalable, sustainable, and well-integrated solutions.

This position is particularly suitable for people who not only design architecture but also want to implement solutions themselves on the platform.

Your responsibilities

  • You design solution architectures based on Microsoft Dynamics 365 and the Microsoft Power Platform.
  • You analyze business requirements and transform them into structured, scalable, and sustainable solutions.
  • You further design and develop data models and process logic.
  • You ensure the interoperability and maintainability of different solutions within the platform.
  • You implement solutions yourself on Dynamics 365 and the Power Platform.
  • You develop workflows and automations using the capabilities of the Power Platform.
  • You define and manage data flows between different systems.
  • You design and implement integrations and extensions.
  • For more complex requirements, Azure Functions may also be used.
  • You further develop the platform structurally and establish standards as well as reusable solutions.
  • You work closely with different specialist departments and technical contacts.

Your profile

  • You have extensive practical experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM .
  • You have very good knowledge of the Microsoft Power Platform and have already implemented corresponding solutions yourself.
  • You have experience in Solution Design or Solution Architecture and can translate business requirements into technically consistent solutions.
  • You have a good understanding of system architectures, data structures, and data models .
  • You understand the interaction between systems as well as the associated integrations and data flows .
  • You enjoy working conceptually while also implementing solutions yourself technically.
  • Experience with workflows, low-code automation, and integration solutions is an advantage.
  • Knowledge of the Azure environment, particularly Azure Functions , is also an advantage.
  • You communicate clearly and enjoy working with different departments and technical stakeholders.

What to expect

You will play a central role within a specialised platform team and have the opportunity to actively shape the CRM and Power Platform landscape.

This is not a purely architectural role. You develop solution approaches, make structural design decisions and see them through to concrete implementation.

You work within a defined technical framework while also having room to shape the functional and structural design of the solutions.

The company offers a hybrid working model with generally three days on-site at the location in the Frauenfeld region and two days working from home. Depending on the project, fixed or additional on-site days may be required.
