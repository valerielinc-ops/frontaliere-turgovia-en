Welcome to Medbase Weinfelden

Our medical center with its own laboratory and X-ray is located in the old town of Weinfelden, is well connected to public transport and can be reached quickly from Konstanz or Winterthur. The team, consisting of 20 employees, specializes in family medicine. We care for patients in a committed and quality-oriented manner. We have different working shifts and part-time work is possible in all functions.

Main goals

  • Ensuring a customer focused and professional service
  • Promoting a high level of patient satisfaction through active participation in quality assurance
  • Ensuring a smooth workflow
  • Ensuring a well-groomed, professional appearance towards patients
  • Working independently is usual or expected
  • Loyalty and correct cooperation with all employees

Main tasks

  • Pharmaceutical advice and care for patients
  • Recipe processing and execution
  • Merchandise management and product range maintenance
  • Warehouse management and administrative tasks

Support for doctors

  • Review and optimization of medication plans

(e.g. duplicate prescriptions, discontinuing outdated medication, carrying out CDS checks)

Support for medical practice assistants (MPAs)

  • Editing, releasing and processing documents and correspondence
  • Reception activities in buffer times:
  • Patient reception
  • Delivery of medication and blood sample
  • Receiving prescriptions and medication orders
  • Instruction of inhalation systems
  • Planning and conducting internal medication training for the MPA team

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to offer people throughout Switzerland integrated, first-class all-round medical care. We support doctors with logistics services and digital solutions so that they can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
