Full description

For Raiffeisenbank Tägerwilen, a successful and locally anchored bank with offices in Kreuzlingen, Tägerwilen, Ermatingen, Siegershausen and Hefenhausen, we are looking for you as an experienced and empathetic leader for team management credit processing.

Our bank operates in an attractive and dynamic market area and offers you an exciting environment with a wide range of development opportunities. Become part of our management team and actively shape the further development of our bank. In this key role, you take responsibility for employees, customers and the successful future of our core process. Tasks: • Leadership and development of a team of credit officers and credit processing staff • Processing of loan applications: Recording and checking of data entry and checking for completeness of documents, preparation of contract documents, timely payment and redemption, filing of fully processed credit dossiers • Management of credit positions, preparation of periodic re-approvals, data collection for balance sheet analysis, construction loan processing, management of mortgage master data • Support for credit counselling • Telephone and written communications with offices, insurance companies and other banks • Co-design of the credit process of the entire bank Requirements • Several years of leadership experience • Completed banking training, knowledge and proven practice in the Swiss credit sector (private and corporate customers) • Service-oriented and networked thinking and acting • Self-employed and exact working methods • Reliability, responsibility and teamwork • Empathic personality • Joy of continuous improvement We offer: Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.
