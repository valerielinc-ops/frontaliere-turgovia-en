Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure.
