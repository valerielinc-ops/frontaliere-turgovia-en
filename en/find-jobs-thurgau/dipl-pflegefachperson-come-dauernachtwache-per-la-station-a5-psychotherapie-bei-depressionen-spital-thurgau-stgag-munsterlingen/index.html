For our station A5 with a focus on psychotherapy for depression, we are looking for 1. February 2027 a dedicated

Dipl. Nursing Professional as a Permanent Night Watch for Station A5 – Psychotherapy for Depression

35%

You value independent work and meet people in psychological crises with peace of mind, understanding and professionalism? Then we look forward to meeting you.

You take over

  • You look after and accompany our patients during the night.
  • You promote a calm and safe station atmosphere and support sleep problems, anxiety and emotional stress.
  • You recognize changes in mental and physical condition and take appropriate measures.
  • You act calmly in the event of crises and emergencies and, if necessary, consult the competent specialists.
  • You carry out medically prescribed measures and document your observations and nursing services.
  • You ensure the flow of information between night and day service through structured transfers.

You bring with you

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or an equivalent diploma recognised in Switzerland.
  • Ideally, experience in psychiatric care and night service.
  • Interested in accompanying people with depression.
  • An independent, responsible and reliable way of working.
  • Empathy, clear communication and a professional approach to proximity and distance.
  • The ability to act calmly and deliberately even in challenging situations.
  • Willingness to cooperate with the interprofessional team.

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves

The Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) together with the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. With over 280 beds and specialized offerings in the areas of psychotherapy, general and acute psychiatry, addiction disorders, age psychiatry and forensics, we offer evidence-based and qualitatively optimal therapies for the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases. An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make PKM a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.
