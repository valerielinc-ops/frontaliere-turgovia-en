For the house Kronenhof in Berlingen we are looking for an

Medical practice assistant / Medical practice assistant EFZ for the management of the pharmacy in geriatric rehabilitation 50-100 %

Your tasks

  • Management of in-house pharmacy
  • Ensuring compliance with all legal requirements in the pharmacy sector
  • Directing and controlling medicines for our patients
  • Implementation of drug orders
  • Medical day-to-day inspections of in-house diagnostic devices
  • Conduct of diagnostic examinations (X-rays and ECG)

Your tasks are supplemented by the variety and the use at the reception, the billing, as well as in the medical secretariat in the month change. A diverse area with us in the Neutal.

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant EFZ
  • Competence for dose-intensive X-ray examinations of advantage or willingness to acquire them
  • High sense of responsibility and strong teamwork
  • Structured, reliable and independent working method
  • Humor as well as the ability to keep an overview even in a lively everyday practice
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
