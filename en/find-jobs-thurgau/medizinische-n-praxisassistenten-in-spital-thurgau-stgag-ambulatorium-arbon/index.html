Medical practice assistants

Are you looking for a varied job and would like to develop further in the field of dialysis? Do you enjoy working in a team and appreciate the contact with patients? Then you are exactly right with us!

Have you become curious? Then we look forward to meeting you and shaping the future of our dialysis in Arbon together with you!

  • A warm and committed team

  • Team spirit, a sense of responsibility and an independent working method
