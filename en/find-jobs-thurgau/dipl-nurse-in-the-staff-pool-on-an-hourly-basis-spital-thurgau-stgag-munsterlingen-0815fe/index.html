Full description

In order to strengthen our nursing teams in the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen and in the outpatient adult psychiatry, we are looking for an employee pool.

Dipl. Nursing professional in the employee pool hourly wage

10-100%

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau are part of Spital Thurgau AG and offer integrated psychiatric care for all ages and for all disorders in the Canton of Thurgau.

They shall take over

  • As a pool employee, you will be used during labour-intensive phases and during staff shortages. This requires a high degree of flexibility. As part of your assignment, you will be used at all stations of the psychiatric clinic and the outpatient adult psychiatry.

They bring with them

  • We are addressing nurses with professional experience in psychiatry who would like to return to work as a side job or after a professional break, maternity or similar and are willing to perform assignments of different durations at short notice according to personal expectations. They work on an hourly basis with a changing workload depending on the needs of the respective stations. We are happy to explain to you during an interview the modalities of employment and assignment planning.

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves to you

The Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) together with the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. With over 280 beds and specialized offerings in the areas of psychotherapy, general and acute psychiatry, addiction diseases, age psychiatry and forensics, we offer evidence-based and qualitatively optimal therapies for the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases. An appreciative culture combined with excellent education and training make PKM a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.
