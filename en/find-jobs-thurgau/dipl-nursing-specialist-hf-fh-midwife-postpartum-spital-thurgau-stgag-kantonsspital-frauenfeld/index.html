For our modern family department, we are looking for a

Dipl. Nursing specialist/woman HF/FH, midwife postpartum

80-100%

Accompaniment of families in the postpartum is your passion? Do you want to accompany families in a quiet, appreciative and self-determined postpartum bed in the first days after birth? Then you shape the future of care with us: In our new family-centered family department, we focus on the needs of mother, child and family. Nurses and midwives work together multiprofessionally and jointly design a modern, holistic care in the early week bed.

You take over

  • Care of mothers and their newborns
  • Care and monitoring of women with complications in pregnancy
  • Participation in the training of students/learners
  • Active participation in the further development of care

You bring with you

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH
  • Training as a Dipl. Midwife HF/FH
  • You are a team player with an empathetic personality
  • Professional experience in the field of week bed is desirable
  • Strong quality and service awareness
  • Open and respectful attitude towards our patients and relatives
  • They are resilient, flexible and routine in the application of electronic aids

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself to you

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
