Role overview

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen belong to Spital Thurgau AG (STGAG), one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland.

Both locations have the most modern infrastructure in the perioperative areas.

The newly cross-site-led Institute for Anesthesia, Intensive Care and Rescue Medicine provides anesthesiological care for a broad spectrum in the surgical and interventional specialist areas with around 20,000 anesthesias annually across the two acute hospitals and the Bellevue Venous Clinic in Kreuzlingen, which belongs to the thurmed group and thus plays a central role in perioperative medicine.
