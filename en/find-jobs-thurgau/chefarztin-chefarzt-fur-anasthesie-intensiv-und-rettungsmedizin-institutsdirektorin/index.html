Full description

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen belong to Spital Thurgau AG (STGAG), one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland. Both locations have the most modern infrastructure in the perioperative areas. The newly cross-location-led Institute for Anesthesia, Intensive Care and Rescue Medicine provides anesthesiological care for a broad spectrum in the surgical and interventional specialist areas with around 20,000 anesthesias annually across the two acute hospitals and the Bellevue Venous Clinic in Kreuzlingen, which belongs to the thurmed group and thus plays a central role in perioperative medicine. The Institute is also responsible for the two interdisciplinary intensive care units with a total of 22 treatment stations, the Centre for Pain Medicine and preclinical emergency and rescue medicine. The various departments of the institute enjoy an excellent reputation as training centres for anesthesiology (cat. A2), intensive care medicine (cat. A/B), pain medicine SSIPM and preclinical emergency medicine (emergency physician) SGNOR.

As part of the succession planning of the long-term chief physician at the Canton Hospital Münsterlingen, we are looking for a professionally and humanly convincing leader as of mid-2027 or by agreement.

Chief physician for anesthesia, intensive care and rescue medicine (Institute Director and member of the hospital directorate)

100%

They shall take over

  • the overall personnel, organizational and business responsibility for the cross-location institute, pain medicine and the emergency service
  • together with the management team and in close cooperation with those responsible for the care, the strategic and professional development of the guided specialist areas
  • ensuring a high level of quality of anesthesiological and intensive care services
  • Commitment to strengthening interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation as well as service-oriented and appreciative cooperation with clinics
  • Representation of the Institute in internal and external expert bodies, official bodies and external partners
  • active and sustainable commitment to securing the training and further education mission as attractive training centres

They bring with them

  • Swiss recognised specialist titles for anesthesiology and intensive care (dual specialist)
  • proven experience in preclinical emergency and rescue medicine as well as several years of clinical professional and managerial experience in senior function
  • Habilitation desired, further education in the field of leadership/management/business administration
  • proven leadership and social competence as well as a strong, inclusive, integrity and responsible personality
  • Pronounced team spirit, enjoyment of interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation in clinical everyday life as well as participation in company-wide strategic projects
