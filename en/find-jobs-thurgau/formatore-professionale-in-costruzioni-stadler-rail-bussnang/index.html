Description

PROFILE

  • Participation in the conception and further development of the training content, including taking into account the latest trends and developments in the relevant professional field
  • Supervision of learners in basic and supplementary training
  • Deputy to Stadler's internal ÜK manager, including taking over individual course and training sequences
  • Implementation of internal courses such as CAD, drawing technology, 3D printing, etc.
  • Technical preparation of learners for their assignments in the internal departments
  • Collaboration in recruiting apprentices, carrying out taster days including aptitude tests
  • teaching the basics of additive manufacturing
  • preparation of the TP partial exams and individual practical work IPA
  • collaboration and management of various projects within vocational training TASKS
  • training to become a designer EFZ or an adequate degree at HF level, including several years of professional experience
  • experience in vocational training or leading learners
  • communicative, empathetic and initiative personality with high adaptability -Enjoy daily work with young adults -Vocational training course / SVEB1 certificate (or willingness to complete the necessary further training) -CAD Catia V5 and V6 as well as Smarteam knowledge is an advantage -Comprehensive knowledge of the MS Office world -Experience and joy in dealing with technologies such as 3D printing
