Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services for the elderly and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and inpatient services merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services in Switzerland.

For the Senevita foundry in Arbon we are looking for a Certified nurse night shift 60% Your tasks

  • Night-time care and support: You will ensure professional and individual care so that our residents feel well cared for even at night.
  • Promoting autonomy: By conducting RAI assessments, you will actively contribute to promoting and maintaining the independence of our residents.
  • Emergency management: You recognize emergency situations at an early stage and confidently initiate the necessary measures.
  • Kinesthetic care: You will actively apply the kinesthetic approach to caring for our residents to support their ability to move.
  • Care documentation: You will manage the electronic care documentation independently, carefully and seamlessly.

What you bring to the table

  • Qualifications: You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF, AKP, DNII, DNI or PsyKP.
  • Professional experience: You have in-depth professional experience in long-term inpatient care and in dealing with people with dementia.
  • Language skills: You have good written and spoken German skills.
  • RAI knowledge: Ideally, you already have knowledge of the use of RAI, especially in the classification of dementia patients.
  • Organizational skills: You are characterized by your organizational talent and your ability to organize everyday work efficiently and in a structured way.

What we offer you

  • Career & Development: Benefit from a wide range of further education and training opportunities through the internal Senevita Academy.
  • Culinary supply: Enjoy high-quality catering at discounted rates in our in-house gastronomy.
  • Exclusive employee offers: Take advantage of a wide range of discounts and discounts, for example for the Half-Fare travelcard, when refuelling, for additional insurance or in pharmacies.
  • Work-life balance & security: Rely on attractive social benefits and a generous vacation policy with at least 5 weeks of vacation. To relieve the burden, this entitlement increases from 50. Age to 6 weeks and from the 60. Age to 7 weeks.

Interested? Does this position appeal to you? We look forward to receiving your online application. Become part of our team and shape the future of nursing at Senevita with us! If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact Corin Fritschi, our nursing service manager and deputy managing director.
