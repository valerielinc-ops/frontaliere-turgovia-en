Full description

About the role

  • Leadership and further development of the international quality teams in Romanshorn, Widen and Saintes as well as active design of the new, integrated quality organization for the injection molding sector
  • Responsible for the implementation, maintenance and continuous development of the quality management system within the Injection Molding business
  • Ensuring compliance with relevant quality standards and customer-specific requirements, in particular IATF 16949, VDA 6.3 and TISAX
  • Responsibility as Quality Management Representative (QMR) and assumption of the functions PSB/PSCR and ISR for the Injection Molding division
  • Management and further development of quality planning using automotive core tools such as APQP, PPAP, FMEA, SPC and MSA
  • Responsibility for quality management towards customers and external production partners as well as for complaint management and ensuring sustainable corrective and improvement measures
  • Planning and coordination of system, process, product and customer audits as well as support for certifications and re-certifications
  • Definition and monitoring of relevant quality indicators as well as derivation and implementation of continuous improvement measures
  • Ensuring product and process quality in series production as well as responsibility for the qualification, validation and release of new materials, compounds
  • Close cooperation with customers, suppliers, external production partners as well as internal and international interfaces

Your skills and experience

  • Degree in engineering, quality management or a comparable technical field
  • Several years of professional experience in quality management in the automotive industry, ideally in the field of injection molding or plastics processing
  • Demonstrable leadership experience in leading and developing international and interdisciplinary teams
  • In-depth knowledge of automotive quality standards and requirements, in particular IATF 16949, VDA 6.3 and Customer Specific Requirements (CSR)
  • Very good practical knowledge of automotive core tools such as APQP, PPAP, FMEA, SPC and MSA
  • Experience in customer and supplier quality management, complaint management as well as in the planning and implementation of audits
  • Strong leadership, project management and communication skills as well as confident handling of various international stakeholders
  • Fluency in written and spoken German and English is mandatory; knowledge of French is an advantage
  • Willingness to travel internationally
