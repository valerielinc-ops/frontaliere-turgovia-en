Clinic St. Katharinental introduces itself to you

Klinik St. Katharinental in Diessenhofen is a company of Spital Thurgau AG with a total of 132 beds. In the historical surroundings of the former monastery we offer the areas of musculoskeletal, internistic, oncological and geriatric rehabilitation, acute rheumatology and long-term care. Every year we look after around 1,700 inpatient rehabilitation patients and provide an extensive outpatient service. In addition to the unique location directly on the Rhine, we expect you to enjoy an appreciative culture, multi-professional cooperation, extensive training and further education as well as attractive wage and social benefits. As an employee of Klinik St. Katharinental, you benefit from the benefits of Spital Thurgau AG.
