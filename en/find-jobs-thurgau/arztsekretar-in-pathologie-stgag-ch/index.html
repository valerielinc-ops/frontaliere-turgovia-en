Full description

For our office of the Pathology Institute in Münsterlingen we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement to strengthen our team

Medical Secretary/-in Pathology

40-60%

The Pathology Institute Spital Thurgau at the Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen offers hospitals and resident doctors of all disciplines in the region and nationally a competent, guideline-oriented and forward-looking morphological diagnosis.

They take over

  • Writing of findings reports according to dictation
  • Recording of entries in the patient data management system
  • Sending samples and reports, preparation of tumor boards and telephone information
  • Flexible assumption of other general administrative and organisational secretarial tasks

They bring with them

  • Training as a medical assistant, health care merchant or comparable professional experience in the medical secretariat, ideally in a pathology institute
  • Knowledge of medical terminology or Willingness to appropriate them
  • Safe handling of PC and Office programs
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method
  • Flexibility as well as team-oriented, friendly and professional appearance

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
