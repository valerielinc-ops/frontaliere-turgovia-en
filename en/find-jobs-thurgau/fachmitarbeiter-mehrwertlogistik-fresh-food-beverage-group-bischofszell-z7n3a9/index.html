Full description

In our value-added logistics, you can repackage various products and operate our unpacking system. If you like to do physical work and do not mind a standing job in the refrigerated area, then we are happy to expect your application documents.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Bischofszell, Industriestrasse 1, 9220 Bischofszell

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Operating, monitoring and working on the automatic unpacking system (pack cell) incl. Incident correction
  • Repackaging of third-party supplier articles in BN containers for Convenience Gate
  • Processing and execution of orders which are packaged internally or externally in other bundle units
  • Systematic order processing (booking, release, feedback, process orders)
  • General warehouse work

Requirements

  • 1-2 years: in the camp
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): management or logistics
  • Readiness for 2- to 3-shift work
  • Readiness to work in cooled rooms (5 - 10° C)
  • SUVA forklift licence
  • Healthy, robust physical condition
  • German (flowing)

Cosa of friamo

  • Club School Migros: Refund of 70% of the costs per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Sports and fitness offers: refund of CHF 200 per fitness annual subscription.
  • Migros Bank: Higher interest rates on personnel investment accounts at Migros Bank.
  • Cumulus points: 3 times points on food, 6 times points on all other products.
  • Food: Delicious daily menus at reasonable prices.
  • Du culture: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are per you!
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
