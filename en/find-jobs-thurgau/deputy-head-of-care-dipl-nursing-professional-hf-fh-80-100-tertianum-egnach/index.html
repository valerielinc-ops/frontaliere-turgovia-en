Original description

In our company, the values of respect, passion, and responsibility are central elements. We place great importance on our employees meeting on an equal footing and cultivate a 'you' culture.

Leadership with impact. Care with responsibility. Actively shaping the future.

In spring 2027, we will move into a newly built, modern care center. You have the opportunity to shape this milestone from the beginning and actively influence structures and processes. Become part of our committed team at Tertianum Seerose.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for as of June 1, 2026 or by agreement:

Deputy Team Leader Nursing 80–100%

Your mission

You combine active, professional care with clear leadership responsibility. You are close to the team and our guests and ensure high care quality in everyday life together with the team leader.

Your tasks

In care

  • You take over the holistic care and support of our guests in the early and late shifts
  • You actively shape the care process from planning to implementation to evaluation
  • You bring in your professional expertise and ensure high care quality in everyday life
  • You are present in the department and work closely with the team and relatives

In leadership

  • You support the team leader in organizational and personnel tasks
  • You take over the operational leadership of the team in case of absence
  • You accompany and support employees in their daily work professionally and humanly
  • You promote a constructive, appreciative team culture

In development

  • You actively participate in change processes
  • You shape the move and future processes in the new care center

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH/DNII or equivalent recognized by SRK
  • Several years of professional experience in long-term care
  • First leadership experience or motivation to take on responsibility in a leadership role
  • High social competence, reliability, and assertiveness
  • Structured, solution-oriented way of working and enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Willingness to work in early and late shifts

Our promise

  • A respectful work environment with real development prospects towards leadership
  • A committed team that supports each other and takes responsibility together
  • Targeted promotion of your leadership skills
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits
  • An additional free day in the month of your birthday
  • Free Halbtax, discounted fitness subscription, shopping opportunities, as well as free breakfast and drinks
  • Centrally located workplace with direct public transport connection
  • The opportunity to actively shape the construction and development of the new, modern care center

Ready to take responsibility and shape the future?

Then we look forward to your application.

For questions, Nicole Dietrich, Head of Care and Support / Deputy Managing Director, is available to you at 071 544 30 30 or by email nicole.dietrich@tertianum.ch.
