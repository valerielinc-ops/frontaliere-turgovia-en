yellowshark AG is an established and successful staffing service provider that connects skilled professionals with renowned companies. For our client in the industrial sector, we are looking for a dedicated and motivated individual as an Export Administrator (m/f).

If you have experience in export processing, preparing shipping and customs documents, and international logistics, and are looking for a new professional challenge, we look forward to receiving your application.

We provide you with professional support throughout your career and connect you with companies that appreciate your skills and potential.

  • Organization and coordination of international transports (trucking, air and sea freight)
  • Telephone and written customer support at an international level (D/E)
  • Monitoring delivery dates and ensuring smooth logistics operations
