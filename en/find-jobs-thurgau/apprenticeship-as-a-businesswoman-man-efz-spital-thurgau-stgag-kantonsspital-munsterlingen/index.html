100%

Your tasks

  • You work in the areas of reception, patient admission, accounting, procurement, human resources and patient billing
  • You take over commercial activities such as mail processing and the operation of the telephone exchange
  • You create letters, contracts and edit patient data
  • You are personally and by telephone in contact with patients and their relatives or health insurance companies as well as with employees

You bring that with you

  • Self-employed work
  • Flair in dealing with numbers, languages and IT resources
  • Learning and enthusiasm and responsibility
  • Joy and fun in dealing with people
  • Multicheck / Interlock

Thurmed AG introduces itself

Thurmed AG provides healthcare services in the Canton of Thurgau and the adjacent regions. It operates Spital Thurgau AG, which includes the cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau, the clinic St. Katharinental and other companies that provide services in and for the health care system.
