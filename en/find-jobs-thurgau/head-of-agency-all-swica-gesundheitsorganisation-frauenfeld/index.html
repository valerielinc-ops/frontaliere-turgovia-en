Full description

Closeness is in our DNA. At SWICA, we in the agencies can decide how we can best help our insured people. Short routes, quick decisions: You are right in the middle, working on a wide range of projects and actively helping to shape solutions. You bring in your ideas directly and see how they have an impact. That makes the difference. This is how you shape your health

  • Main responsibility for the personnel and results-oriented management of the Frauenfeld agency
  • Leading, coaching and developing subordinate employees
  • Responsible for achieving quantitative and qualitative goals in private and corporate customer business
  • Implementation of the necessary measures to achieve defined customer satisfaction
  • Responsibility for lead and campaign management including the creation and implementation of sales and action planning for the assigned agency area
  • Expansion and maintenance of your local customer and business network

This is what sets you apart

  • You have very good sales/leadership skills and support your team with care and appreciation
  • Further training in insurance and/or sales management desired. In addition, a VBV or comparable certification is required.
  • You have several years of professional experience in the Swiss insurance industry and a convincing track record in sales and sales management.
  • You live in the Frauenfeld region and actively maintain a broad network of relationships
  • You impress with your negotiation skills, solution-oriented and entrepreneurial thinking and actions, a networked way of thinking and consistent determination
  • You have a winning personality with a confident demeanor, a high level of service and a strong customer focus

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That's why we put openness, respect and trust at the center of our everyday work. We get involved, take responsibility and think of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of vacation and, if desired, additional vacation time. With part-time models and a spacious home office within the scope of the position, you can structure your everyday life largely flexibly.
  • We value and reward your work – with a wage in line with the market, up to 1,500 francs an annual allowance for personal wishes and a generous contribution to the basic and supplementary insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you perspectives. We support your professional and personal development with generous training regulations – for your role today and for your goals tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if things turn out differently: 100% net salary continued payment.
  • We think long-term. With three savings plans, a workload-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you can take good care of your future.
