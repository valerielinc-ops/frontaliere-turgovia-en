Full description

The Institute of Pathology of Spital Thurgau AG at Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen is a central component of medical care in the Canton of Thurgau. It provides comprehensive diagnostic services for inpatient and outpatient patients and is closely involved in interdisciplinary tumor centers and clinical decision-making processes.

Senior physician / Senior physician Pathology

80-100%

In the course of a generational change and the future orientation of the institute, we are looking for 1. April 2027 or by agreement a committed, professionally experienced and leadership-strong personality.

They take over

  • Active participation and further development of the Institute of Pathology in close cooperation with the institute management
  • Independent macroscopic and histopathological diagnosis
  • Rapid-cutting examinations
  • Professional and personnel responsibility for defined sub-areas
  • Active participation and participation in interdisciplinary tumor boards and tumor centers
  • Supervision and further training of assistant doctors
  • Participation in quality assurance measures and in the further development of diagnostic standards and processes
  • Engagement in clinical projects, possibly research and network maintenance with referrals

They bring with them

  • Specialist title Pathology (FMH or equivalent recognized by MEBEKO)
  • Several years of diagnostic experience in the entire spectrum of pathology
  • Subspecialization in a subfield of pathology (cytology/molecular pathology) is advantageous
  • Experience in a professional leadership role, ideally with first administrative responsibility
  • High interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Commitment and pleasure in the education, training and further education of young medical researchers
  • Structured, independent and quality-oriented working method
  • Strong social skills, teamwork and communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least B2)

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
