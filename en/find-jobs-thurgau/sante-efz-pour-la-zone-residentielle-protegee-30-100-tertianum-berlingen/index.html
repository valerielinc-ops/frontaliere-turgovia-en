Care with heart and mind – We are looking for you!

You are an expert in health EFZ and would like to work in a warm environment in which humanity and team spirit are the focus? Then you fit us perfectly!

We are a motivated team in long-term care and are looking for reinforcement by dedicated professionals who provide with pleasure and expertise for the well-being of our guests.

For the retirement and nursing home Haus Seefeld in Berlingen we are looking for an

Health EFZ for the protected living area (30-100%)

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and care of guests in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Carrying out care activities
  • Electronic care documentation through CareCoach
  • Taking on responsibilities as a reference person
  • Cooperation in the implementation of the care process
  • Assuming daily responsibility
  • Assumption of tasks as a topic carrier, technical authority

Your profile

  • Completed training for EFZ or equivalent training recognised by the SRC
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Responsibility and teamwork
  • Humor and the ability to keep track even in turbulent everyday life
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
