Raiffeisenbank Neukirch-Romanshorn, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Neukirch (Egnach) and Romanshorn, is looking for a qualified personality as a financial advisor.

What awaits you?

  • Continuous support of existing customers and systematic exploitation of the recognized potential along the topics of investing, provisioning and financing
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Ensuring competent advice and comprehensive asset management
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual needs, wishes, goals and living conditions of the customers within the segment-specific product and service range
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Banking education with further training in banking or finance
  • Years of experience in the financial services sector
  • Good knowledge of financing and pensions as well as in-depth knowledge of investing
  • Strong service-oriented thinking with a degree-oriented action
  • Sympathetic, winning and competent appearance with a pronounced customer and service orientation
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as enjoying intensive customer contact
  • Networking in the region is an advantage
