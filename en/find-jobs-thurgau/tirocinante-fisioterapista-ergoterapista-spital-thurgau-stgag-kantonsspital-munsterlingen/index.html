Full description

Future students of physiotherapy or occupational therapy will have the opportunity to do the preliminary internship required by the universities of applied sciences (working experience) in the desired professional environment. Therefore, we offer quarterly employment for each 3 months as

Pre-practical/in physio/occupational therapy

100%

You have the opportunity to complete an interesting, varied preliminary internship with us. As a team member of physiotherapy and occupational therapy, you will have the opportunity to watch or assist in various forms of therapy. Are you an open, independent person and interested in an intensive insight into our great therapy professions?

Then we look forward to your contact!

They take over

  • Assistance in individual treatments and group therapies
  • Hospitation in treatments
  • Participation in team and student training
  • Delegated tasks for the therapy secretariat
  • Cleaning and maintenance services of therapeutic equipment
  • Intervening and various delegated tasks

They bring with them

  • Intent to study as a physiotherapist or occupational therapist

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.
