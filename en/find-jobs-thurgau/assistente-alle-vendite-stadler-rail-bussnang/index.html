PROFILE Are you looking for a job with meaning and responsibility? Then become a sales assistant at Stadler! As a sales assistant, you will pursue the overarching goal of successfully supporting the sale of rail vehicles throughout the world. If you enjoy working in a team and completing your tasks responsibly - then you've come to the right place. We are looking for a committed and communicative personality who will successfully develop the offer documents together with our sales and bid managers.

  • Completed commercial apprenticeship
  • Good use of MS Office, advanced in:
  • Word (formatting, table of contents, layouts, references)
  • Excel (pivot tables, S-references)
  • PowerPoint
  • German and English, other languages are a significant advantage
  • Independent, responsible and able to work in a team
  • High flexibility in terms of commitment, especially during the period of submitting the offer TASKS
  • Correspondence for Sales Manager and Head of Bid Management & Shared Services
  • Support the Sales and bid manager in the offer process/project assistance in preparing and sending offers
  • Preparation of prequalifications and support for offers
  • Formatting of offer documents
  • Obtaining and verifying official documents/confirmations
  • Obtaining customer reference documents
  • Organizing and carrying out the physical creation (copying process) of offers
  • Supporting the sales team in all organizational and administrative activities
  • Maintaining customer master data
  • Courier shipments
