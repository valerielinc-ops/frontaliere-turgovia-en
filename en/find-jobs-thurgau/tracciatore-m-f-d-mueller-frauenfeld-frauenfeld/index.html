Our core tasks include the construction and maintenance of railway tracks and safety at railway construction sites. Although track construction and the company have a long tradition, we offer our services in a modern segment. By modern track construction we mean efficient, technically sophisticated machines operated by trained specialists. We ensure free travel on rails! Newcomers welcome! Do you have a completed apprenticeship as a track builder, landscaper, scaffold builder or forest warden and would like to jump on the train into the future at Müller Gleisbau? Although track construction and the company have a long tradition, we offer our services in a modern, future-oriented segment. By modern track / track foundation construction we mean good specialist staff and an efficient, technically sophisticated machine and safety park. Your tasks

  • Drainage and gravel construction work
  • Banqueting and securing services
  • Contact line foundations (track and point assembly)
  • Laying of cable channels
  • Implementation of various concrete works

Our expectations

  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship as a track builder, or road builder, as a landscaper, scaffold builder, forester, mason
  • You like to tackle and working outdoors gives you pleasure
  • You have a good knowledge of German (minor level B1) and are ready for night and weekend work
  • You are physically resilient, reliable and keep a cool head in stressful situations
  • As a team player, you have a great sense of responsibility
