This is what awaits you

Much more than a job - Generalagentur Mittelthurgau - the focus is on people.

Our damage is the central service area to deliver on our performance promise to our customers. We are therefore looking for a player to strengthen our team who is willing to go the extra mile for our customers.

This is what you do with us

We are looking for you who communicates openly, who acts with passion and empathy in the team, who values fair play and has the heart in the right place. Is the safety of customers also the focus for you? All of us in the team are committed to this.

The focus of your job is the uncomplicated and empathetic support of our customers in the event of damage – from reporting, processing to completion.

You maintain a close exchange with your team and all parties involved in the event of damage.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance sector
  • Completed training «Insurance intermediary/in VBV» or willingness to complete it
  • Professional experience in the claims department
  • Helpfulness is your passion
  • You want to inspire customers
  • Team orientation, independence and positive attitude
