Apprenticeship as an assistant in health and social affairs (2 years)

100%

Patient-friendly, eager to learn, motivated, empathetic, accurate and flexible - that's us. Do these values apply to you? Then apply with us – we look forward to you!

Your tasks

  • As an assistant in Health and Social Affairs (AGS) EBA, you support and care for our patients in everyday hospital life. You work in a team with dipl. Nurses, health specialists (FaGe) and trainees from the nursing professions.
  • In your area of competence, you will carry out work such as nursing activities (e.g. support in personal care, while eating), nursing tasks (e.g. blood pressure / pulse controls), everyday design as well as domestic, logistical and administrative tasks.
  • As AGS you make an important contribution to the promotion resp. Maintain the health and well-being of patients.

You bring that with you

  • Completion of compulsory schooling
  • good oral and written communication in the German language (language level B1), understanding of the Swiss German dialect
  • You like working in a team
  • You are reliable and responsible
  • You are physically and psychologically resilient

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.

You also benefit as a learner from

  • Motivated education and nursing teams who expertly accompany, promote and support you
  • A learning environment in which you learn the networking of theory and practice in the best possible way through versatile learning activities
  • In case of appropriate suitability from a training place to / to the specialist health (FaGe) EFZ, directly after the AGS training
