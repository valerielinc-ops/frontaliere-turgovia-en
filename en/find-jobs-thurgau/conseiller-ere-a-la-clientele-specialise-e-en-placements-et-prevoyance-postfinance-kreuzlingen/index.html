Full description

At PostFinance, our customers are the focus – and you are right with them. As a customer consultant, you recognize what people really need about financial issues and accompany them with competent advice at eye level – digitally, by phone or on site. In a dynamic team with broad experience in the financial and insurance sector, you will develop innovative solutions together. In this way, you shape the future of advice personally, relevantly and effectively.

You can do that.

  • You advise private customers: inside holistically and needs-oriented with focus on investing and provision
  • You actively address customers, recognize their needs and use sales opportunities specifically
  • You build up existing customer relationships and develop them sustainably
  • You advise in the areas of numbers in everyday life, saving and financing as part of daily business
  • You inspire customers for digital solutions and self-service offers
  • You are responsible for the Kreuzlingen location and you design the consulting day independently for three days a week.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic banking and finance training and further education as an IAF or financial planner
  • Experience in need-oriented advice for private customers: inside
  • In-depth knowledge and strong affinity in the areas of investing and prevention
  • Sales flair, initiative and enthusiasm for an active customer approach with suitable solutions
  • High customer and service orientation as well as strong communication skills
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, strong drive and will to take responsibility and achieve goals
  • Affinity for digital solutions as well as very good knowledge of German in words and writing
