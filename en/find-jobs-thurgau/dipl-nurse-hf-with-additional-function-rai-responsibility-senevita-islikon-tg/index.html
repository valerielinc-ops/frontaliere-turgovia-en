Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. If a warm relationship with your fellow human beings is the be-all and end-all for you, you are enthusiastically committed to older people, team spirit and the joy of working are your top priority, then we have still missed you!

For the Senevita Sunnwies in Islikon we are looking for a Dipl immediately or by agreement. Nurse with additional function RAI responsibility 80 - 100% Your tasks

  • Professional and individual care and care of residents on the conceptual basis of the model of promoting process care
  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of nursing measures in compliance with the criteria of the nursing process and involving the affected residents and their caregivers.
  • Co-creation of an optimal interdisciplinary cooperation with all services involved in the care and care process
  • Assumption of day-to-day responsibility
  • Careful and seamless management of care documentation
  • Accurate recording of care and care services as well as the care and consumable materials used.
  • RAI responsibility:
  • Implementation and safeguarding of timely RAI assessments in accordance with legal and operational requirements.
  • Responsibility for the correct classification of care needs as well as the care classification of residents.
  • Creation, review and ongoing updating of individual care planning based on the assessment results.
  • Ensuring a complete, professional and comprehensible maintenance documentation.
  • Advice and support to care professionals in the application of the RAI system and care planning.
  • Monitoring assessment dates and ensuring data quality and completeness of documentation.

You bring that with you

  • Completed professional training as a qualified nurse (HF, FH, AKP, DNII, DNI, PSYKP) or equivalent diploma recognised by the SRC
  • Experience in long-term care and dealing with the elderly
  • Experience with the RAI system
  • User knowledge of the software CareCoach (maintenance documentation) is an advantage
  • Empathetic and resilient personality with high commitment
  • Very good written and oral German skills

We offer you

  • Extensive and free training on the Senevita Campus and in the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development
  • Free intermediate meals (bread, fruit, coffee and mineral), as well as min. 50% discount in our own restaurants
  • Attractive Senevita benefits (SBB half-ax, fleet discounts, participation in fitness subscription, discount on Senevita holiday apartments, various partner discounts, etc.)
  • Generous holiday regime (at least 5 weeks, from 50 years 6 weeks and from 60 years 7 weeks)
  • Regular team events and shared experiences that strengthen cooperation
  • Our statement "closer to people" counts for our employees as much as for our residents.

Is this exactly the challenge you are looking for? Our helpful and dedicated team is looking forward to meeting you! We look forward to your online application incl. CV, relevant diplomas and employment certificates.
