Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Schönholzer.

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • Goods of our customers you transport safely and on time with trucks, trailers or semi-trailers
  • Before each trip you check your vehicle, load it professionally and unload it at your destination
  • You comply with national transport regulations at all times, including when handling dangerous goods
  • Within Switzerland you are on the road, observe traffic rules and drive environmentally friendly
  • You keep your vehicle fired, maintain it and ensure operational readiness
  • In daily customer contact, you are always friendly and helpful
  • During your apprenticeship you will also get an insight into a truck workshop
  • You help in the fleet and in technology and learn to develop your craft skills there.
  • You work in the envelope and in logistics and experience the first steps you need for a transport.

Profile

  • Technology inspires you, and craftsmanship you also bring with you
  • Independence, reliability and peace under time pressure are among your strengths
  • With physical fitness and stress resistance, you master your everyday work on the road
  • Openness, contact and friendliness characterize your interaction with others

Benefits

  • Respect, trust and fair treatment count with us – we are with you and accompany you personally on your way.
  • In the first year of apprenticeship you have 8, in the 2nd 7 and in the following years 6 weeks of vacation.
  • During your training time with us, you participate in various events such as, WelcomeDay, specialization seminar, apprentice camp, apprenticeship graduation party, etc.
  • With us, not only the wage is right: you get a fair apprentice wage plus 13. Monthly wages. If you show full commitment at school, in the üK and in the company, you can even secure a performance bonus. We pay for the school material and for the notebook we give you a financial boost. On top of that, there is a half-tax subscription for your entire apprenticeship.
  • In road transport you have to make the driver’s licenses B/BE and/or C/CE. We support you and cover the costs of the training.
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free.

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
