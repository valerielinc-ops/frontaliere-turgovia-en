Companies … Job Portal Handling of kitchens (AVOR) Handling of kitchens (AVOR) In this task, you ensure that kitchen projects continue to run cleanly after planning. They revise existing kitchen plans according to customer needs, adjust additional and reduced costs and create execution and installation plans. Assembly dossiers, orders, re-orders and defect lists are also part of your area of responsibility. You fit well to this task if you work exactly, think technically and bring pleasure to the kitchen industry. The most important in short Place of work Sirnach (TG) Pensum 80 - 100 %

Responsibilities

  • Professional order processing of versatile kitchen projects
  • Re-planning of existing kitchen plans tailored to customer needs as well as revision of additional and reduced costs
  • Preparation of execution and installation plans and assembly dossier
  • Total order processing including follow-up orders
  • Processing of lists of defects

What you bring

  • Ideally completed vocational training as carpenter or draftsman
  • Professional experience in the kitchen industry desirable
  • Good IT user knowledge (planning software winner of advantage)
  • Precise and reliable operation
  • Enthusiasm and commitment
