We offer training places for the

Education dipl. Nursing specialist HF (4 years, part-time, BfGS) January 2027

70%

Are you interested in training in the exciting field of psychiatry? Newcomers and newcomers are also welcome! With this training model you can complete the training with an employment level of 70%.

You take over

You will become a dipl with us in four years. Nurse HF trained and takes on the following tasks with increasing independence depending on the year of training:

  • Reference work of people with various psychiatric diseases
  • Accompanying people of different ages and with different socio-cultural backgrounds
  • Professional responsibility in the area of care as part of the care process
  • Cooperation with the intra-/interprofessional team and with relatives
  • The everyday and milieu design on the stations
  • The design of health-promoting offers such as patient education
  • Medical engineering and diagnostic assistance

You bring with you

  • Higher secondary education, i.e.:
  • vocational qualification with a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ), or
  • graduate degree (FMS) or
  • Gymnasium maturity
  • Interest in the field of psychiatry and personal development

The Psychiatric Services introduces

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau (PDT) consists of the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen, the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry and the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service. PDT is part of the successful Spital Thurgau AG with more than 4100 employees. Company locations are located in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.
