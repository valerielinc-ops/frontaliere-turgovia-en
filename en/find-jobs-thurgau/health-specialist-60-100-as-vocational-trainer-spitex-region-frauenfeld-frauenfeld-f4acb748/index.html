The Spitex Region Frauenfeld is a forward-looking, continuously growing nonprofit organization with around 120 employees and respectful DU culture. Then you are exactly right with us! Immediately or by agreement. Specialist:r Health 60 - 100% as a vocational trainer:in As a specialist employee:r Health (FaGe) with an additional function as a vocational trainer: you get our learners on track – you motivate, demand, give honest feedback and celebrate their progress. You are actively involved in shaping learning processes and yes – you stay in the middle of the action: care, advice, support for customers: within and relatives are just as much a part of it. Because real professionals can do both.

  • You bring our guides forward – professionally and personally.
