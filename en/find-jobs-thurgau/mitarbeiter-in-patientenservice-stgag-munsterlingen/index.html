We are looking for a patient service for the hotel industry immediately or by agreement

Employee/patient service

60-80%

You take over

  • the care of our patients on the Privée Station and are part of our Guest Relations Ambassador team
  • interesting and challenging tasks, which include the well-kept food and drink service in the patient rooms as well as in the dining room of the additional insured patients
  • Services between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m., sometimes with room hour, on all days of the year
  • the advice of patients and their guests in the daily menu selection
  • Working with the cash system including collection and settlement
  • the provision of room equipment for new entrants and the holding of an initial interview on behalf of the hotel industry
  • a close cooperation as an interface function with care, nutritional advice and kitchen

You bring that with you

  • Basic training (EFZ) in the hospitality, catering or housekeeping industry
  • routine use of electronic aids
  • Friendly and team capable personality
  • German (language and writing), understanding in dialect
  • Reliability, quality awareness and a very pronounced service awareness – simply enjoying our profession!

Clinic St. Katharinental introduces itself to you

Klinik St. Katharinental in Diessenhofen is a company of Spital Thurgau AG with a total of 132 beds. In the historical surroundings of the former monastery we offer the areas of musculoskeletal, internistic, oncological and geriatric rehabilitation, acute rheumatology and long-term care. Every year we look after around 1,700 inpatient rehabilitation patients and provide an extensive outpatient service. In addition to the unique location directly on the Rhine, you can expect an appreciative culture, multi-professional cooperation, extensive training opportunities and attractive wage and social benefits. As an employee of Klinik St. Katharinental, you benefit from the benefits of Spital Thurgau AG.
