Full description

Metal construction projects with steel, aluminum and chromium steel form your everyday work. You can work independently according to design plans and bring your craft precision. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Manufacture and assembly of complex steel construction projects, including doors, fire doors, facade elements, windows, stairs and railings
  • Perform welding work on materials such as steel, aluminium and chromium steel
  • Design workflows independently and precisely according to design plans

Requirements

  • Completed training as a metalworker
  • Several years of practical experience in the metalworking workshop
  • Own initiative and team-oriented and flexible working methods
  • Careful work with high accuracy and quality awareness
  • Willingness to continuously train

Offer

  • Small motivated team with flat hierarchies and fast decision paths
