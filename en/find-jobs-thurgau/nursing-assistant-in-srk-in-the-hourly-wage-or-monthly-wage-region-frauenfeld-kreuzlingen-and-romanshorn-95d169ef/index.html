Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. For the Senevita Casa Thurgau we are looking for an immediate or by appointment for the region Frauenfeld, Kreuzlingen and Romanshorn.

Nursing assistant/in SRK in hourly or monthly wages Your tasks

  • You ensure compliance with the hygiene guidelines and also take on domestic tasks

  • Your several years of professional experience in long-term care or Spitex area is not a requirement, but an advantage
  • You are accustomed to resilient, flexible and independent work
  • You have your own car and live in the region

  • The extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy is free of charge for our employees
  • We also offer lucrative employee benefits & partner benefits
