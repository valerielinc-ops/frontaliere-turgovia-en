PROFILE Degree: technical or IT-related training Experience: Application of engineering and manufacturing processes Further training: Further training in the PLM environment, ITIL and system training Further knowledge: TEAMCENTER, CATIA, SAP ERP Technical and methodological competence:

  • Distinctive ability to provide structured user support, including moderation of key user communities, target group-oriented communication and creation of practical documentation
  • High competence in the systematic analysis of complex disruptions with the help of Logs, system tools and PLM-specific specialist knowledge as well as methodical ticket and incident management
  • Sound experience in carrying out and documenting system tests after updates, patches or releases as well as in supporting rollouts and migrations
  • Methodical understanding of the evaluation, further development and optimization of PLM processes (e.g. CAD integration, change management) and to derive improvements based on user feedback Interpersonal skills:
  • Strong teamwork skills to work effectively with different departments and cross-cultural teams to share knowledge and achieve common goals
  • Strong empathy, the ability to put yourself in the shoes of users, understand their problems and communicate with patience and understanding
  • Strong communication skills to clearly and understandably convey complex technical information to different stakeholders and ensure that everyone involved is on the same page TASKS Main tasks
  • Carrying out the analysis of complex disruptions using logs and system tools, including processing second-level issues as well as structured documentation and tracking in the ticket system and passing them on to the 3rd level support level
  • Carrying out system tests after updates, patches or releases as well as participating in rollouts and migrations to ensure stable system operation
  • Feedback to optimize PLM processes and workflows based on user feedback as well as support in standardization and improvement initiatives Additional and secondary tasks
  • Creation of documentation, version control and close collaboration with other IT and specialist departments
  • Support in the maintenance and control of data in the PLM ecosystem
