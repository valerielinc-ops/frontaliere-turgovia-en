The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau (PDT) consists of the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen, the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry and the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service. PDT is part of the successful Spital Thurgau AG with more than 4'100 employees. Company locations are located in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn.
