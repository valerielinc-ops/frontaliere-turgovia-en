PROFILE

  • Completed mechanical training or equivalent qualification
  • In-depth knowledge of various technical disciplines such as mechanics, hydraulics, pneumatics and MSR technology and safe handling of technical drawings
  • Several years of professional experience in rail vehicle construction are advantageous
  • High willingness to travel (10–60%) for national and international missions
  • Existing category B driving licence
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Other foreign languages are a plus

TASKS

  • Independent performance of maintenance and repair work on railway vehicles
  • Processing of mechanical warranty and rework as well as efficient solution of technical faults
  • Professional installation and disassembly of assemblies, components and systems
  • Complete documentation of all work steps as well as material and spare parts consumption
