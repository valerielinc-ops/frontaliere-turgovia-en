Full description

For the logistics area, we are looking for a pharmaceutical assistant, PTA or druggist, logistics 80-100%, immediately or by appointment. We offer you a varied and demanding job in the hospital environment. You can expect an initiative and motivated team as well as a modern infrastructure in a future-oriented and successful service company. Together with our motivated team, you will ensure the supply of medicines to hospitals and other institutions, process the daily receipt of goods, manage the stocked goods as well as provide and monitor medication deliveries for our customers, act as the contact person for our customers for all matters relating to drug deliveries, train as a pharmaceutical assistant EFZ, PTA or druggist EFZ. Motivated career starters immediately after completing their training are also welcome to apply. reliable, independent and efficient way of working in a team, resilient, efficient and customer-oriented personality, ideally experience in using MS Office and SAP Spitalpharmazie Thurgau AG in Münsterlingen looks after and supplies various hospitals and health organizations in eastern Switzerland with medicines. Our core competencies include hospital-oriented medication logistics, the production of medicines, the operation of a cytostatics laboratory and the operation of our own public pharmacy near the hospital.
