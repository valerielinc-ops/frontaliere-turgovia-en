Full description

PROFILE

– Completed vocational training as an automatic technician:in EFZ

– Further education in the field of electrical engineering, system engineering or comparable technical training (HF/FH of advantage)

– Several years of professional experience in commissioning complex electrical systems, preferably in the rail vehicle sector

– In-depth knowledge in electrical engineering, control and diagnostic engineering

– Experience in type tests, vehicle approvals and customer projects

– Very good knowledge in reading and evaluating electrical schemes and technical specifications

– Experience in training, professional leadership or development of employees is beneficial

Strong analytical skills and high problem-solving skills

– Independent, structured and quality-conscious working method

– strong communication personality with high technical competence and assertiveness

– Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

– Willingness to travel for technical operations at home and abroad

TASKS

– Overall technical responsibility for commissioning across all projects and vehicle platforms

– Technical support for projects in challenging issues, disruptions and escalations in the plant, during type tests and at customers on site

Implementation and management of reviews of electrical schemes, functional descriptions and software requirements specifications

– Definition, maintenance and further development of technical standards, methods and work instructions for commissioning

– Planning, organisation and implementation of training and training programmes for commissioning technicians

– Responsibility for establishing, promoting and securing technical know-how within commissioning

Ensuring structured and sustainable onboarding of new employees

Responsibility for the training and professional development of learners within the start-up

– Evaluation, procurement and standardization of operating, measuring and testing equipment

– Support for type tests, initial commissioning, registration runs and technical approvals

– carry out technical root cause analyses and develop sustainable corrective measures

– Contact for customers, engineering and production in complex technical issues

– Professional responsibility for the establishment and further development of the commissioning and type test department

Definition of test strategies, test methods and technical standards for future vehicle platforms

– Promoting the exchange of knowledge and experience between commissioning, type test, engineering and service
