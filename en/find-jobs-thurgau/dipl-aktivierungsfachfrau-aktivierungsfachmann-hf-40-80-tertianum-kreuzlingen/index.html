Your heart beats for the elderly? Is the care profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully apply your skills and experiences with us to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are perfect for us!

For our Tertianum Meeresstern Kreuzlingen facility, we are looking for a

Dipl. Activity Specialist HF 40-80%

Your tasks

  • Independent planning, organization, and implementation of resource-oriented activation and daily management in group or individual activities in close collaboration with the nursing department
  • Willingness to work evenings and weekends during events
  • Planning and implementation of excursions and events
  • Supporting and promoting interdisciplinary collaboration in all areas

  • Supporting future HF students

Your profile

  • Professional experience in long-term care
  • Experience in long-term psychiatry or psychiatric rehabilitation for the elderly
  • Organizational talent and sociability
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoyment of diverse challenges
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written
