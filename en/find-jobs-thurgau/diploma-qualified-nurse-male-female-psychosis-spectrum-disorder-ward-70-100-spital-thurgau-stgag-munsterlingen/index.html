The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau (PDT) consists of the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen, the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry and the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service. PDT is part of the successful Spital Thurgau AG with over 4,100 employees. Company locations are located in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.

The Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) Thurgau offers excellent conditions for classical child and adolescent psychiatric training as well as for comprehensive psychotherapeutic training. Every year, we treat more than 3,000 patients with high quality standards in family-friendly, outpatient, outreach and day-clinical settings, if possible with evidence-based therapy (multi-systemic therapy MST, Parent Child Interaction Therapy PCIT, ESDM for early intervention in autism). An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the KJPD a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.

The Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) together with the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. With over 280 beds and specialized offerings in the areas of psychotherapy, general and acute psychiatry, addiction diseases, age psychiatry and forensics, we offer evidence-based and qualitatively optimal therapies for the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases. An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make PKM a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.

The Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) together with the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. Every year, we treat more than 7,500 patients and offer specialized outpatient, outreach and daily clinical services in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the AEP as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau a highly attractive employer.
