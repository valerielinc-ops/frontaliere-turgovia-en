Full description

As a sanitary installer: in EFZ you are responsible for ensuring that buildings are safely and reliably supplied with hygienic drinking water and natural gas in accordance with the regulations. With reliable planning, you ensure the required quality and an efficient process. You get structured information, take mass, create workshop plans, draw assembly details and determine the required material. In the workshop you carefully manufacture the pipes, which you then lay in the shell for water, sewage and natural gas pipelines. You also carry out maintenance and servicing work step by step, fill out reports, write useful protocols and create documentation. You bring this with
