Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Horn

Work where others go on holiday! Our modern pharmacy with direct views of Lake Constance is located in Horn. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks or consultations. In addition, we have a well-equipped laboratory for the production of magistral recipes, our own house specialties or pharmaceuticals in contract manufacturing. Medbase Apotheke Horn is also involved in the training of apprentices and pharmacists in the assistant year. We appreciate the diversity of our tasks, cultivate an appreciative relationship with each other and love the lively exchange with customers.

Your tasks

  • The focus of your work lies in the area of patient-specific production of medicines such as ointments, capsules, solutions, suspensions and suppositories
  • You work in the laboratory team, there is no customer contact

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a drugist, specialist pharmacy, pharmaceutical-technical assistant (PTA)
  • Pleasure in laboratory work, manufacture of medicines
  • Teamwork and willingness to help
  • Reliable, independent, precise and efficient working methods
  • Word and Excel knowledge
  • Experience in dealing with the rules of Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and in quality management is beneficial
