Full description

You do not just want to operate a plant, but understand what happens in the process and really make a difference with your know-how? In our milk bottling, you take responsibility for modern production facilities, stable processes and the quality of our products. You work closely with your team, contribute your experience and have the opportunity to actively shape processes.

You can do that.

  • You can reliably get our bottling plants up and running and ensure that production starts around from the start.
  • During operation, you keep an eye on performance, quality and process values and react specifically when something does not fit.
  • Format changes, settings and minor technical adjustments are carried out independently.
  • You recognize disturbances early, solve them directly where possible and work closely with the technology on more complex topics.
  • Quality and hygiene are more than just specifications for you: You actively contribute to ensuring that our high standards are met.
  • With your expertise, observations and ideas, you help make processes smarter and more efficient.

You bring that with you

  • You bring an education in the food sector or in-depth experience as a machine operator:in, plant operator:in or in a similar production environment.
  • Technology excites you and you enjoy really understanding equipment and processes.
  • Taking on responsibility is up to you and you also work in a structured way when it has to be fast in everyday production.
  • You join the team, communicate openly and can rely on others just as much as they rely on you.
  • Quality, hygiene and safety are a matter of course for you and you work accordingly accurately and attentively.
  • Working in 3-shift operation suits you and with good knowledge of German you can communicate safely in everyday life.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
