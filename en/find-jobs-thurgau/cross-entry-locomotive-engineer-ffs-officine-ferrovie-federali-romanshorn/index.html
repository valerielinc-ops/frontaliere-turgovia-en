Cross-entry train driver: in shunt 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry train driver: in shunting Romanshorn, by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Approximately 14,000 passenger transport employees ensure that the more than 1.4 million Customers of SBB, which we transport daily, get easy access to public transport and travel from A to B both safely and punctually. The shunting staff as a central professional group makes a central contribution to this. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you move the SBB together with your teammates, as well as a varied and paid education. You also have various internal training opportunities. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even for irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us - e.g. with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night bets, you get additional time surcharges between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments.

Possible start of training in 2027

  • 01. January 2027
  • 01. May 2027

You can move that.

  • You prepare passenger trains for their journey and move them in the station and in its surroundings (up to 30 km/h) to the right starting point.
  • You put together trains, separate individual cars and make sure that everything is in the right place.
  • Make the train ready to go, check the most important functions and carry out brake checks.
  • During your work, you keep track and ensure that all processes work safely and smoothly.
  • Minor disturbances can be resolved independently or reported to the responsible body.

Here you will learn more about the job profile. You bring that with you.

  • You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully handle this demanding work in 24-hour shift operations. You also like to work outside in any weather.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly find your way around with digital tools.
  • You have completed a basic education (minor EBA) and already bring practical experience – ideally from a craft or logistical environment.
  • In German you bring in word and writing very good knowledge (level B2).
  • You have a perfect color sense (no red / green weakness).
