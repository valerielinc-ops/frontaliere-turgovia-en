Full description

In this varied environment, you set up CNC lathes independently and optimize production processes. The company offers you space for your own ideas as well as challenging tasks from prototypes to series. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Install, equip and operate CNC lathes independently
  • Programming machines using ISO code and optimizing production processes
  • Produce prototypes, individual parts and series according to drawing
  • Inspect components, perform maintenance and troubleshooting

Requirements

  • Experience in setting up, operating and programming CNC lathes
  • Good knowledge of ISO programming
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as precise and independent working methods
