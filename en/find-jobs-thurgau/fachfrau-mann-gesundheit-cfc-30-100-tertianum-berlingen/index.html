Full description

Care with heart and mind – we are looking for you!

Are you an EFZ health specialist and would you like to work in a warm environment where humanity and team spirit are the focus? Then you're a perfect fit for us!

We are a motivated team in long-term care and are looking for support from committed specialists who will ensure the well-being of our guests with joy and expertise.

We are looking for someone for the Haus Seeheim retirement and nursing home in Berlingen immediately or by appointment

Health specialist EFZ for long-term care 30-100%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our care principles and the care process
  • Carrying out treatment-related nursing activities
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach
  • Taking on tasks as a caregiver
  • Participate in the implementation of the nursing process
  • Assuming day-to-day responsibility
  • Taking on tasks as a subject matter representative, technical authority to give instructions

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health specialist EFZ or equivalent training recognized by the SRK
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Sense of responsibility and ability to work in a team
  • Humor and the ability to keep an overview even in turbulent everyday life
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

Why Tertianum?

It is important to us that our employees can balance their professional development and their lifestyle. This includes a wide range of different benefits, e.g.:

  • Flexible working models (job sharing, part-time models, flexible roster design, returning to work after a break)
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Fitness perks
  • SBB half-fare subscription
  • Insurance discounts
  • Fleet discounts
  • Discounts at Sunrise

You can find further benefits on our website.

Are you interested?

Then we offer you an exciting perspective: With us, you can use your individual skills and professionalism in a motivated and flexible team. You will also take on a high level of responsibility and thus actively shape the future of Tertianum. Apply now and become part of our team.

We look forward to receiving your complete online application via our application tool.
