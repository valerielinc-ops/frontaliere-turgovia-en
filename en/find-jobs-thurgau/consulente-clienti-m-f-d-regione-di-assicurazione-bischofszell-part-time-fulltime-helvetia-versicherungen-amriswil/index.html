Client Consulting – Sales

Pensum: 50-100% Workplace: Experience Level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, that means much more than simply ticking off tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our clients, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • Forward-looking consulting & support: You accompany private and corporate clients in all insurance matters with genuine dedication and ensure tailored solutions for every individual need. As a client advisor, you are there when it counts.
  • Building long-term relationships: Trust is at the center of your work – you cultivate and develop sustainable client relationships based on your excellent service.
  • Actively shaping growth: You have a good network and contribute your energy to acquiring new clients. Your commitment ensures that our client base grows steadily. How can we support you in this? By providing you with your own client base of private and corporate clients.
  • Marketing & sales support: With fresh ideas, you support our marketing campaigns and attend trade fairs and events to strengthen our network.
  • Always up to date: Through our basic training and continuous further education, you remain professionally up to date and offer the highest level of consulting competence.

What you bring

  • Experience & expertise: You bring experience in client consulting or sales – ideally in the financial or insurance industry. Ambitious career changers ready to hit the ground running are also very welcome.
  • Communication talent: Inspiring and winning people over is in your blood. You shine with strong communication and negotiation skills. As a client advisor, you can argue convincingly and possess fluent German language skills.
  • Service is your strength: Field service is your passion, and your high customer orientation and willingness to serve distinguish you.
  • Engagement & personal initiative: With above-average motivation and entrepreneurial spirit, you are ready to achieve great things and exceed targets.
  • Regional anchoring: Your place of residence is in the Bischofszell region, where you look after your clients. You have a strong network in the Bischofszell region and appreciate building and maintaining new contacts.
