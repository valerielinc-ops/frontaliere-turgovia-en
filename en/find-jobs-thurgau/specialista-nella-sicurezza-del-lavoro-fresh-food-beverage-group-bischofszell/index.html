Full description

In this responsible key function, you take on the topics of occupational safety, health protection and fire protection. You ensure that legal requirements and internal standards are adhered to, strengthen the safety culture sustainably and actively promote effective prevention and protection measures.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Bischofszell, Industriestrasse 1, 9220 Bischofszell

Mansioni

  • Incident investigations on occupational safety and health protection incl. Evaluations and action planning
  • Head of the intervention group (damage prevention)
  • Implementation of organisational fire protection
  • Support for technical fire protection (including integral tests)
  • Collaboration on various projects

Requirements

  • Completed initial or further training: training as a safety professional and/or specialist ASGS

  • Patient, assertive personality
  • Basic education with a technical background
  • Possible assumption of the deputy management of SHE
