Full description

Are you a well-positioned, team-oriented personality and would you like to actively shape the future of our people work? Then you've come to the right place!

To strengthen our dedicated team, we are looking for a people generalist who will take on a key role as a central contact point for our employees. In addition to your role as a People Business Partner, you will also take on tasks in the People Operations area and actively contribute to strengthening and further developing our Zur Rose culture.

Tasks

  • Responsible for supporting all employees in the area of responsibility throughout the entire employee life cycle
  • Advice and support for employees and managers on personnel and labor law issues
  • Supporting managers in the recruitment process (including the entire recruitment process and salary policy)
  • Close exchange and collaboration with our managers, such as in supporting change processes or conflict situations
  • Support in identifying development measures, succession planning and talent management for all employee levels
  • Participation in administrative HR processes relating to the employee life cycle from entry and exit as well as in the event of mutations
  • Ensuring high-quality HR services
  • Participation in the implementation and further development of our HR processes and HR projects

Strengths

  • Completed further training to become an HR specialist
  • Several years of experience as a people business partner or HR generalist, preferably in a comparable environment
  • Strong communicator and empathetic personality
  • Solution-oriented, service-oriented and committed way of working
  • Experience with HR tools such as Workday, Abacus and RTM an advantage
  • Experience in supervising learners including vocational training courses
  • Professional and well-groomed appearance
  • German native language, R/D knowledge is desirable

About us With our innovative and effective pharmaceutical solutions, we at Zur Rose do everything we can to positively influence people's lives. Our team of 500 employees, headquartered in Frauenfeld, pursues the common goal of improving the future of healthcare. Since quality, comfort and effectiveness are particularly important to us, patients can take their health into their own hands easily, quickly and safely. Our aim is to predict future requirements in the healthcare system. When developing our solutions and medicines, we rely on a high level of reliability and efficiency and take care of complex issues so that our customers can sit back and relax.

Cross-functional teamwork is encouraged through our corporate culture, so that every team member is equally valued and encouraged to actively participate in the decision-making process. Open communication and empathy are our top priorities. We always try to find a common denominator and thus avoid compromises.

At Zur Rose, our passion is to positively impact the world. Do you share the same values ​​and do you also want to improve people's lives? Then we would be happy to welcome you as part of our team.
