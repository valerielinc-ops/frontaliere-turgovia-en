Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita foundry in Arbon TG we are looking from 15. October 2026 or by agreement a committed Employee/in housekeeping EFZ 80 - 100% with professional experience. Your tasks You take on versatile and responsible activities that enrich the everyday life of our residents and guests. Your tasks include:

  • The professional cleaning and care of public spaces, senior citizens and holiday apartments as well as the care departments to ensure a pleasant and hygienic environment.
  • The management of materials as well as the maintenance of facilities and furniture to ensure a smooth operation.
  • The help with seasonal decoration, where you can let your creativity run wild to create a welcoming atmosphere.
  • The active participation and development of your area by thinking in everyday life and bringing in innovative ideas. With your commitment and your experience, you make a significant contribution to making our residents and guests feel comfortable.
  • support for laundry care and distribution of professional and resident linen.
  • Compliance with standards in both hygiene and safety areas, in accordance with the specifications of the company.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a housekeeping specialist EFZ or a comparable qualification.
  • Professional experience in a similar function, ideally in the nursing home or in a comparable environment, sets you apart.
  • Your diplomatic skills, discretion and strong empathy with older people make you a valuable support for our team.
  • You work reliably, are capable of teamwork and bring a high level of service orientation.
  • You are flexible about your working hours, also on weekends and public holidays.
  • Your knowledge of German is impeccable in word and writing and a basic requirement for communication with our residents and guests.
  • If you enjoy working in a dynamic environment and make a positive contribution with your commitment, we look forward to your application!
