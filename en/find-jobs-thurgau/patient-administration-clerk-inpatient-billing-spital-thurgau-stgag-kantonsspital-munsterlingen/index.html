Full description

You keep track when it gets demanding, like to work exactly and really want to make a difference with your skills? Become part of our stationary billing from January 1, 2027 and take on an important role behind the scenes of the hospital operation.

Personnel/in patient administration – stationary invoicing

80 - 100%

Do you know the Swiss healthcare system and are familiar with the procedures surrounding health insurance? With us you can contribute your knowledge, take responsibility and develop yourself professionally. You can expect an experienced team that sticks together, supports each other and masters challenges together.

You take on

  • Testing and correct billing of inpatient treatment cases
  • Application of tariffs, legal bases, contracts and internal requirements
  • Processing of rejections and inquiries from health and accident insurance
  • Professional correspondence with clinics, insurance companies, patients and internal bodies
  • Collaboration in process optimizations and technical projects within invoicing

You bring with you

  • Completed training in a medical, health or commercial environment
  • Professional experience in Swiss health or health insurance is an advantage
  • Good understanding of administrative processes in healthcare
  • Ability to quickly capture, correctly assess and reliably implement complex issues
  • Very precise, structured and responsible working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, foreign languages are advantageous
  • Secure handling of common IT applications

Look forward to a versatile and technically exciting job at the interface of medicine, administration and insurance. We provide a well-founded introduction, give you space to expand your expertise and offer you a working environment in which you can contribute and make a difference.

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities.
