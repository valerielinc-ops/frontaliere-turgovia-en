Full description

To complement our surgical storage team, we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by agreement one or more

OP storage keeper

100%

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld has a versatile and varied operation department. Every year we carry out approximately 9000 inpatient and outpatient procedures in the fields of surgery, orthopaedics, traumatology, gynecology and obstetrics as well as urology, ORL, hand and plastic surgery. The newly opened operation department in 2022 comprises 8 operating rooms and 2 urological intervention rooms.

They take over

As a storage care provider, you are an important part of our medical treatment team in the operating room. They are responsible for the correct and operation-specific storage of patients before, during and after the operation. They work closely with the anesthesia and surgery teams to ensure the safety and well-being of our patients.

  • Preparation of the operating tables for the individual storage of patients
  • Reception and preparation of patients in the operating room in cooperation with the anesthesia or surgery team
  • Professional storage of patients during the operation
  • Supporting the surgical team in the general preparation of the operating room
  • Provision, operation and maintenance of medical equipment
  • Entering the services provided on the PC

They bring with them

  • Professional experience as surgical warehouse maintainer, if possible with completed specialist course in surgical warehouses. Alternatively, training as a FAGE with an interest in working in the operating room. New entrants with professional experience in the operating room, in care or in a comparable medical environment are also welcome
  • Strong teamwork and high social competence
  • Great reliability, responsible and careful working methods
  • Good physical constitution, resilient and flexible
  • Technical flair and skill
  • Readiness for shift and weekend work
  • PC knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing (minor level B2)
  • If suitable, it is possible after at least 6 months to attend the specialist course in operations camps

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself to you

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
