Full description

You want to work flexibly, get to know different teams and actively shape your service planning? Then you fit us perfectly. To complement our team in the surgery pool, we are looking for an hourly wage from immediately or by agreement.

Health specialist EFZ in the surgery pool

30-60%

You work without a fixed station on all 3 surgical bed stations and thanks to individual planning assignments you actively shape your services - ideal if you value diversity and flexible working models.

You take over

  • the care and support of patients before and after surgery
  • the implementation of primary care and support for mobilisation and activation
  • diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Tasks in the Station Secretariat
  • Team-oriented and interdisciplinary cooperation with qualified nurses and other professional groups
  • Share responsibility in the training of our learners

You bring with you

  • Completed vocational training as a health EFZ specialist with a federal certificate of competence EFZ
  • Experience in Acute Hospital
  • A careful and forward working method
  • Adaptability, independence and responsibility
  • a quick understanding and willingness to engage with different teams
  • very good communication and service skills

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
