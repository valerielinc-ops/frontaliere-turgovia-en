Put to the Point

The postdoctoral researcher will work on two projects; from 1 March 2027 to 31 July 2028 on the project “Enhancing Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services through Legume Production and Dietary Change” funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation, then from 1 August 2028 to 31 July 2030 on the project “Addressing Hard-To-Abate Emissions to Reach the Net-Zero Target of Switzerland” (https://www.sweet-achieve.ch/about). To this end, he/she will work in the group “Agricultural Economics and Policy” at ETH Zurich (Robert Finger).

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

Additional information

Swiss agriculture is in the field of tension of various social expectations. It is intended to provide for the population, the preservation of natural resources, the care of the cultural landscape, decentralised settlement and animal welfare. The research group Economic Modelling and Policy Analysis evaluates the effects of agricultural policy instruments on societal goals on the basis of economic models and empirical methods and shows conflicting goals.
