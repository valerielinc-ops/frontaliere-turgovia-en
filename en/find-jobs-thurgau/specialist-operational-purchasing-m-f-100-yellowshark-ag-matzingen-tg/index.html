Our client is looking for an experienced professional for operational procurement and material requirements planning in an industrial environment. The role includes timely material supply, active management of suppliers, and close collaboration with production, planning, and logistics. In addition, the position offers the opportunity to contribute to the optimization and digitalization of procurement processes.

02 Your task

  • Operational procurement and material requirements planning to ensure material supply
  • Review of requirements and follow-up on order confirmations and delivery dates
  • Active management of suppliers and early handling of supply bottlenecks
  • Optimization of inventories and procurement-related master data
  • Analysis of operational KPIs and participation in process standardization and digitalization
