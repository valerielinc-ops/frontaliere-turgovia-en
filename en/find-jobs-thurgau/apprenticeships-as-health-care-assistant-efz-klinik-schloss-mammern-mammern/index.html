Full description

The Klinik Schloss Mammern, embedded in an impressive landscape, is a leading private clinic for every phase of rehabilitation and has been run since 1889 as a family business in the meantime 4th generation. With around 440 dedicated employees, we offer first-class medical care and therapy in four disciplines: cardiological, internistic-oncological, musculoskeletal and pneumological. Our patients enjoy an upscale hotel industry at five-star level in a stylish atmosphere and with the latest infrastructure. Starting in August 2027, we offer the following exciting vocational training: Specialist Health EFZ/Fachmann Health EFZ (FaGe) 3-year course from August 2027 As a specialist in health, you work with us in rehabilitation and get to know different specialist areas. You support our patients on the way to their recovery in rehabilitation.

You perform the following activities

  • In the care and care you support patients e.g. in personal care, eating and moving.
  • You learn with the help of communication techniques a mindful approach to patients.
  • Your observations and suggestions are incorporated into the care planning of the qualified nurses.
  • In the area of living environment and everyday life design, you support people in high adulthood in their daily routine.
  • In the field of medical technology, you take care of the blood draws, part of the wound care or administer tube food and learn many other interesting medical technology activities.
  • In addition, you take on varied administrative and logistical tasks regarding material, medication and organize various other assignments.

You can find further descriptions of the professional profile of health professionals under OdASanté.
