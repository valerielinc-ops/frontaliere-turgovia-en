Full description

For the versatile, broad-based outpatient and inpatient neuropsychological diagnosis with responsibilities in the Memory Clinic, ADHD assessment, psychiatric, neurological and geriatric wards in Münsterlingen as well as the Stroke Unit in the Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen and Frauenfeld, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

Neuropsychologists

80-100%

They take over

  • Neuropsychological investigations in people with psychiatric and neurological disorders in the ambulatory and inpatient setting
  • Documentation and interpretation of findings
  • Advice to affected persons and relatives
  • Presentation of neuropsychological findings in interdisciplinary teams, together with the doctors
  • Supervision of colleagues in training without specialist title

They bring with them

  • Started or advanced or completed postgraduate training as specialist psychologist for neuropsychology
  • Well-founded neuropsychological and test psychological experience as well as clinical psychiatric and neurological prior knowledge
  • High independence and personal responsibility in a multi-professional treatment setting

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves to you

The Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) together with the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. Every year, we treat more than 7,500 patients and offer specialized outpatient, outreach and daily clinical services in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the AEP as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau a highly attractive employer.

We prefer direct applications, job agents please refrain from contacting.
