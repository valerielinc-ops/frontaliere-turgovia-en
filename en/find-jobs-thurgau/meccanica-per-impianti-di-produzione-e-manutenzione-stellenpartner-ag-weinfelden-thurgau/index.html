Full description

Complex faults at production plants are quickly eliminated in this versatile application. The operation ensures with you the availability of modern sheet metal processing machines. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Rapidly analyse and correct malfunctions at production facilities
  • Mechanical maintenance and repair of equipment
  • Ensure availability of sheet metal processing equipment
  • Supporting new machines during installation and commissioning
  • Implement technical improvements for stable processes

Requirements

  • Basic technical training as a polymechanic or equivalent
  • Multi-year practice in industrial maintenance is ideal
  • Broad mechanical expertise in pneumatics and hydraulics
  • Experience in conventional turning and milling
  • Readiness for occasional piquettes and flexible working hours

Offer

  • Various tasks in the modern production environment
  • At least five weeks of vacation
