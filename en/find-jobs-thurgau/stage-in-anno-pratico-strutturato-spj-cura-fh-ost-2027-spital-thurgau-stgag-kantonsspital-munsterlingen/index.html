We offer internships as

Internship in Structured Practical Year (SPJ) Care FH OST 2027

100%

Patient-friendly, eager to learn, motivated, empathetic, accurate and flexible - that's us. Do these values apply to you? Then apply to us – we look forward to you!

You take over

  • As an intern SPJ, you support and support our patients in everyday hospital life and actively participate in the care. You are part of a nursing team of qualified nurses, health specialists and trainees from the various nursing professions.
  • In your area of competence, you carry out various nursing and medical-technical activities such as simple personal care, vital sign control, everyday design, change of dressing as well as domestic, logistical and administrative tasks.
  • As an intern you make an important contribution to the promotion resp. Maintaining the health and well-being of patients

You bring with you

  • You like working in a team
  • You are reliable and responsible
  • You are physically and psychologically resilient
  • You are interested in nursing and working in nursing
  • High School Matura
  • Specialist education in a different direction than health
  • Non-occupational or vocational Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) and professional diploma
  • Completed theory block at the OST

The Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!

We attach great importance to a varied and exciting learning environment in which you can achieve the goals of the internship. Motivated education and nursing teams will gladly accompany, promote and support you.

We prefer direct applications, job agents please refrain from contacting.
