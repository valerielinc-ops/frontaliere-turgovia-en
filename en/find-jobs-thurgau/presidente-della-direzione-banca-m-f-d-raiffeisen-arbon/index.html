27.11.2026 Arbon

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Regio Arbon is a well-positioned and locally oriented bank with offices in Arbon and Steinach. As an independent cooperative banking institution of the Raiffeisen Group, it operates successfully in the market. Thanks to continuous growth in recent years, the bank today has a balance sheet total of around CHF 1.1 billion. 46 employees provide daily with enthusiasm and commitment for satisfied, needs-based cooperatives and customers. In order to continue to develop our bank in the coming years, we are looking for an entrepreneurial leader as Chairman of the Bank Management.

What do you expect?

  • You lead the operational banking business and together with the employees implement the strategic guidelines of the Board of Directors
  • You set priorities in the optimization of the sales organization and in the increase of consulting and sales competence
  • As an entrepreneur, you recognize potential in a challenging market and develop ideas on how to exploit it to ensure healthy growth of the bank.
  • They consistently align the bank with the needs of our customers
  • You maintain an active network with different stakeholders

What do you bring?

  • You are an entrepreneurial personality with proven leadership skills
  • As a charismatic leader, you bring your experience and act pragmatically
  • They manage to bundle different views and lead to a decision
  • They act as a motor for upcoming process optimizations, have a high level of implementation competence and overcome obstacles
  • You identify with the unique Raiffeisen culture and also live our values in your leadership work
