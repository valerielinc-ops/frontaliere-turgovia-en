Further training for / to the Dipl. Experts in Anesthesiology NDS HF

100%

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld has a versatile and varied operation department. Every year, we carry out around 9 000 inpatient and outpatient procedures in the fields of surgery, orthopaedics, traumatology, gynecology and obstetrics as well as urology, throat-nose-ear healing, hand and plastic surgery and dentistry. The newly opened department in 2022 comprises eight modern operating rooms and two urological intervention rooms.

You take on

  • Participation in anesthesia monitoring and management in constructive cooperation with the medical service
  • Care of patients in the perioperative phase, in emergency situations and in diagnostic interventions
  • Practical use within the framework of the post-graduate study in anesthesia care
  • Use in a broad range of surgical disciplines

You bring with you

  • You are a committed and open-minded personality with a tertiary level degree as a dipl. Nursing specialist HF or a Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH – or have an equivalent education
  • At least six months of professional experience in a somatic acute care sector at 100%
  • You are drawing You stand out through manual skill and technical flair, are flexible, team-oriented and communicative – and want to Develop yourself professionally
  • For foreign nursing degrees, we presuppose that the diploma is recognised as equivalent by the State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SBFI) and has been registered with the Swiss Red Cross (SRC). Applications from abroad can only be considered after SRC registration has been completed.

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself to you

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
