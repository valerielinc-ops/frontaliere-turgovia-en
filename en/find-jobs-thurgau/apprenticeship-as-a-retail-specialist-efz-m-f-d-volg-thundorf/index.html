LANDI THULA Genossenschaft is an agricultural, retail and energy trading company based in Matzingen. With approximately 160 employees, we operate four Landi shops, six Volg shops, a primary shop, a top shop, eight gas stations and four agricultural locations. We offer regional agriculture a comprehensive range of production tools. With our stores, fuels and renewable energies, we supply the entire region from Hörnli to Thurland efficiently and sustainably.At the beginning of the apprenticeship in August 2027, we offer you an optimal education in our VOLG shop in Thundorf. You are an established, sociable and motivated personality and are interested in the apprenticeship as a retail specialist EFZ (m/f/d) "Food: Designing Shopping Experiences"? Then you are exactly right with us!

Your tasks

  • You complete the 3-year apprenticeship as a retail specialist EFZ
  • You learn to serve the customer professionally and are a competent contact person and actively sell our products
  • You acquire a comprehensive industry, specialist and assortment knowledge
  • You present the goods in the store attractively, so that it stimulates the purchase
  • You help with warehouse management, ordering and online orders
  • Working at the cashier is also one of your tasks

Your profile

  • As a secondary student, you bring good academic achievements
  • Daily customer contact and advice will give you pleasure
  • You are reliable, honest and helpful
  • You have a positive attitude towards work and are motivated to achieve your learning goals

About us

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small spaces in German and French Switzerland. We live close to customers – as a close shopping opportunity as well as in personal contact. The employees are the heart of the shop and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That’s why our motto is “fresh and cheerful”.

You can look forward to that.
