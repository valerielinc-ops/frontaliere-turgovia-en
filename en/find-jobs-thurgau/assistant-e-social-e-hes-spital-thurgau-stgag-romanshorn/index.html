To complement the multi-professional team of our day clinics, we are looking for one from 1 December 2026

Social worker/FH

80%

Are you looking for a working environment that is more than just a place of daily work? With us you experience an inspiring working atmosphere. Our corporate culture is characterized by cohesion, respect and appreciation.

With an employment rate of 80%, you work in three day clinics: the Romanshorn Psychiatric Day Clinic, the Romanshorn Old Age Clinic and Weinfelden Old Age Clinic. As a social worker, you are a valuable addition to the treatment teams, consisting of doctors, psychologists, nurses and specialist therapists. It awaits you a varied, exciting and meaningful activity.

They take over

  • Assessing the current social situation of patients and defining the social problem areas in cooperation with the multi-professional team and those affected
  • Advice and support of patients and their relatives in matters of work, finance and housing as well as social security matters
  • Maintain contacts with authorities and follow-up institutions
  • Participation in exit management and the organization of further supply and / or new forms of housing
  • Participation in multi-professional reports, team meetings and supervision
  • Coordination of social work tasks involving the entire patient environment and the multi-professional team

They bring with them

  • Completed studies in social work FH
  • When studying abroad: recognition of the professional qualification in Switzerland or the willingness to acquire it
  • Flexibility, independence, resilience and teamwork
  • Professional experience in psychiatric or medical context is beneficial
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss social system
  • Basic understanding, empathy and communication skills in dealing with mentally ill people

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves to you

The Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) together with the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. With over 280 beds and specialized offerings in the areas of psychotherapy, general and acute psychiatry, dependency disorders, age psychiatry and forensics, we offer evidence-based and qualitatively optimal therapies for the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases. An appreciative culture combined with excellent education and training make PKM a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.
