Full description

The acute somatic emergency is a highly dynamic environment in which teams perform at their best on a daily basis. At the same time, complexity, speed and expectations of emergency care are also increasing for people with psychiatric problems. For our emergency departments at the cantonal hospitals Münsterlingen and Frauenfeld we are looking for a committed and experienced personality as a nursing expert in APN psychiatry 80-100% In this demanding interface function between psychiatry and somatics, as a nursing expert of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau in close coordination with the medical psychiatric consular service, you play a central role in psychiatric care within somatics and emergency setting and contribute significantly to the quality and safety of patient care. You coordinate the cooperation between the emergency team and psychiatry, as well as external partners (police, rescue service) and actively contribute your expertise to interdisciplinary decision-making You take over crisis interventions in emergency setting and support in the de-escalation of stressful or escalating situations You bear the professional responsibility for the psychiatric care quality and develop evidence-based standards and concepts in the emergency area You advise, train and accompany the treatment team in dealing with psychiatric emergencies You organize further clarifications (e.g. somatic diagnostics or consultants) and ensure a smooth cooperation at the interfaces Several years of professional experience in psychiatric care, Master's degree as an Advanced Practice Nurse (APN) with a focus on psychiatry or comparable qualification Strong clinical expertise as well as pleasure in counselling, coaching and knowledge transfer Strong multiprofessional and interdisciplinary communication and cooperation skills
