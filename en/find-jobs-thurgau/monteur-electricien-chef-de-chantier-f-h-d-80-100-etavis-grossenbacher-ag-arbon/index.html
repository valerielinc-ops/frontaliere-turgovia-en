ETAVIS Grossenbacher AG is one of the leading companies in the Swiss electrical installation and telecommunications market in Eastern Switzerland. In order to continue to maintain our high standard, we are looking for a customer-oriented, flexible, and committed person like you!

Your area of impact

  • Lead electrical installation teams as well as a wide variety of projects in the commercial, industrial, and service sectors

  • Carry out new installations, maintenance work, and repairs

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training as an electrician EFZ with a Swiss diploma or an equivalent qualification recognized in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience
  • Strong quality awareness, a professional demeanor, and an independent way of working
