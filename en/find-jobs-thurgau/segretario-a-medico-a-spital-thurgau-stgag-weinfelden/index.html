Full description

For our secretariat KJPD/EPD in Weinfelden/Frauenfeld (about 2 – 4x/week plus holiday representation in Frauenfeld) we are looking for 15. January 2027 or by agreement to strengthen our team

Medical secretaries

100%

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau (PDT), consisting of the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen, the outpatient area with day clinics and outpatients at four locations as well as the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service, treat mentally ill people of all age groups. At the Weinfelden site, the Child and Adolescent Psychiatry Service (KJPD) and the Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (EPD) are located under one roof.

They shall take over

  • In our secretariat, you are the first contact person for patients, referrals, authorities and insurance companies and perform, among other things, all administrative secretarial tasks such as scheduling office hours, collecting patient data via SAP, supporting the reception at the counter and telephone service as well as occasionally writing dictates from Diktaphon and editing reports.

They bring with them

  • You have completed vocational training as medical secretary and/or medical assistant and at least 3 years of professional experience in a medical secretariat.
  • You are a flexible, resilient, friendly and team capable personality with a sense of responsibility, are motivated and can not be put out of peace even in hectic times.
  • Your further qualifications include an accurate, forward-looking and independent working method, stylish German in word and writing. Good IT skills (MS Office, KISIM (Clinical Information System), SAP) round off your profile and are essential.

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves to you

The Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) Thurgau offers excellent conditions for classical child and adolescent psychiatric training as well as for comprehensive psychotherapeutic training. Every year, we treat more than 3,000 patients with high quality standards in family-friendly, outpatient, visiting and day-clinical settings, if possible with evidence-based therapy procedures (Multisystemic Therapy MST, Parent Child Interaction Therapy PCIT, ESDM for early intervention in autism). An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the KJPD a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.
