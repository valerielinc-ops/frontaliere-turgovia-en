Full description

The Klinik Schloss Mammern, embedded in an impressive landscape, is a leading private clinic for every phase of rehabilitation and has been run since 1889 as a family business in the meantime 4th generation. With around 440 dedicated employees, we offer first-class medical care and therapy in four disciplines: cardiological, internistic-oncological, musculoskeletal and pneumological. Our patients enjoy an upscale hotel industry at five-star level in a stylish atmosphere and with the latest infrastructure. The personal and heartfelt treatment of patients is important to us and we attach great importance to a long-term and mutually enriching cooperation. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a specialist health 80%-100 %

Responsibility

  • They are responsible for professional rehabilitation care, treatment and individual care of patients, including basic and treatment care in the 2-shift system (early and late shift).
  • They carry out patient and family education in delegation.
  • As part of the care team, you will work closely with the interdisciplinary team.
  • You take on administration and station regulations tasks.
  • They participate in the practical training of nurses and health professionals.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence as a specialist in health or a comparable diploma.
  • Your thinking and actions are patient- and customer-oriented.
  • Teamwork is yours and you bring a motivated and open-minded personality.
  • They have specialist and social competence as well as resilience, flexibility and a high degree of commitment.
  • The basic PC applications are familiar to you.
