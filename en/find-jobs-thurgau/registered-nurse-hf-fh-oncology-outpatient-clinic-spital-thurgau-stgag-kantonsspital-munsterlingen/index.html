Full description

In the two oncological ambulatories at the Münsterlingen and Frauenfeld sites, 27,000 consultations of patients with a tumor disease take place every year. The two ambulatories are part of the Thurgau Tumor Center, which is certified according to the German Cancer Society (DKG).

To complement the teams of the two locations, we are looking for a /- n

Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH Ambulatorium Oncologie

60-100%

Within our clinic, we value cooperation at eye level and personal contact in the interprofessional treatment team. We offer you a varied and exciting task in which you can actively implement your nursing expertise and your experience in accompanying oncological patients.

You can look forward to an experienced management team, the close professional support of experts and a targeted introduction according to your needs. After well-founded training at one of the two locations, an assignment at both locations takes place after consultation.

You take over

  • Care and support of outpatient oncological and/or haematoncological patients with drug tumor therapy
  • Administration of antitumor therapies according to applicable quality standards and guidelines
  • Monitoring the course of therapy and early detection and initiation of measures in the event of side effects or complications
  • Accompaniment and support of patients and relatives in coping with the various symptoms and effects of their disease or their therapy
  • Carrying out nursing assessments for symptom control
  • Patient education and advice on therapy and side effects management
  • Independent planning, implementation and evaluation of care as part of the care process

You bring with you

  • Diploma as Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH with several years of professional experience in oncology
  • Ideally, in-depth training in oncology (NDS Oncology, CAS in Oncology Care or equivalent)
  • In-depth knowledge in the administration and monitoring of antitumor therapies and supportive treatments
  • High professional and social skills in dealing with oncological patients and their relatives
  • Strong sense of responsibility, professionalism, flexibility and independence to engage in rapidly changing situations
  • Process- and solution-oriented thinking and responsible action for a committed, well-established treatment team

The Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
