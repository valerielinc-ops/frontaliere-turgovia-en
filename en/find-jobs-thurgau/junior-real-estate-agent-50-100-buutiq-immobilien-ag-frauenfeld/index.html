Engel & Völkers Frauenfeld

Engel & Völkers is a world-leading service provider of high-quality real estate. Our many years of experience combine a strong regional and worldwide network. At Engel & Völkers Eastern Switzerland you will experience professionalism, competence and real passion. As an established brand, we combine in-depth market knowledge with commitment and high standards of service and quality. People and real estate are a matter close to our hearts. You value a varied working day and would like to advance your career? With the support of our motivated team, your chances of success are particularly good.

Their tasks

  • Acquisition and building new customer relationships in your own farming area.
  • Advising and accompanying the entire sales process.
  • Conduct of object visits and sales negotiations.
  • Independent handling of sales until registration.

Your profile

  • They bring a strong passion for real estate.
  • You have been working as a real estate agent for more than 2-5 years.
  • It is easy for you to talk to different people and you enjoy building customer relationships.
  • They have a strong willingness to learn.
  • You convince through your reliable and trusting personality.
  • Pronounced initiative and good teamwork characterize you.
  • They bring a high degree of flexibility and commitment.
  • Experience in sales or in the real estate industry is an advantage.
  • You have completed a school education.
  • Appropriate knowledge of German is required.
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • You have a valid driving licence of category B.

Our offer

  • You are responsible for real estate marketing in your farming area and offer an excellent service from initial consultation to successful contract conclusion.
  • You make your day-to-day work flexible and proactive.
  • You work in a reliable and motivated team.
  • As a junior, you will be accompanied by your experienced site manager in the initial phase and will be looked after in a targeted manner so that you can enter the world of Engel & Völkers smoothly and be introduced to the level of a senior in a timely manner.
  • They use the strong Swiss and worldwide Engel & Völkers network.
  • They use modern software tools
  • Good further education opportunities are available at the internal Engel & Völkers Academy.
  • Permanent employment with performance-based delivery.

If you want to take your sales success into your own hands, we look forward to your application, including CV, letters of motivation and testimonials.
