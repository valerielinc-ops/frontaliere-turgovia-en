The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves to you

The Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) Thurgau offers excellent conditions for classical child and adolescent psychiatric training as well as for comprehensive psychotherapeutic training. Every year, we treat more than 3,000 patients with high quality standards in family-friendly, outpatient, visiting and day-clinical settings, if possible with evidence-based therapy procedures (multi-systemic therapy MST, Parent Child Interaction Therapy PCIT, ESDM for early intervention in autism). An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the KJPD a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.
