Full description

For our ambulatorium of Frauenklinik Spital Thurgau AG at the Frauenfeld site, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

Leading Med. Practice assistants/-in Ambulatorium Frauenklinik

90-100%

Do you love to keep track, motivate your team and make a difference together? Then you are exactly right with us! In our ambulatorium of the Frauenklinik am Kantonsspital Frauenfeld you can expect a versatile management task in which you contribute your ideas, actively shape processes and together with a committed team ensure a smooth everyday life and a hearty care of our patients.

Our varied everyday life includes gynecological and urogynecological consultation hours, gynecological oncology and senology, pregnancy checks, ultrasound examinations and minor surgical procedures. Consultation assistance, patient coordination and administrative activities are also part of our broad remit. With us, you combine your passion for medicine with your pleasure in leadership, bring your own ideas and actively shape the future of our ambulatory together with your team.

They shall take over

  • They ensure the personnel and professional leadership of the MPA's, the medical secretariat and the station assistance in the women's clinic
  • You take over the recruitment, introduction and promotion of employees as well as the service and holiday planning
  • You are responsible for the organization and coordination of consultation hours as well as efficient capacity and resource management
  • They ensure a smooth workflow in the ambulatory and actively support the team in day-to-day business with patient care, office hours assistance and administrative tasks.
  • They coordinate interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors and other internal and external interfaces
  • You take on administrative and organizational tasks and participate in the continuous optimization and standardization of our work processes

They bring with them

  • You have completed training as a Med. Practice assistant and bring several years of professional experience, ideally in an acute hospital
  • They have several years of management experience and enjoy the leadership, promotion and further development of employees
  • Ideally, you have already completed or are ready to complete the further training for the Med. Praxisberatung (MPK)
  • You have strong organizational skills, work independently and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • You are a communicative, empathetic and team-oriented personality
  • You are well versed in dealing with digital applications and ideally already have experience with SAP and KISIM
  • You think solution-oriented, like to take on responsibility and actively shape changes

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
