PROFILE

  • Completed degree (FH/ETH/TU) in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, mechatronics or industrial engineering or comparable degree
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (level B2), knowledge of Italian is advantageous
  • Experience in licensing and technical knowledge of rail vehicles is an advantage
  • Open, communicative and flexible personality with independent, structured and forward-looking working methods
  • Affinity for process-compliant use of requirements, proof and document management systems (e.g. DOORS, Teamcenter) TASKS
  • Responsible for the licensing and certification activities in international rolling stock modernization projects
  • Coordination with authorities, infrastructure managers, assessment bodies and experts
  • Coordination with engineering departments, type test management and Stadler service locations
  • Development of generic approval concepts for comprehensive modernization topics on rail vehicles
  • Advising engineering and product development on approval issues in the sales phase
  • Assessment of the changed configuration for approval and safety relevance in cooperation with the Safety Manager.
