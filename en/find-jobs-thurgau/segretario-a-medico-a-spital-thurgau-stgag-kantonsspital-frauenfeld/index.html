In order to strengthen our secretariat in the Medical Office, we are looking for a person as of March 2027 or by agreement

Medical secretary

40-50% (Monday and Tuesday)

They take over

  • Writing off e-dictate, correction of medical reports of internal medicine stationary, endocrinology, nephrology and consular neurology and dispatch
  • Reporting control
  • Correspondence and telephone calls with medical practices, hospitals, patients and insurance companies
  • Processing of electronic correspondence and hospital-internal e-mail inboxes
  • General administrative and organisational secretarial tasks

They bring with them

  • You have several years of professional experience as medical secretary
  • You have stylish German
  • You master the medical terminology
  • You have very good PC skills and can deal with a wide variety of programs such as MS-Office, KISIM and SAP and learning more in-house software will not cause you any problems.
  • You are a reliable, flexible and resilient personality
  • They are capable of teamwork, sociable, flexible and resilient
  • Fresh and exact work is a matter of course for you and you have a quick understanding
