Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed degree in computer science, information technology or comparable qualification
  • Several years of practical experience with IAM solutions in the enterprise environment, ideally One Identity Manager
  • Very good understanding of IAM concepts such as RBAC, ABAC, SSO, MFA, Identity Lifecycle Management and Access Governance
  • Experience with Active Directory, LDAP, Entra ID / Azure AD and hybrid IT and cloud environments
  • Good knowledge of PowerShell, SQL and ideally OneIM scripting and customizing
  • Structured, analytical and service-oriented working methods as well as strong communication and team skills in international environments

TASKS

  • Design, implementation and operation of the enterprise IAM platform based on One Identity Manager in hybrid IT environments (on-prem & cloud)
  • Implementation and further development of identity lifecycle processes such as joiner/mover/leaver, provisioning, de-provisioning, role management and access reviews
  • Development and automation of IAM processes using PowerShell, SQL and One Identity Manager Scripting & Customizing
  • Integration of target systems such as Active Directory, Entra ID, HR systems (e.g. SAP SuccessFactors), cloud platforms and business applications
  • Ensuring stability, performance, security and compliance of the IAM platform including monitoring, patch and release management
  • Support for audits and preparation and maintenance of technical documentation, operational processes and architectural documents

Requirements

  • Completed degree in Computer Science or comparable qualification

  • Several years of practical experience with IAM solutions

  • Very good understanding of IAM concepts

  • Experience with Active Directory and hybrid IT environments

  • Good knowledge of PowerShell and SQL

  • Structured and service-oriented way of working

  • Strong communication and teamwork skills

  • Experience with One Identity Manager
