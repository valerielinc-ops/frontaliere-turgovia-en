Full description

Our facilities are at the heart of our production – and that’s where you come in. As an operational mechanic, you ensure that everything runs smoothly, faults are fixed quickly and our technology stays up to date. You can expect a varied everyday life between maintenance, repair and optimization, in which your technical know-how and commitment make the difference. If you like to tackle, think and take responsibility, you are exactly right with us.

You can do that.

  • You ensure that our facilities run reliably - from scheduled maintenance to recurring maintenance such as valve revisions.
  • When it becomes technical, you are in your element: you replace seals, bearings and bring machines back to top shape.
  • In the event of disruptions, you remain calm and solution-oriented - in on-call service you are the first port of call for production.
  • You think along, question existing ideas and actively contribute your ideas to continuously improve our facilities.
  • You work with modern systems such as SAP and help build a future-oriented maintenance.

You bring that with you

  • You have a technical education (mechanics, electrics or similar) and like to work independently and solution-oriented.
  • You take responsibility, tackle and find pragmatic solutions even in challenging situations.
  • New things motivate you - whether new technologies, systems or technical challenges.
  • You are a team player you can rely on and at the same time organize your work independently.
  • You can be used flexibly (including Pikettdienst) and ideally bring additional know-how in electrical engineering or automation (e.g. NIV 13/15).

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
