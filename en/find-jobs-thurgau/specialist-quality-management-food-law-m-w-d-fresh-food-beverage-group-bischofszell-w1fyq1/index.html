You like to face new challenges every day and are looking for a varied job in which you can contribute your knowledge and professional experience in quality management.

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Test of the label templates "Good for printing"
  • Professional responsibility, management and documentation for label requirements.
  • Examination of new developments to ensureall information from the point of view of food law
  • Verification of raw material data in specifications
  • Maintenance of the database in the respective IT system for creating declarations and product specifications
  • Participation in projects

Requirements

  • Professional experience: 3 - 5 years: Food law
  • Higher School: Food Sciences
  • Completed basic training as a food technologist and professional experience in quality management of advantage (or study food sciences ETH / FH)
  • Very good IT knowledge, experience with SAP advantage
  • Good knowledge of food law
  • Strong enforcement, resilient and flexible
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (advantageous)
  • French (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications (FFB-Group): We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email.
  • Personal video or telephone conversation (FFB Group): For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round.
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (FFB-Group): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post.
