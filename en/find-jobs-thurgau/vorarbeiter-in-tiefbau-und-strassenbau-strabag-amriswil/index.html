Construction Foreman: Civil Engineering and Road Construction Your Contribution with Us

  • Deployment as a construction foreman in the areas of civil engineering, demolition, earthworks and road construction in the Eastern Switzerland region
  • Independent handling and execution of construction projects, as well as acquisition, bid processing and bid preparation
  • Management of the companies involved in construction from the start of construction to the completion of warranty work
  • Preparation of work calculations and site result control
  • Responsibility for site organisation, quantity surveying and billing as well as maintenance of work calculations
  • Control of the construction process and site results
  • Implementation and co-responsibility for work preparation and site organisation
  • Accurate and timely quantity surveying and billing
  • Responsibility for cost, schedule and quality issues
  • Participation and shaping of innovation topics in construction (e.g. BIM)

What Counts for Us

  • Completed basic training in construction with further training to become a certified construction foreman and/or engineer or equivalent
  • Several years of experience as a construction foreman in civil and road construction is an advantage
  • Convincing and confident appearance in negotiations

  • Very good MS Office skills and experience with RIB iTWO is an advantage
  • High commitment and reliability
  • Assertiveness, entrepreneurial thinking and acting as well as a strong goal orientation
  • Teamwork and leadership experience, negotiation skills, flexibility as well as an independent and responsible working style complete your profile
