Full description

For our specialized ward for internal medicine / acute geriatrics with 20 beds, we are looking for an agreement to supplement the care team.

Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH Medizin

60-100%

Within the medical clinic, we appreciate the cooperation on an equal footing and the personal contact in the interprofessional treatment team. We offer you a varied and exciting task in which you can contribute your nursing expertise and your experience in accompanying and caring for patients.

You can look forward to an experienced management team, the close technical support of experts on the station as well as a targeted introduction according to your needs.

You take over

  • Responsibility for the professional, holistic care and care of our patients and their relatives
  • Implementation of the care process from entry to exit in the sense of reference care
  • Supporting patients in regaining their independence and their everyday skills
  • Promotion and support of students and trainees
  • Goal- and solution-oriented cooperation in the interprofessional treatment team

You bring with you

  • Basic training as a qualified nurse HF/FH and professional experience in the acute hospital
  • Ideally, experience in the care and care of elderly people in the acute hospital
  • High sense of responsibility, resilience and independent working methods
  • Process- and solution-oriented thinking and responsible action for a committed, well-established treatment team
