Full description

Precisely produced workpieces and clean quality are at the heart of this task. Modern CNC-controlled machines and a lot of personal responsibility shape everyday work. On behalf of our customer we are looking for a Activities

  • Production of individual parts and small series on CNC-controlled milling and lathes.
  • programming, setting up and operating the CNC machines.
  • Independent machining of workpieces according to technical drawings.
  • High personal responsibility for quality assurance and compliance with quality standards.
  • Independent inspection and testing of the finished workpieces with regard to dimensional accuracy and quality.
  • Coordination and monitoring of production deadlines as well as ensuring timely order processing.
  • Responsibility for a smooth and efficient production process.

Requirements

  • Completed training as polymechanic/EFZ.
  • Several years of professional experience in CNC manufacturing.
  • In-depth knowledge in CAM programming as well as programming and setting up CNC machines.
  • Very good knowledge of turning and milling technology.
  • Independent, precise and efficient working method.
  • High sense of responsibility and a strong understanding of quality.
  • Secure reading and implementation of technical drawings.
  • A sense of responsibility in quality assurance and appointment monitoring.
  • Reliability, personal initiative and pleasure in working independently.

Offer

  • Interesting and varied activities in the production of single parts and small series.
  • Modern CNC-controlled lathes and milling machines.
  • Lots of personal responsibility and independent work.
  • Exciting development opportunities and targeted further training.
