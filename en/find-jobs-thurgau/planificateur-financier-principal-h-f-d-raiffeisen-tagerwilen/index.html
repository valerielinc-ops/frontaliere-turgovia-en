14.09.2026 Tägerwilen

Become part of our team!

With around 85 employees, a balance sheet total of CHF 2.6 billion and over 20,000 customers, we are one of the largest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland. At our location in Kreuzlingen, embedded in the exciting and diverse region on Lake Constance, we are strengthening our important strategic pillar – financial, pension and estate planning. Today we are a dedicated team of three specialists who accompany our customers in the most important financial decisions of their lives. Due to the increasing demand and our ambitious growth targets, we are looking for an independent, committed and winning personality as Senior Financial Planner (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Implementation of holistic financial planning focusing on pension and estate planning
  • Competent advice to customers on challenging issues relating to retirement, asset succession, inheritance law, provision and long-term asset structuring
  • Development of individual and needs-based solutions taking into account the personal goals, life situations and family circumstances of our customers
  • Conduct 360-degree needs analysis to identify customer needs and develop sustainable financial strategies
  • Close cooperation with our customer consultants, support in identifying potential and customer book planning
  • Active participation and further development of our competence centre for financial, pension and estate planning
  • Collaboration at customer events, specialist presentations, seminars and target group-specific market development measures
  • Ensuring high-quality advice in compliance with regulatory and strategic requirements

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking specialist training with higher education
  • Degree as a financial planner: with federal certificate, CFP®, MAS Financial Consulting or comparable qualification
  • Experience in financial planning with proven expertise in pension and estate planning
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss pension system, property succession, matrimonial property and inheritance law and tax issues
  • Experience with financial planning software such as Allfina/Logismata or similar applications
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and pleasure in further developing financial planning as an important advisory field within the bank
  • High customer orientation, strong communication and secure presence
  • Team player mentality combined with independent and structured working methods
