##

  • Your tasks

This exciting position offers you the opportunity to support our Brand Management and Sales & Marketing Excellence teams, each for 50% of your time, allowing you to gain valuable experience in both areas. Specifically, the position includes the following key areas: Brand Management:

  • Support with the market launch of new products and our communication online and offline.
  • Content management in our sia brand portal and media database.
  • Independent project work for the creation of communication materials. Sales and Marketing Excellence:
  • Independent execution of analyses and research in the fields of Market Intelligence and Sales.
  • Responsibility for various reports using state-of-the-art reporting tools.
  • Support for the Pricing Team.
  • Your profile

Have we sparked your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your application if you bring the following:

  • Education: Degree in business administration, marketing, economics, industrial engineering, business informatics, communications, or a similar field.
  • Experience and know-how: Good knowledge of MS Office, especially Excel
  • Personality: You are a team player and have an independent, structured, and reliable way of working.
  • Language: Good command of German and English, both written and spoken.

Contact & additional information

Start: from February 2027, for a period of 6 months. Enrollment at a higher education institution is a prerequisite for the internship.
