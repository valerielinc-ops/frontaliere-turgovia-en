Welcome to Medbase Frauenfeld

Our versatile medical center with its own ultrasound and X-ray is centrally located in the capital of the canton of Thuringia and is well connected to public transport. Around 20 employees from the disciplines of General Internal Medicine, Gynaecology or Dermatology, Physiotherapy or Manual Medicine work closely together interprofessionell.
