Full description

PROFILE

  • Creation and maintenance of workbooks incl. Documents, stamp cards and documents required
  • Definition and preparation of work instructions and assembly schedules
  • Establishment and maintenance of work packages in the PLM system (e.g. Teamcenter)
  • Transfer of E-BOM to M-BOM and maintenance of parts lists
  • Provision and bundling of all relevant technical documents and certificates
  • Definition of material packages and triggering material requirements for purchasing
  • Ensuring complete production documentation (what and how is manufactured)
  • Coordination with PPS and scheduling to ensure production readiness

TASKS

  • Basic technical training with further education (e.g. technician HF)
  • Several years of experience in work preparation or production
  • In-depth knowledge in rail vehicle construction or plant construction
  • Strong understanding of production processes and parts lists
  • Secure handling of PLM and ERP systems
  • Structured, independent working methods and a high level of personal responsibility
  • Strong communication and coordination skills
