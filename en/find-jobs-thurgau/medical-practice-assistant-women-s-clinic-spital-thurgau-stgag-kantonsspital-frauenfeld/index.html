For our ambulatorium of Frauenklinik Spital Thurgau AG at the Frauenfeld location, we are looking for a supplement to our team on 1 January. November 2026 or by agreement one or more

Med. Practical assistants /-in Frauenklinik

90-100%

Are you looking for a working environment that is more than just a place of daily work? With us you experience an inspiring working atmosphere. Our corporate culture is characterized by cohesion, respect and appreciation.

They shall take over

  • In this very varied activity, you are in close contact with our patients, their relatives, our doctors and are the contact person on site or on the phone.
  • Your responsibilities include the organization, preparation and assistance of consultation hours during gynecological and urogynecological examinations, ultrasound examinations, pregnancy checks and small-surgical procedures.
  • General secretarial tasks, such as writing medical reports, scheduling and triagulation of assignments and emergencies are also part of your tasks
  • They independently carry out blood sampling, vaccinations and other injections

They bring with them

  • They bring a completed education as a Med. Practical assistant / in EFZ or an equivalent education in health care with
  • You have good knowledge of medical terminology and, in the best case, already have experience in an acute hospital
  • You enjoy working together in a large, interprofessional team
  • Independent, forward-looking and exact work are a matter of course for you

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
