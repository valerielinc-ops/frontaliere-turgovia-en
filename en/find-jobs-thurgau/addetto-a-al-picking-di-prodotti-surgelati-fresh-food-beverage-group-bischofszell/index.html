Full description

You like to pick goods, have gained experience in logistics and like to work with full physical effort. If you do not mind working in the cold, we are happy to expect your application.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Bischofszell, Industriestrasse 1, 9220 Bischofszell

Important information

The workplace is located in Sitterdorf TG, about 10 minutes walk from the headquarters. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Picking of cooled and frozen products by means of pick-by-voice
  • Route-friendly touring composition

Requirements

  • 1-2 years: in picking
  • Completed initial or continuing education: in logistics
  • Readiness to work in the frozen area (-25°C)
  • Readiness for 2-3 shift work (Monday to Saturday)
  • SUVA recognised forklift certificate (S1, R1, R2)
  • German (flowing)

Cosa of friamo

  • Du culture: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are per you!
  • Cumulus points: 3 times points on food, 6 times points on all other products.
  • Handy subscriptions: discounted mobile subscriptions at Wingo, Digitec and Sunrise for you and if necessary for three other people you know.
  • Occupational pension provision: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
  • Club School Migros: Refund of 70% of the costs per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Sports and fitness offers: refund of CHF 200 per fitness annual subscription.
