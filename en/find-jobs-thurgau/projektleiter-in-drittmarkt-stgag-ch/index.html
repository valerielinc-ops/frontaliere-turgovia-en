Full description

Join us in developing the future of the Swiss health infrastructure!

thurmed Immobilien AG (TIAG) is the owner, client and operator of the comprehensive building park of the thurmed Group, which includes the cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the clinic St. Katharinental and the psychiatric services Thurgau. In this role, she develops areas and manages the healthcare properties over the entire life cycle - from strategy, planning and realization to and with everyday operations. The focus is on functional, flexible and sustainable infrastructures that optimally support the core medical processes.

With its experience and expertise, TIAG also supports selected third-party customers in their concerns regarding their healthcare real estate portfolio throughout the entire real estate cycle – strategically, analytically and consistently geared towards operations. The service portfolio “Third Market” spans an arc from organizational development and real estate valuation to real estate strategy and site development and the concrete planning and construction task.

Project manager/third market

80-100%

For our team in the third market division, we are looking for an experienced, committed and solution-oriented personality immediately or by agreement.

They shall take over

  • You are part of TIAG and support external healthcare providers in the role of a client representative or consultant within the framework of external mandates
  • These mandates include in particular the early SIA phases 1 and 2 as well as the planning phases. This includes real estate strategies, site developments as well as concrete project developments and quality assurance checks (QA) of ongoing planning and projects from the point of view of use and operation.
  • They analyze the client’s question and task and derive solution strategies and a structured approach concept from this.
  • You support in project organization and project initialization. They ensure that the necessary stakeholders are involved and form the project-specific planner and expert teams
  • For specific project developments and planning tasks, you support the client with comprehensive "order competence" and advise on the selection of the appropriate implementation and processing model.
  • They coordinate, lead and lead the mandate-specific project teams and ensure careful and timely handling
  • In addition, there is also the possibility to operate within TIAG’s own building portfolio.

They bring with them

  • You have a passion for complex operating properties and are looking forward to being part of the dynamic TIAG and participating in the new “Third Market” division.
  • You are looking forward to expanding your experience through in-depth insight into TIAG’s field of activity as well as into other healthcare organizations under external mandates.
  • You enjoy complex tasks and enjoy working with different stakeholders
  • You have an education as a dipl. Architect/in ETH/FH or comparable degree, ideally with a real estate economic training (e.g. MAS Real Estate Management)
  • You have several years of professional experience in the planning and execution of complex operational properties, preferably in Switzerland, and are well versed in project management.
  • You are familiar with procurement law, possible implementation models and contracting and can find your way around the building-specific standards and legislation in a targeted manner.
  • You are a strategically and entrepreneurially thinking and acting team player personality for whom a good communication culture is a matter of course
