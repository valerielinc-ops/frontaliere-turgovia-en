Full description

Would you like to lead and develop our national HR Service Delivery at our headquarters in Weinfelden? You want to take responsibility for 16 employees and lead four of them directly? Would you like to be the first point of contact for all HR inquiries from our employees and managers and part of the HR Services division? You have several years of experience managing a team and securing service level agreements? Then you are exactly right with us! HR Service Delivery from Lidl Switzerland serves all employees and managers of the more than 190 branches, 3 warehouse locations and the headquarters.

Your tasks

  • National management and further development of the central HR contact center including the handling of HR mass processes as well as ensuring all administrative processes in compliance with legal and company-internal guidelines
  • Support and advice as well as interface to our stakeholders in the context of all operational topics
  • Administration, maintenance and further development of HR and document management systems (including digital personnel files) as well as handling of HR documents in strict compliance with data protection regulations for securing sensitive employee data
  • Ensure management and provision of organisational structure in HR systems
  • Responsibility for process improvements as well as data quality and audit security in all relevant systems and documents
  • Responsibility for ensuring efficient and user-friendly HR services such as our HR portal, Lidl help hotline and chatbot for employees and managers
  • Participation in the further development / transformation of our HR systems (e.g. SF Employee Central)
  • Coordination and quality assurance of a structured HR knowledge management for all internal HR customers

Your profile

  • Completed studies (Uni/FH/HF) with focus on business administration, HR or an equivalent qualification (e.g. Federal HR Professional ID, CAS or MAS in HR)
  • Several years of in-depth experience in HR administration or in an HR Shared Service Center as well as several years of proven leadership experience
  • Sound knowledge of Swiss labour and social security law
  • Ideal experience with SAP HCM
  • High digital affinity and pleasure in the further development of HR processes, systems and structures
  • High willingness to change, strong hands-on mentality and a pragmatic, solution- and efficiency-oriented mindset
  • Perseverance and diplomatic skill in overcoming internal and external resistance
  • Resilient, integrity and responsible personality with strong team orientation
  • Negotiable German in word and writing as well as good English skills
