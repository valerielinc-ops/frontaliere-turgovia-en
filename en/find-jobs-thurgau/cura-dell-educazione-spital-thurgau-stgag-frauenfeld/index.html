Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau (Münsterlingen, Kreuzlingen, Weinfelden, Romanshorn) and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen.
