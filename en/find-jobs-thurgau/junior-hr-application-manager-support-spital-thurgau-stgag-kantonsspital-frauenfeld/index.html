Full description

Modern HR systems are a key success factor in the healthcare sector.

For our team, we are looking for a committed personality who enjoys HR IT and digital transformation.

From 1 January 2027 or by agreement we are looking for a

Junior HR Application Manager Support

60-70%

They take over

  • the role as the first point of contact for employees and managers in questions relating to our HR systems
  • First-level support and ensure timely, structured and solution-oriented processing of requests and disruptions
  • the analysis, prioritization and processing of incoming requests and coordinate the forwarding to second-level support or external partners as required
  • Tasks in user and authorization management within the HR system landscape
  • Support and training of users in dealing with HR systems
  • the preparation and maintenance of documentation
  • the support of the team in the implementation and securing of the monthly closing in the time management system Polypoint PEP
  • the implementation and documentation of system tests for changes and releases

They bring with them

  • Commercial or IT training completed
  • First experience in application/IT support, ideally in dealing with HR systems (e.g. Polypoint PEP, Carema, swiss+ etc.)
  • Enjoy contact with employees as well as a service-oriented and solution-oriented working method
  • Technical understanding and interest in digital HR processes
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
