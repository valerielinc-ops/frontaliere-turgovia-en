Expert catenary installations 80 – 100 % (m/f/d) Expert catenary installations 80 – 100 % (m/f/d) Expert catenary installations 80 – 100 % (m/f/d) Place of work Frauenfeld As an engineering firm, Bahninfra AG accompanies construction projects in or next to the track, from the feasibility study to realisation. Here, we take over individual project services or, as total and general contractors, accompany the entire construction phase of several disciplines, such as road, catenary, safety systems and cable systems. With our extensive experience in the field of project planning, realization and maintenance of railway infrastructure buildings, we are the ideal partner on the track. For exciting road renewal projects on the Swiss railway network, we are looking for an experienced specialist to strengthen our team. Expert catenary installations 80 – 100 % (m/f/d) You accompany the traction current and catenary work on site, ensure quality and safety and represent our interests to customers, project partners. Your tasks

  • Exercise of the function of expert catenary equipment in road renewal projects
  • Technical support, implementation and monitoring of traction current and catenary work during execution
  • Representation of the technical and economic interests of our company towards customers and project partners
  • Ensuring contract-compliant, standard-compliant and high-quality execution
  • Implementation of inspections, inspections and acceptances on construction sites
  • Assessment of technical issues and support in finding solutions on site
  • Close cooperation with construction management, project managers, railway engineering specialists and entrepreneurs

Your profile

  • Training as an expert mileage equipment (alternatively dipl.) Master grid electrician HF, electrical engineer HF or comparable training
  • Experience in implementing railway infrastructure projects
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • High safety and quality awareness (inspections and acceptances on construction sites)
  • Readiness for Switzerland-wide assignments, as well as occasional night and weekend assignments
  • Very good knowledge of German, knowledge of French advantageous
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Our offer

  • Demanding projects with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Professional environment with short decision paths
  • Modern work equipment and competent support
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Long-term prospects and development opportunities
  • Other benefits

Assuming responsibility, securing quality, successfully implementing projects. We look forward to meeting you. Please send your application documents to personal@mueller- Gleisbau.ch. For further information, please contact Timo Graber, CEO Bahninfra AG, at 052 557 91 18. Overview of PDF download
