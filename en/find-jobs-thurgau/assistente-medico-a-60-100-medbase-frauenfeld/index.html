Welcome to Medbase Frauenfeld

Our versatile medical center with its own ultrasound and X-ray is centrally located in the Thurgau cantonal capital and is well connected to public transport. Around 20 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine, gynecology or dermatology, physiotherapy or manual medicine work closely together interprofessionally. After consultation, we are also happy to carry out home visits. Our flexible working models and deputy solutions enable a good work-life balance.

As an MPA, you are the first contact person for our patients and ensure a pleasant practical experience. Medbase offers you a dynamic team that values quality and collegiality.

  • Reception and care of patients, including agenda planning and appointment coordination
  • Performance of laboratory tests, ECGs and X-rays
  • Assistance in small-scale surgery
  • Management and electronic billing of patient records
  • Ensuring smooth practices
  • Cooperation with the team in administrative tasks

What you bring

  • Education as a Med. Practical assistant/EFZ
  • Readiness for further training as a vocational trainee and/or in X-ray technology
  • Organizational talent and pleasure in patient care
  • Empathy and flexibility
  • Teamwork and humor in challenging situations
  • Good knowledge of German (minimum B2 level)

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from different disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
