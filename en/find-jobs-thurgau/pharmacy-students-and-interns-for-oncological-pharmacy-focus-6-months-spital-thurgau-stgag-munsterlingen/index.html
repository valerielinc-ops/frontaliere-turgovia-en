Full description

We are constantly looking for central cytostatics production

Pharmaceutical practitioners and faculty for focus on oncological pharmacy (6 months)

100%

We offer you a varied and demanding job in the oncological hospital environment. They work at both locations, Münsterlingen and Frauenfeld, directly at the oncological ambulance and are thus directly involved in the hospital. An initiative and motivated team awaits you.

Their tasks

With us, you have the opportunity to get to know the tasks of an oncological pharmacist. For this purpose, you will be actively involved in the day-to-day work of the Central Cytostatic Manufacture and learn the following processes:

  • Aseptic preparation of cytostatic and antibody therapies
  • Plausibility and release controls of oncological therapies
  • Construction and operation of the oncology software program Cato
  • Insight into the creation of oncological therapy protocols
  • Furthermore, you have the opportunity to get to know the other areas of hospital pharmacy in a short internship of 4 weeks.

They bring with them

  • You have successfully studied pharmacy and are in the 3rd year. Exam section or are currently a student of pharmacy in undergraduate studies
  • You are used to reliable and efficient work
  • They excel as a resilient, efficient and customer-oriented personality
  • They are interested in aseptic production and oncological pharmacy

Spitalpharmazie Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

Spitalpharmazie Thurgau AG in Münsterlingen supports and supplies various hospitals and health organizations in eastern Switzerland with medicines. The core areas are logistics, production of drugs and the operation of two cytostatic laboratories, information on all aspects of drug therapy and the operation of an own public pharmacy near the hospital.
