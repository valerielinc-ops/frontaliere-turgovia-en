Put to the Point

Diverse weapon systems, exciting technical challenges and a working environment in which your skills count – here you combine precision and technology with a task of particular importance.

  • Date of entry: 01.11.2026
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20644
  • Location: Haubitzenstrasse 11, 8500 Frauenfeld

You can make this contribution

  • Hand and hand guns, infantry armament, vehicle armament including weapon stations (on armoured wheeled vehicles)
  • Perform periodic inspections and work on various weapon systems and accessories according to technical instructions
  • Assignments as a specialist to the troops’ material returns (WEME) on site for technical inspections and repairs of weapons, weapons stations and weapons accessories
  • Technical support for troops and third parties on the occasion of material takeovers, initial equipment of recruiting schools and for military personnel dismissals
  • Carry out inspection and repairs in the area of personal armament (loan weapons)

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship with a federal certificate as a gunsmith, polymechanic, similar basic mechanical training or military training as a weapons mechanic
  • Category B driving licence, category C or C1E driving licence and SUVA recognised forklift
  • Dutiful, structured and precise working methods
  • Customer and solution-oriented service concept
  • Readiness for flexible use at various locations and teamwork

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
