Original description

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

Sales Manager

__

Are you passionate about drive technology and automation — and excited to bring technical expertise directly to the customer interface? ABB is looking for a Technical Sales Support Specialist to join our team in a hybrid working model.

If you have a solid background in drives and automation systems, enjoy working closely with customers, and want to be part of a team where technology meets business impact — this opportunity is for you!


The work model for the role is: #LI-Hybrid

This role is contributing to the Machine Automation Division in Switzerland.


You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Perform motor sizing, concept development, and topology design to deliver technically sound sales solutions

  • Prepare and manage technical quotations accurately and efficiently

  • Maintain and improve sales demonstration setups, and provide technical support to the order management team

  • Engage directly with customers during technical discussions and solution development

• Qualifications for the role:

  • Minimum 2 years of professional experience in a customer-facing technical role

  • Solid knowledge of drive technology and automation systems

  • Completed higher education (FH or HF) in Automation, Robotics, or a related field

  • Proficient in Microsoft Office applications; SAP knowledge is a plus

  • Confident, professional presence with strong communication and interpersonal skills

  • German as native language; proficient in English (written and spoken)


More about us:

Machine Automation is the global center for Machine Automation within the ABB Group.
