Full description

Care with heart and mind – We are looking for you!

You are an expert in health EFZ and would like to work in a warm environment in which humanity and team spirit are at the centre? Then you fit us perfectly!

We are a motivated team in geriatric rehabilitation and are looking for reinforcement by dedicated professionals who provide with pleasure and expertise for the well-being of our guests.

For the house Kronenhof in Berlingen we are looking for an

Professional health EFZ for geriatric rehabilitation (50-100%) with and without vocational trainer function

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and care of patients in the sense of our care mission statement and the care process
  • Carrying out care activities
  • Electronic maintenance documentation
  • Taking on responsibilities as a reference person
  • Cooperation in the implementation of the care process
  • Assuming daily responsibility
  • Assumption of tasks as a topic carrier, technical authority
  • Participation in the interdisciplinary reports
  • In the vocational trainer function comes the supervision of our learners, with the accompaniment in the learning sequences, the preparations of the exams and the implemented of the learned from the courses.

Your profile

  • Completed training for EFZ or equivalent training recognised by the SRC.
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Responsibility and teamwork
  • Humor and the ability to keep track even in turbulent everyday life
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
