Full description

You have experience in the food sector and a flair for machines and plants? Then together with our team produce ravioli, various sauces and many other fine articles. The job is also suitable for trained cooks.

Luogo di lavoro: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Bischofszell, Industriestrasse 1, 9220 Bischofszell

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Manufacture of sauces on marinox cookers
  • Operating the ravioli plant
  • Operating the Bolzkocher for Marroni
  • General assistance in production
  • Cleaning of equipment

Requirements

  • 1-2 years: in the food industry (advantageous)
  • Completed initial or continuing education: as a cook or in the food sector
  • Healthy, robust physical condition
  • SUVA recognized truck test desired
  • Readiness for shift and weekend work
  • German (flowing)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Du culture: Hoi, hoi zämä, sali - we are per you!
  • Sports and fitness offers: refund of CHF 200 per fitness annual subscription.
  • Club School Migros: Refund of 70% of the costs per course (max. CHF 1,000 per course).
  • Cumulus points: 3 times points on food, 6 times points on all other products.
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages.
  • Occupational pension: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services.
