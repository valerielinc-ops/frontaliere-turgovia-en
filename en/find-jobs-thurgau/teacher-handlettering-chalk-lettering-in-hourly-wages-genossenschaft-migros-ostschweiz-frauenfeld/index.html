Full description

The Migros Club School is the largest adult education provider in Switzerland. The cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland operates 7 club schools in which our customers are offered more than 600 different courses and courses in a pleasant teaching atmosphere.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club School Frauenfeld, Bahnhofstrasse 70-72, 8500 Frauenfeld

Mansioni

  • You will design the lessons in Handlettering / Chalk Lettering professionally according to adult education criteria, taking into account the different life experiences, learning styles and needs of the participants
  • Your joy in teaching, your commitment and your winning appearance inspire the participants
  • You create a positive learning atmosphere that encourages learners’ motivation and build relationships based on trust and respect.
  • Through regular agreements with the relevant contact persons of the club school administration you ensured a smooth cooperation

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the field of adult education
  • Ideally, you will have the SVEB certificate 1 or an equivalent degree
  • You distinguish yourself through self-reflection and empathy towards the participants
  • You design your work responsibly and independently
  • You communicate clearly, effectively and respectfully
  • Initial or continuing education completed
  • German (very good knowledge)
