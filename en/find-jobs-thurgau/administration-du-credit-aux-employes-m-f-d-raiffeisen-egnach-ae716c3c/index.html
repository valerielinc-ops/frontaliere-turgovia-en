Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Neukirch-Romanshorn, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Neukirch (Egnach) and Romanshorn, we are looking for a service-oriented personality as Stv. Head of Credit Processing.

What awaits you?

  • You check loan applications from private and corporate customers for compliance with regulatory requirements and support our credit consulting team in the preparation competently and reliably.
  • You process new loan transactions from the preparation of the contract documents to the timely payment.
  • You manage the current credit positions and take on varied tasks around the credit business.
  • You take over the deputy of the team leader and coordinate the day-to-day business in his absence, set priorities, support the team with technical questions and ensure compliance with our quality and control standards.
  • You are actively involved and shape the further development of our credit processing.

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking training and experience in the Swiss credit business (private and / or corporate customers).
  • Ideally, you already have experience in credit processing and can use your knowledge in a targeted manner.
  • You are characterized by an independent, exact and responsible way of working.
  • You enjoy working in a team, want to actively shape processes and bring your own ideas to the further development of our credit administration.

Do you enjoy a varied and exciting activity? Then you are exactly right with us! Become part of our team, actively shape the future of our bank.
