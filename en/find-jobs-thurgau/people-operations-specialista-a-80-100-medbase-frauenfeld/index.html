Full description

To complete our team, we are looking for a service- and process-oriented personality. In this role, you are the first point of contact in the people team and therefore take on a key script writing function.

Tasks

  • Responsible for the operational handling of all HR processes relating to the employee life cycle from entry and exit as well as for mutations
  • Organization of the onboarding process including employee information
  • Ensuring service-oriented communication with our employees, managers and applicants and reliably processing all inquiries
  • Ensuring high data quality in our core systems (such as Workday, RTM and Abacus)
  • Supervision of time management RTM including creation of evaluations for HR controlling
  • Handling and processing absences as well as responsibility for illness and accident management in UKA Solution as well as other administrative HR tasks

Strengths

  • Basic commercial training with further training in HR and/or 3-5 years of professional experience in HR
  • Reliable and team-oriented personality with high quality standards
  • Native language German, R/D knowledge is an advantage
  • Workday, Abacus, RTM knowledge an advantage
  • Experience in supervising learners including a vocational training course is an advantage
  • Willingness to work on-site as well as to act as a substitute in the case of absences in the people team
