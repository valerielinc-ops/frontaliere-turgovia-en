Full description

PROFILE

  • Completed studies (FH/University) in electrical engineering, mechatronics, automation or a comparable qualification.
  • Several years of experience in the railway environment, ideally in the system, vehicle or complete vehicle technology.
  • Proof of responsibility in technical leadership or lead functions is advantageous.
  • Ability to interpret standards (e.g. EN, TSI) at system level and anchor them in the project environment.
  • Strong ability to make technical decisions at the system level and secure them in terms of costs, deadlines and quality.
  • Team-oriented personality with strong communication skills, assertiveness and experience in the professional leadership of engineering teams or interdisciplinary specialist groups.
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Good English skills in words and writing.

TASKS

  • Professional management of systems, electrical engineering and electrical installation planning in our modernization projects and close cooperation with the technical project manager
  • Responsibility for electric vehicle components and systems, in particular low-voltage on-board electrical systems, propulsion and traction systems
  • Definition of system requirements, interfaces and creation of integration concepts.
  • Technical cooperation with suppliers and external service providers and ensure quality and timely implementation
  • Implementation of the customer obligations specification and consideration of the relevant requirements
  • Responsible for creating, testing and releasing engineering documents such as circuit diagrams, cable lists, apparatus lists, interface documents and technical specifications.
  • Development of integrated system solutions in coordination with software, mechanical engineering, as well as approval and safety management
  • Analysis of existing systems and derivation of modernization solutions
  • Technical support for assembly, production, commissioning and test activities
  • From the concept phase to tests and approval, you accompany the technical implementation
