Full description

Are you flexible, customer-oriented and reliable? Then you are exactly right with us! For our Car Wash Center in Arbon we are looking for dedicated reinforcement. Become part of Migrol and apply with us. We look forward to meeting you.

Mansioni

  • Independent operation, service and cleaning of the car wash system
  • Pre-washing of vehicles
  • Attentive and friendly advice to our customers
  • Correct operation of the cash register on site

Requirements

  • High customer friendliness and sales flair
  • Flexibility in the organisation of working time
  • Good technical understanding of maintenance work
  • Physically robust
  • Basic PC knowledge
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: gifts on special occasions such as wedding, birth or retirement
  • Sports and fitness offers: Own sports club. Financial participation in Migros fitness subscriptions (up to CHF 250 per year)
