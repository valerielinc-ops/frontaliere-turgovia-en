You know Spitex from practice and would like to contribute to a reliable supply with a thoughtful operational planning? Then we look forward to meeting you. We are the Spitex Regio Tannzapfenland, a future-oriented non-profit organization with around 75 employees. In the municipalities of Bichelsee-Balterswil, Fischingen, Eschlikon, Münchwilen, Rickenbach, Sirnach and Wängi, we support people in making their everyday life at home as independent as possible. Operational planner / Operational planner with Spitex experience This is what awaits you

  • You plan and coordinate the assignments of our employees in Perigon and coordinate the needs of the clients with the available professional and human resources.
  • You are the telephone contact person for concerns about care and housekeeping.
  • You supervise the reception and support clients and relatives with information and advice.
  • You coordinate with GP practices and hospitals and prepare the first assignments administratively.
  • You ensure the flow of information between management, departmental management care and help at home, team leaders and employees as well as clients.
  • You take on further administrative tasks.
  • In alternation with your teammates, you take over the planning and the telephone picket in the home office at the weekend (every 5-6 weeks), from 06.30 until 11.00 o’clock at the latest.

You bring that with you

  • Experience in Spitex and in operational planning in the Spitex environment is absolutely necessary for this position.
  • You work independently, structured and quality-conscious and think beyond your own area of responsibility.
  • Even with short-term changes, you set priorities and keep track.
  • You communicate clearly, kindly and patiently and meet different concerns with understanding.
  • You are team-oriented, flexible and bring a warm, humorous way.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Further language skills are welcome.
  • You are confident in using digital work equipment and MS Office. Perigonal knowledge is beneficial.
  • You are ready to develop professionally through training and further education.
  • You have a category B driving licence.
