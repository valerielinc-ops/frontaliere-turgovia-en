Full description

Do you want to accompany people with mental illnesses in everyday life and pass on your expertise in the team? With us, you combine regular assignments with our clients with the professional management of our three-member psychiatry team. The function is located as a staff position and offers you an ideal entry into a professional management career – even if you are just starting your career as an APN or are still studying for a master’s degree. Nursing expert / Nursing expert APN Psychiatry | Pensum from 60% | Staff office This is what awaits you

  • You take on active and regular assignments with our clients and ensure individual, technically sound psychiatric care.
  • You lead the psychiatric team professionally and strengthen the employees through advice, coaching and practice support.
  • You carry out in-depth clinical assessments and accompany complex care and crisis situations.
  • You design case discussions and training courses and promote professional exchange.
  • You will further develop evidence-based care concepts, standards and processes and accompany their implementation.
  • You work closely with the lines, the handling specialists as well as the quality management and the care development.
  • Attractive employment conditions Mo-Fr

You bring that with you

  • A nursing degree recognised in Switzerland and a Master of Science in Nursing with APN orientation or an equivalent qualification. Even if you are still in the appropriate Master's degree, we look forward to your application.
  • At least two years of professional experience in psychiatric care.
  • Enjoy advice and knowledge transfer as well as the motivation to take on professional leadership responsibility and develop yourself in it.
  • Enjoy the direct work with clients and the further development of the team.
  • An independent, reflective working method and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation.
  • An appreciative attitude and an open ear to the concerns of the people you accompany.
