Full description

For the team of Campus Apotheke we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

Pharmaceutical assistant, PTA or specialist Pharmacy EFZ

100%

Customer-oriented, committed and responsible. We care for our customers both pharmaceutically and humanly, as well as a good team cohesion. Is this important to you too?

In times of demographic change at the hospital location Münsterlingen, would you like to make your contribution to the supply of our customers with urgently needed medicines? The main focus is clearly on the delivery of prescription medicines.

Can you identify with this activity? Then apply today!

Whether you want to gain new experience from apprenticeship or already have relevant professional experience - part-time or full-time - our motivated team is looking forward to you!

They shall

  • the pharmaceutically competent and courteous advice of our customers
  • Participation in assortment maintenance
  • Supporting our learners
  • further areas of responsibility, also with regard to hospital pharmacy, may arise depending on individual suitability and interest

They bring with them

  • Completed training as pharmaceutical assistant or specialist pharmacy EFZ or pharmaceutical technical assistant
  • Education or work experience in Switzerland
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact
  • Enthusiasm for advice on Rx drugs, because this is our strength
  • A high degree of responsibility for your actions
  • an independent, careful, team-capable and structured way of working
  • Ideally, knowledge in dealing with the pharmacy software ProPharma X

Spitalcampus Apotheken AG introduces itself to you

The Campus Pharmacy of the Spitalcampus Apotheken Thurgau AG is a public pharmacy on the grounds of the Canton Hospital Münsterlingen. It bridges the medical care of patients immediately after hospital treatment until resumption by other healthcare institutions. We are distinguished by competent pharmaceutical advice and a very wide range of medicines close to hospitals. Customers who are not in treatment at Spital Thurgau AG also use our prescriptions or receive advice on health issues.
