Imagine coming to a place where your work is not only valued, but seen as an essential part of a big whole. A place where you not only treat people, but accompany them on their way to a better quality of life – with time, empathy and the latest knowledge of medicine and therapy. If you understand physiotherapy not only as a job, but as a real vocation, then you are exactly right with us. At Physio Knobelspiess GmbH (Physio K), we are rethinking physiotherapy. At our locations in Romanshorn, St. Gallen and Balgach, we stand for holistic, patient-centered care at a high professional level. We are convinced that sustainable therapeutic successes are achieved where active rehabilitation, interdisciplinary cooperation and trusting, individually oriented support work together. We focus not only on diagnoses, but on people with their own goals, resources and realities. That is why we design each therapy competently, attentively and personally – with the aim of enabling progress that remains noticeable in everyday life in the long term.
