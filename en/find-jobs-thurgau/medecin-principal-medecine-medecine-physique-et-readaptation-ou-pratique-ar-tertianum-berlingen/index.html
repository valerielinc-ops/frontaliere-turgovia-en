Full description

For our clinic for geriatric rehabilitation Tertianum Neutal in Berlingen with 38 beds, we are looking for an immediately or by appointment:

Senior physician Medicine, Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation or Practical physician 80–100 %

Our clinic for geriatric rehabilitation is on the hospital list of the Canton of Thurgau with a service mandate for geriatric rehabilitation.

We offer an interesting medical challenge, a workplace with direct lake views, flat hierarchies and flexible working hours for a healthy work-life balance.

At diagnostic possibilities we offer the most important laboratory tests as POC test, ECG, LZ-EKG, digital X-ray, ultrasound incl. Duplex and others.

Your tasks/profile

  • Specialist title for General Internal Medicine, Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation or Practical Doctor / Practical Doctor
  • Interest in geriatrics, especially in geriatric rehabilitation
  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in a team with empathy and commitment
  • Understanding and empathy in dealing with elderly and partly multimorbid people
  • Ability to respond to individual patient needs in diagnostics and therapy
  • Participation in on-call services
  • Foreign diplomas with recognition in Switzerland (MEBEKO)
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
