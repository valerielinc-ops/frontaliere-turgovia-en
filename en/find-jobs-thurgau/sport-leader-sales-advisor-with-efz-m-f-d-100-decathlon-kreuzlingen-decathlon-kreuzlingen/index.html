Your department is at the heart of our local strategy in Kreuzlingen. Here you will meet a highly motivated team of passionate athletes who equip locals and tourists with the perfect equipment every day. Become part of a Swiss success story Decathlon Switzerland is growing rapidly: Since the start of 2017 in Neuchâtel, we have expanded our network to around 53 sales outlets. And we continue to expand – especially in German-speaking Switzerland. For you, this means a crisis-proof workplace with real, nationwide career opportunities.
