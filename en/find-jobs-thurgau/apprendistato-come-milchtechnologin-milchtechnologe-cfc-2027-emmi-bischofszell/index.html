Full description

You want to produce high-quality dairy products using the latest technologies? In this exciting profession, you are directly involved in production – you ensure smooth processes, the highest quality and support your team with your commitment. At Emmi, we process over 1.8 million liters of milk every day! With your commitment you make an important contribution to the supply of Switzerland with cheese, yogurt and Co. And this in a state-of-the-art environment that lives innovation and team spirit.

You can do that.

  • You learn how our dairy products are made – from acceptance to packaging – and work closely with your team and take on more responsibility step by step.
  • You control, monitor and clean our highly automated production lines.
  • You carry out quality and hygiene checks and record your results accurately.
  • You take samples, analyze them in the laboratory 🧪 and make quick decisions.
  • You change department regularly and get to know fresh produce or cheese production.

You bring that with you

  • Secondary or secondary school degree with good grades in science and math.
  • Passion for dairy products 🔥 and a strong sense of smell and taste.
  • Technical understanding and enjoyment of working with computer-controlled systems.
  • Team spirit, joy of communication, sense of responsibility and great willingness to learn.

This is what we offer you

  • Extra wage & voluntary participation in success: Look forward to a 13. Monthly wages and participation in the company’s success.
  • 6 weeks holidays: More time for relaxation, travel and your hobbies.
  • Top future prospects: 80% of our learners receive a fixed employment contract at Emmi after their training.
  • Costs for more focus on your teaching: We cover the costs for schoolbooks, SBB Half-Tax subscription, workwear and support you with CHF 400 for your school laptop.
  • Fun & team spirit: Every year you look forward to an unforgettable trip to the Europapark. In addition, we have a common introduction day and a common camp for learners at the end of the first year of apprenticeship.
  • Free Emmi products: Enjoy our delicious Emmi products during work – and also after work. Thanks to up to 20% discount in our stores, you never have to give up your favorites.
