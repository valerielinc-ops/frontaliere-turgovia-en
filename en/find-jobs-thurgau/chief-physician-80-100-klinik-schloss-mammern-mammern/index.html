Full description

The Klinik Schloss Mammern, embedded in an impressive landscape, is a leading private clinic for every phase of rehabilitation and has been run since 1889 as a family business in the meantime 4th generation. With around 440 dedicated employees, we offer first-class medical care and therapy in four disciplines: cardiological, internistic-oncological, musculoskeletal and pneumological. Our patients enjoy an upscale hotel industry at five-star level in a stylish atmosphere and with the latest infrastructure. The personal and heartfelt treatment of patients is important to us and we attach great importance to a long-term and mutually enriching cooperation. To complement our team, we are looking for a senior doctor by agreement 80%-100 %

Responsibility

  • Independent medical care of patients
  • Participation in in-house on-call duty
  • Specialist diagnostics in the focus area (e.g. ECG / echocardiography, lung function, sonography, etc.)
  • Ensuring high medical quality and further development of the cooperation network

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a Swiss recognised specialist title Internal Medicine, ideally an internist with additional qualification (e.g. cardiology, pneumology, oncology).
  • interest in rehabilitation medicine
  • Experience in rehabilitation is desirable.
  • They enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • High social skills and empathy in dealing with patients distinguish you.
  • MEBEKO recognition is mandatory.

This is what we have to offer you, among other things

  • A versatile and varied work in a small, highly motivated medical team in an exciting environment of rehabilitation.
  • Modern hospital infrastructure.
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 10 days of compensation for public holidays, 10 days of further training.
  • Increase your holiday quota by purchasing an additional five days.
  • Comprehensive introductory program in the interdisciplinary team.
  • Take advantage of our sports offer with training equipment and indoor pool, which is available free of charge at marginal times.
  • Trust our professional childcare (12 weeks to 12 years) in the company’s childcare centre at discounted rates.
  • Take advantage of our free parking spaces and reach us comfortably by public transport.
  • Enjoy first-class food and comfortable living options.
  • Join our team events, Christmas parties and summer parties.
  • Relax on our private beach on the beautiful Untersee.
