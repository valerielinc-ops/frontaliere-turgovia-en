Our family business offers you a varied and exciting apprenticeship as a merchant EFZ. If you are interested in this profession, then you are exactly right with us! We are looking for motivated, innovative and enthusiastic talent. Start your future with us! Your tasks

  • Work in the accounting, marketing, sales, IT and HR/reception departments
  • Processing of accounts payable and accounts payable
  • Assistance in the dunning run and various bookings as well as in the monthly, quarterly and annual accounts
  • Ordering and management of office and consumables
  • Participation in personnel administration (entry and exit, personnel correspondence)
  • Communicate with employees, customers and suppliers

You bring that with you

  • Completed compulsory schooling with good grades in German, Mathematics, French and English
  • Team-ready, sociable and customer-oriented
  • Quick understanding, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Flair for numbers and IT systems

Our offer

  • A challenging field of work in a successful Swiss family business
  • Many years of experience in training learners in an established team
  • The opportunity to shape the success of the next few years by bringing in valuable ideas
  • We make a subsidy to the public transport billet and you have the opportunity to achieve a performance wage with good performance in the school and in the company
  • Six weeks of holidays
  • Cost of school supplies
