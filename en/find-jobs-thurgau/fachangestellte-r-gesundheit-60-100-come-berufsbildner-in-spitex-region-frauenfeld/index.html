Full description

The Spitex Region Frauenfeld is a forward-looking, continuously growing nonprofit organization with around 120 employees and respectful DU culture. With a public service mandate, we provide outpatient care and care for people of all ages. You are a professional, have drive and desire to accompany and shape young nurses ? Then you are exactly right with us! Immediately or by agreement. Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH 60 - 100% as a vocational trainer:in As dipl. Nurse HF/FH with an additional function as a vocational trainer: you get our learners on track – you motivate, demand, give honest feedback and celebrate their progress. You are actively involved in shaping learning processes and yes – you stay in the middle of the action: care, advice, support for customers: within and relatives are just as much a part of it. Because real professionals can do both.

What to expect

  • You bring our students forward – professionally and personally.
  • You accompany them until the end, give honest feedback, challenge and promote targeted. You actively shape learning processes instead of just managing them.
  • You have your back: Our education managers and a strong team are at your side.
  • You work in a team, take on assignments in basic and treatment care, advise customers and relatives and develop individual care plans.

What do we want from you?

  • You are dipl. Care professional HF/FH with a professional working attitude
  • For the vocational trainer task: min. 2 years of professional experience + BB course or motivation to complete this
  • Professionally top, resilient, flexible & independent
  • Driver ID B available (service car waiting for you)

You can look forward to this

  • A modern, growing and innovative organization where quality of care and education really matter
  • Work-life balance: annual working time model + min. 5 weeks holidays, no night services
  • Teams that stick together: mindful du culture, respect and appreciation included
  • In addition, there are attractive employment conditions and extensive training and development opportunities

Elisabeth Kienast, Education Responsible (e.kienast@spitex-frauenfeld.ch) is at your disposal for questions. Please send your application to bewerbungen@spitex-frauenfeld.ch.
