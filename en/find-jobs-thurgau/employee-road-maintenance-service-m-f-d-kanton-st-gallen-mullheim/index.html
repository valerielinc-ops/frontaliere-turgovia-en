Full description

The National Roads Division VI of the Civil Engineering Office of the Canton of St.Gallen makes a significant contribution to the safety of the national roads in the cantons of Glarus, Thurgau and St.Gallen. For this purpose, the territorial unit has four workshops in Thal, Mels, Biäsche and Oberbüren as well as two bases in St.Gallen and Müllheim.

What to expect

  • Signalling work for construction sites
  • Greenery and forestry work
  • Cleaning work
  • Road and civil engineering work
  • Operation of vehicles and equipment
  • Winter service and accidents

What sets it apart

  • Completed vocational training in construction, forestry or as a gardener
  • Team-capable, independent and reliable personality
  • Readiness for piquetting and night work
  • Enjoyment in all work of road maintenance and winter service
  • Cat C/CE driving licence or willingness to acquire it
  • Accessibility of the base within 20 minutes

What we offer

  • Attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities
  • Exciting and interesting activities in a motivated team
  • Careful training

Contact: Simon Stäbler, Head of Base at Müllheim, telephone 058 229 85 06
