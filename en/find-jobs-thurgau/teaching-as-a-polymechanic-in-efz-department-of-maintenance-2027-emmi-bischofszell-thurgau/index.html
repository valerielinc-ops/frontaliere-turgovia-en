Full description

Together we create the best Emmi moments – maybe soon with you? Immerse yourself in the exciting world of maintenance technology! This teaching gives you the unique opportunity to actively participate in the maintenance of our production facilities and ensure that they function smoothly. At Emmi, you work in a modern environment, learn from experienced professionals and develop into an important support in technical production. Sounds good? Then start with us! We make more of milk. You make more of us. We are Emmi.

You can do that.

  • You ensure that our technical facilities run smoothly at all times – whether in ongoing operation or during troubleshooting. ️
  • When commissioning new production plants, you actively participate and help to set them optimally.
  • During training, you will manufacture workpieces, assemble machine parts and learn how to make prototypes.
  • Maintenance work, maintenance and inspections are part of your everyday life – so that our facilities are always up-to-date.
  • You put technical knowledge directly into practice: You learn how mechanical and electronic components work together. 
  • With your commitment, you help ensure that production runs smoothly – reliably, safely and efficiently. 

You bring that with you

  • You bring enthusiasm for technology and mechanics – so you are ready for the exciting challenges of this profession. 
  • Your technical understanding allows you to take care of machines precisely – you work quickly, but always carefully.
  • A secondary school degree with good grades in mathematics and physics forms the ideal basis for your education.
  • Spatial thinking is easy for you, which allows you to quickly grasp complex technical connections. 里
  • Accuracy and a good eye for detail make you stand out – Quality comes first for you.
  • Whether in a team or alone: Rely on you. With your own initiative and teamwork, you are a valued part of our company.

This is what we offer you

  • Extra wage & voluntary participation in success: Look forward to a 13. Monthly wages and participation in the company’s success.
  • 6 weeks holidays: More time for relaxation, travel and your hobbies.
  • Top future prospects: 80% of our learners receive a fixed employment contract at Emmi after their training.
  • Costs for more focus on your teaching: We cover the costs for schoolbooks, SBB Half-Tax subscription, workwear and support you with CHF 400 for your school laptop.
  • Fun & team spirit: Every year you look forward to an unforgettable trip to the Europapark. In addition, we have a common introduction day and a common camp for learners at the end of the first year of apprenticeship.
  • Free Emmi products: Enjoy our delicious Emmi products during work – and also after work. Thanks to up to 20% discount in our stores, you never have to give up your favorites.
