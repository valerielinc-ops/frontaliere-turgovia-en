Role overview

The Spitex Region Frauenfeld is a forward-looking, continuously growing nonprofit organization with around 120 employees and respectful DU culture. With a public service mandate, we provide outpatient care and care for people of all ages. To strengthen our cadre, we are looking for you immediately or by agreement: a committed and competent leader. Team management 80 - 100% (dipl. nursing specialist HF/FH) For our Spitex organization, we are looking for a responsible and committed team leader who, with expertise, empathy and organizational talent, accompanies and develops our employees in everyday care. What to expect

  • Professional, human and organizational management of an interprofessional care team with approximately 15 employees
  • Operational planning and ensuring needs-based and high-quality care
  • Support and coaching of employees in everyday life
  • Close cooperation with the Head of Core Service and external partners (doctors, relatives, etc.)
  • Co-design and implementation of projects for the further development of Spitex care
  • You are close to the base and ready to take on assignments in care

What you bring

  • several years of professional and managerial experience (advantage in the Spitex and/or in the acute field)
  • completed leadership training or willingness to complete
  • Good IT user knowledge as well as knowledge of Perigon and interRAI HC

  • No applications from recruiters shall be accepted for this post.

Contacts
