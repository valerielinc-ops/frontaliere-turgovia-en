Full description

For the production of our fine finished menus, we are looking for a chef who wants to further develop and use the passion for food in the food industry.

Mansioni

  • Cooking of sauces and side dishes according to the recipe and production instructions
  • Preparation of various mixtures
  • Mise and Place
  • Implementation of controls and documentation

Requirements

  • 1-2 years: in the food industry (advantageous)
  • Completed initial or continuing education: cook or other professions in the food sector
  • Readiness for 2 to 3 shift work
  • High quality and hygiene awareness
  • Good physical condition
  • German (flowing)
