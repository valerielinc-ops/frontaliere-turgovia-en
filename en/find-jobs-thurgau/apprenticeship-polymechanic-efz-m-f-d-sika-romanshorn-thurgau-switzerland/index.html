About Sika Sika is a global company with over 33,000 employees. Through constant innovation and high quality, Sika is a leading producer of specialty chemicals for construction and industry in the fields of densities, gluing, steaming, reinforcement and protection. Sika Automotive AG uses these core competencies as an important partner and supplier in the development and production of products for the vehicle industry. Their technologies increase the safety and comfort of vehicles and help reduce the weight of future vehicles. The goal is for vehicles to be more stable and calmer on the road and for production to become more efficient and flexible.
