Full description

The nephrology of Spital Thurgau AG (FMH training category B) is organised across all locations. At the locations Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen there are dialysis stations with 16 (main location Frauenfeld) and 10 treatment stations (next location Münsterlingen). From February 2027, another dialysis station with 10 treatment stations will also be operated at the Arbon location. Across all locations, practically the entire spectrum of specialized kidney specialist medical examinations, consultations and treatments is offered.

As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement we are looking for a location in Frauenfeld

Senior physician (depending on the qualification mbF) Nephrology

50-100%

They shall take over

  • Own consultation hours in our versatile nephrological ambulatorium and support of the dialysis station
  • Assistance and consultancy for all inpatients of the house with nephrological problems
  • Collaboration in the cross-location team of nephrology at Spital Thurgau AG
  • Commitment to Teaching in the Nephrology and Medical Clinic Team
  • Participation in nephrological and, if appropriate, internist emergency services

They bring with them

  • Swiss or Swiss recognised specialist title as well as several years of clinical experience in nephrology, specialist title General Internal Medicine desirable
  • Sensitive, socially competent and team capable personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Joy of teaching assistant doctors of nephrology and general internal medicine
  • Communicative, integrative, consensus- and solution-oriented personality

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
