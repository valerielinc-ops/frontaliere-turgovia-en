Full description

As part of succession planning, we are looking for an experienced and entrepreneurial leader. In this key role, you will assume overall responsibility for customer service and play a significant role in shaping its strategic and operational development.

Your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for the strategic and operational management of Customer Service B2C with over 60 employees
  • Ensuring stable, efficient day-to-day business across all service channels and continuous optimization of the customer journey
  • Building and further developing a scalable, automated and data-driven omnichannel service organization
  • Control of relevant KPIs (e.g. customer satisfaction, productivity, service level, complaints) including implementation of improvement measures
  • Further development of service and support concepts to actively increase customer acquisition and retention
  • Close collaboration with COO, CCO and internal interfaces and active participation in cross-departmental transformation projects

Your strengths

  • Several years of management experience in large customer service or operations organizations, ideally in a regulated environment
  • Proven experience in transforming and developing service organizations
  • High digital affinity and understanding of automation, CRM, self-service and AI-supported processes
  • Strong customer orientation combined with entrepreneurial thinking and cost awareness
  • Strong communicator, empathetic leader with change management skills
  • Very good knowledge of German and very good knowledge of French would be an advantage
