Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services for older adults and employs around 4'000 people. Outpatient and inpatient services seamlessly complement one another. While Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is Switzerland's leading provider of private home care services.

For Senevita Giesserei in Arbon, we are looking for a trainee for the upcoming fall semester:

Trainee / apprentice as a qualified nursing professional HF in long-term care, as well as in the in-house home care service.

Your tasks

  • Development by year of training:
  • 1st year: Lots of guidance, focus on basic care & observation.
  • 2nd year: More independence, first more complex tasks.
  • 3rd year: Almost independent care, taking responsibility for groups of patients.

What you bring

  • Qualified educational background: You hold a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ), a vocational, specialized or academic baccalaureate, a specialized upper-secondary school certificate or an equivalent qualification.
  • Passion for care and teamwork: You enjoy working with older people and value constructive exchange and collaboration within the team.
  • Responsible conduct: You demonstrate a high level of responsibility and reliability when carrying out your tasks.
  • Strong communication skills: You impress with excellent communication skills and a cooperative, team-oriented attitude.
  • A keen eye: Thanks to your keen powers of observation, you quickly and accurately identify changes and the needs of residents.
  • Resilience: You are resilient both mentally and physically and keep a cool head even in demanding situations.
  • Awareness of hygiene: A clean, careful way of working and a strong sense of hygiene standards are a matter of course for you.
  • Flexibility: You are willing to work flexible hours and adapt to operational requirements.
  • Organizational skills: With your organizational talent, you make the working day efficient and structured.
  • Language skills: You have good spoken and written German skills to ensure smooth communication and documentation.

What we offer you

  • Above-average training compensation: We offer you an attractive salary that provides financial security and ensures you can cover your living expenses without worry.
  • Career & development: Take advantage of a broad range of training and continuing education opportunities through the internal Senevita Academy.
  • Culinary catering: Enjoy high-quality meals at reduced rates in our in-house restaurant.
  • Exclusive employee offers: Take advantage of a wide range of discounts and special offers, for example on the Half-Fare Travelcard, for fuel purchases, for supplementary insurance or at pharmacies.
  • Work-life balance & security: Rely on attractive social benefits and a generous vacation policy with at least 5 weeks of vacation. To provide relief, this entitlement increases from the 50. Year of life to 6 weeks and from the 60. Year of life to 7 weeks.
