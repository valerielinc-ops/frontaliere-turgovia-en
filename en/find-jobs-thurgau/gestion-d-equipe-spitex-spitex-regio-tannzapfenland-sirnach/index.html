Full description

Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organisation Spitex Regio Tannzapfenland. Our team of around 85 employees brings a lot of commitment in the communities of Bichelsee-Balterswil, Fischingen, Eschlikon, Münchwilen, Rickenbach, Sirnach and Wängi joie de vivre into the home of our clients. A meaningful task that is fun. We search immediately or by agreement Team management Spitex Their tasks:

  • Professional, human and organizational leadership of an interdisciplinary nursing team
  • Ensuring professional, client-oriented and legally compliant care and support
  • Responsibility for compliance with and further development of the quality of care and nursing standards
  • Organization and management of day-to-day operations including operational, service and holiday planning
  • Support and support of employees in technical and organizational issues
  • Conducting employee and development discussions and promoting professional development
  • Close cooperation with needs assessment, doctors, hospitals and other network partners
  • Participation in projects, quality development and the continuous optimization of processes Your profile:
  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in outpatient care (Spitex)
  • Leadership training or willingness to complete it
  • Strong leadership, organisational and communication skills
  • High social competence as well as an appreciative and solution-oriented leadership attitude
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Good IT user knowledge and driving licence category B We offer:
  • A responsible leadership function with plenty of room for manoeuvre
  • The opportunity to actively shape the further development of a modern Spitex organization
  • A motivated and committed team as well as an open and appreciative corporate culture
  • Contemporary employment conditions and attractive social benefits
  • Support for management and specialist training
  • Modern digital work equipment and efficient work processes Interested? We look forward to your complete electronic application at bewerbungen@srtzl.ch For any questions, the department management, Kerstin Jung, is available at the telephone number 071 978 73 11.
