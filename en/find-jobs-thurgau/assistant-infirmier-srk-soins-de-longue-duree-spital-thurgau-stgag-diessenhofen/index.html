Clinic St. Katharinental introduces itself

Klinik St. Katharinental in Diessenhofen is a company of Spital Thurgau AG with a total of 132 beds. In the historical surroundings of the former monastery we offer the areas of musculoskeletal, internistic, oncological and geriatric rehabilitation, acute rheumatology and long-term care. Every year we look after around 1,700 inpatient rehabilitation patients and provide an extensive outpatient service. In addition to the unique location directly on the Rhine, there is an appreciative culture, multi-professional cooperation, extensive training opportunities and attractive wage and social benefits. As an employee of Klinik St. Katharinental, we benefit from the benefits of Spital Thurgau AG.
