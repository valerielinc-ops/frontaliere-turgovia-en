Full description

Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organisation Spitex Regio Tannzapfenland. Our team of around 75 employees brings the joy of life into the homes of our clients in the communities of Bichelsee-Balterswil, Fischingen, Eschlikon, Münchwilen, Rickenbach, Sirnach and Wängi with great commitment. A meaningful task that is fun. Training place as a qualified nurse / qualified nurse HF You want your education as a Dipl. Do nurses graduate in a future-oriented company? At Spitex Regio Tannzapfenland, we accompany you individually and with commitment through your studies – practically, personally and with heart. From day one, you are in direct contact with our clients. The many encounters with people of all ages and different backgrounds make your education exciting and varied. Depending on the previous training, the training lasts two to four years and concludes with the diploma as a nursing specialist HF. This is what awaits you with us

  • A varied working day in the practical part of your studies
  • Competent vocational trainers who support you personally and individually in your learning process
  • Weekly learning times to combine theory and practice as well as learning afternoons with fellow students
  • A motivated, collaborative team with an appreciative corporate culture
  • A modern working environment with digital processes and smartphone use
  • Free choice of training location (BfGS Weinfelden, ZAG)
  • Development opportunities during and after training – including permanent employment
  • We pay an attractive wage and cover the semester costs
  • For applicants aged 22 and over who reside in the Canton of Thurgau: Possibility of nursing education 22+ also with an attractive adjusted training wage
  • Extra: We take over CHF 1000.- of your laptop equipment so that you are optimally equipped for school and practice!
  • Extra: You get a General Subscription 2nd Class

Your profile

  • Completed 3-year basic education with EFZ, graduate degree or Matura
  • For the shortened 2-year training: FaGe EFZ
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Advantageous driving licence Cat B

You bring that with you

  • High sense of responsibility and independence
  • The joy of self-responsible learning
  • Reflection capability and communication strength
  • Empathy and openness towards people of different cultures
  • Physical and psychological resilience
