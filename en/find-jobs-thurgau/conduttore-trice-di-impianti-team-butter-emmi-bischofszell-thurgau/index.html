Full description

Whether fresh butter, classic butter products or steeped butter - at our Biedermann dairy in Bischofszell, modern production facilities and dedicated employees ensure high-quality products. As a plant manager, you will become part of a team that works together and ensures the best quality every day. You are interested in technology, like working in a team and want to learn new things? Then we look forward to meeting you!

You can do that.

  • Together with your team, you ensure a smooth production process and service and monitor our various production and filling plants.
  • From the production of fresh butter to classic butter products to steeped butter - the various production processes ensure a varied day-to-day work.
  • Quality comes first with us. That is why you pay attention to compliance with our hygiene and quality standards and help to keep production losses as low as possible.
  • The correct recording and documentation of our production orders in the MES is also part of your area of responsibility.
  • With your commitment and your ideas, you help to improve our processes on an ongoing basis and get to work where your support is needed.

Why do you succeed?

  • A completed basic training EFZ in the food sector, for example as plant operator:in or dairy technologist/milk chinologist is an advantage.
  • Technical understanding, a careful way of working and a willingness to learn new things set you apart.
  • As a reliable and honest team player, you think along, tackle yourself independently and maintain your endurance and accuracy even during recurring work.
  • Good knowledge of German will help you to communicate as a team and safely implement our production requirements.
  • Flexibility is especially in demand during our peak season, although there may be bets on weekends and public holidays.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You will receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
