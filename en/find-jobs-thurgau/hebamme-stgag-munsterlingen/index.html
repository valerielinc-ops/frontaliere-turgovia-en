To complement the team on the family department, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Midwife

80-100%

Co-create, accompany together and take care of the entire care sheet of pregnancy, birth and postpartum – are you looking for a working environment that is more than just a place of daily work?

Together – individually – respectfully.

In our future family department we accompany families individually and with their needs in the center. The combination of evidence and experience in the interprofessional team forms the foundation of our obstetrics.

Midwife-guided births, spontaneous pelvic end position and twin births as well as the conscious use of alternative healing methods are of course part of your midwife everyday life.

You take over

  • Care and accompaniment of pregnant women, childbearers and women who have recently given birth
  • Conduct of therapies, including complementary medical methods
  • Comprehensive advice for families during pregnancy, childbirth and postpartum
  • If interested take courses in the family department possible
  • Professional support and promotion of students

You bring with you

  • Training as a midwife FH with several years of professional experience in the birth department
  • Process- and solution-oriented action, responsibility
  • Appreciative communication and good self-reflection
  • Strong team skills as well as a high level of social and professional competence
  • Resistance and flexibility for use in shift operation
  • Safe handling of technical devices as well as good IT knowledge

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
