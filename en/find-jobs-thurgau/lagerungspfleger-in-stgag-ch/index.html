Full description

For our operations department we are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Storage keeper/in

100%

In this function, you support the graduated surgical and anesthesia staff as well as the surgeons in the patient positioning and storage for the individual operations. You take on varied, exciting activities in an open-minded and innovative surgical team.

They shall

  • Provision, testing, handling and maintenance of all required operating tables
  • Professional storage of patients according to current house standards to avoid pressure points, nerve injuries and burns in all specialist areas
  • Intraoperative support of the surgical team
  • Operation of technical equipment related to surgical procedures
  • Installation of plaster casts and extensions in cooperation with operators
  • Entering patient and surgical data into the computer system
  • Proper supply of consumer goods within their own department

This is what you bring with you

We are looking for people who ideally already have experience in supporting patients in the acute care hospital or are ready to learn it. They communicate openly, are proactive, resilient and enjoy working in a team. Ideally, they have basic medical training (FAGE or MPA and/or have already completed a "specialised course for surgical camps" or "perioperative assistance"). You are a flexible personality and like to work in a dynamic team. Furthermore, you have good knowledge of German in word and writing.
