Full description

Care with heart and mind – We are looking for you!

You are a nurse FH/HF/DNII and want to work in a warm environment where humanity and team spirit are at the center? Then you are perfect for us!

We are a motivated team in long-term care and are looking for reinforcement by committed professionals who take care of the well-being of our guests with joy and expertise.

For the nursing home Haus Seeheim in Berlingen, we are looking for a

Dipl. Nurse FH/HF/DNII for Long-Term Care 30-100%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and nursing of guests according to our nursing philosophy and the nursing process
  • Performing therapeutic nursing activities
  • Planning and accompanying medical visits
  • Electronic nursing documentation using CareCoach
  • Taking on tasks as a contact person
  • Controlling the nursing process
  • Leading case discussions
  • Taking on daily responsibility
  • Taking on tasks as a theme carrier, technical supervisory authority

Your profile

  • Completed training as Dipl. Nurse FH/HF/DNII or an equivalent training recognized by the SRK
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Responsibility and teamwork
  • Humor and the ability to keep an overview even in a hectic everyday life
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written

Why Tertianum?

We care a lot about our employees being able to balance their professional development and their personal life. This includes a diverse range of benefits, for example:

  • Flexible working models (job sharing, part-time models, flexible shift planning, re-entry after a break)
  • Internal and external training opportunities, particularly in the field of CAS in healthcare
  • Fitness discounts
  • SBB half-fare ticket
  • Insurance discounts
  • Fleet discounts
  • Discounts at Sunrise

Further benefits can be found on our website.

Are you interested?

Then we offer you an exciting perspective: With us, you apply your individual skills and your professionalism in a motivated and flexible team. In addition, you take on a high level of responsibility and actively shape the future of Tertianum. Apply now and become part of our team.

We look forward to receiving your complete online application via our application tool.
