Introduction

You have already gained experience in administration or in the area of further education / HR and enjoy organizational tasks? Are you looking for a new challenge in which you can actively support the Faculty of Talent Development & Learning with your organizational talent? Become part of our team! With us, you can expect a varied everyday work in the dynamic environment of the retail trade. We are looking for an independent, customer-oriented personality with pleasure in contact with people. Your tasks

  • Organizational and administrative hub in Talent Development & Learning.
  • Holistic planning, coordination and booking of training courses, seminars and further education events (e.g. invitation and participant management, spatial and resource planning, preparation of seminar documents).
  • Central contact person for internal and external requests
  • Recording and administration of training, development initiatives and follow-up of compulsory training in the Learning Management System (SuccessFactors).
  • Administrative involvement in the implementation of onboarding processes as well as in learning and development programmes.
  • Close cooperation and coordination with internal departments (e.g. talent acquisition), external training providers and speakers.

Your profile

  • Completed basic commercial training.
  • First professional experience in an administrative function, preferably in the area of HR, education and training or event organization.
  • Good user knowledge in Google Workspace (as well as preferably first experience with SuccessFactors / LMS systems is advantageous).
  • Independent, exact and solution-oriented working method.
  • Strong organisational talent, high service orientation, flexibility and resilience.
  • Good knowledge of French and English.
