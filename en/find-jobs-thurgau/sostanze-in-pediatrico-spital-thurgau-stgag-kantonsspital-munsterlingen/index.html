Full description

The Clinic of Urology offers at the Münsterlingen location by agreement the position of a

Sub-assistant in urology

100%

We offer an internship of at least 1 month duration.

They take over

  • Medical history survey/entry examinations and basic ultrasound in scheduled patients and in the emergency department
  • Assistance in the operating room (theoretical and practical training), if suitable independently carrying out minor surgical procedures
  • Care and assessment of patients in the bed ward, day clinic, emergency ward and ambulatorium (depending on suitability) up to assistant medical responsibility
  • Participation in departmental work such as visit, patient care, report and management of the electronic KG
  • No night, on-call or other services on holidays or weekends

This is what they bring with them.

  • Ongoing study of human medicine (in the elective year or at least in the faculty)
  • Ability to integrate into operational processes

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself to you

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4’100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
