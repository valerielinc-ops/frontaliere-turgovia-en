Full description

We are the SPITEX Thurgau Nordwest and, with around 50 dedicated employees, provide professional and individual outpatient care and care in five communities. Our organisation is evolving: structures are changing and cooperation across team and organisational boundaries is becoming increasingly important. For our base in Diessenhofen, we are looking for an experienced leader who takes responsibility, empowers people and actively shapes our future. Team management Care (80%) Admission by arrangement Your field of activity You lead a team of around 15 employees in terms of expertise, personnel and organisation. You create orientation, promote a trusting cooperation and develop your area of responsibility forward-looking.

  • Lead and develop people: You recognize potential, promote your employees and strengthen team cohesion through appreciation, clarity and an open feedback culture.
  • Take responsibility: You make decisions, set priorities and manage resources in a forward-looking and solution-oriented manner.
  • Strengthen cooperation: You communicate transparently, also address difficult topics and promote a constructive conflict and team culture.
  • Shape change: You accompany your team through development processes, bring your own ideas and think beyond team and organizational boundaries.
  • Ensure quality of care: Together with your team, you ensure a high quality of care and service and stay close to the core business through your own care assignments and case management.

This sets you apart

  • Diploma as a nurse HF/FH and sound professional experience, ideally in Spitex
  • Several years of leadership experience, preferably supplemented by further training in the field of leadership
  • An empathetic, reflective and decisive personality who can inspire, promote and also challenge employees
  • Strength of communication, capacity for conflict and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, organizational skill and the ability to keep track even in demanding situations
  • Experience with change processes and enjoyment of networked and cross-divisional cooperation

This is what we offer you

  • Responsibility with creative leeway: A varied activity in which you can act independently and actively shape our further development.
  • A good start: A structured familiarization so that you can start your new leadership task safely and successfully.
  • Appreciative collaboration: An open corporate culture with respect, trust and exchange at eye level.
  • Attractive employment conditions and living work-home balance: We attach great importance to ensuring that work and private life are well compatible. We offer at least five weeks of holidays and progressive social benefits (PK 55/45).
  • Modern working conditions: contemporary tools and an environment open to new ideas and developments.
  • Development with perspective: We support your professional and personal further education.

Ready to shape the future with us? You not only want to organize, but also empower people, take responsibility and make a difference together? Then we look forward to meeting you!
