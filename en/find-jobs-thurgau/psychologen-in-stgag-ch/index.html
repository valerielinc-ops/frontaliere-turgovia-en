For our expertise and youth forensics in Münsterlingen we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by arrangement one or more

Psychologists/in

50-70%

The expert centre for expert opinions and youth forensics in Münsterlingen produces child and adolescent psychiatric opinions for authorities (KESB, courts, JugA) or the IV. We are a multidisciplinary team of 4 - 5 employees in the field of psychology (legal psychology) and child and adolescent psychiatry or adult psychiatry.

They take over

  • Largely independent expert work in primarily family and civil law issues, depending on the level of experience or after careful familiarization
  • Supplementary assessments in the field of criminal law as well as IV assessments

They bring with them

  • You have training as a clinical psychologist or are in further training in legal psychology or child and adolescent psychotherapy
  • You have a clear interest in the expert work and ideally already gained initial concrete experience in this area
  • They like to work independently and at the same time networked in an interdisciplinary team. The joint exchange as well as the discussion and evaluation of assessments are a valuable addition to your work.
  • They have a hands-on approach to dynamic, challenging tasks and pragmatic creativity and flexibility.

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves

The Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) Thurgau offers excellent conditions for classical child and adolescent psychiatric training as well as for comprehensive psychotherapeutic training. Every year, we treat more than 3,000 patients with high quality standards in family-friendly, outpatient, outreach and day-clinical settings, if possible with evidence-based therapy (multi-systemic therapy MST, Parent Child Interaction Therapy PCIT, ESDM for early intervention in autism). An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the KJPD a highly attractive employer as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau.
