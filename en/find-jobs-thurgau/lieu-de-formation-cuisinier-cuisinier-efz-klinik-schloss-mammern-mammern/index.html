Full description

The Klinik Schloss Mammern, embedded in an impressive landscape, is a leading private clinic for every phase of rehabilitation and has been run since 1889 as a family business in the meantime 4th generation. With around 440 dedicated employees, we offer first-class medical care and therapy in four disciplines: cardiological, internistic-oncological, musculoskeletal and pneumological. Our patients enjoy an upscale hotel industry at five-star level in a stylish atmosphere and with the latest infrastructure. The personal and heartfelt treatment of patients is important to us and we attach great importance to a long-term and mutually enriching cooperation. We offer from the 1.
