Full description

As a succession regulation we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement one or more

Head of department/in Tariffs & IBI-care

100%

They bring management experience in the Swiss healthcare sector and understand how to perfectly combine leadership with active participation in day-to-day business. Then you can expect a technically demanding task in an experienced team that lives cooperation, reliability and mutual support.

They shall take over

  • Professional and disciplinary management of the Tariffs and IBI-care department with 5 employees
  • Resource and goal management of the department
  • Control, optimization and further development of department-internal processes
  • Targeted promotion and coaching of the team
  • Close exchange and cooperation with the other teams and areas of patient administration, performance recording, IT and other internal specialist areas
  • Ensuring smooth application operation of IBI-care as well as error analysis and troubleshooting
  • Professional participation and responsibility for the correct application of the tariff and central contact person for ambulatory hospital tariffs
  • Project management, partial project management and participation in projects
  • Preparation of evaluations and analyses
  • Participation in the further development of new ambulant tariff structures (TARDOC / ambulant packages)

They bring with them

  • Completed training or studies in business administration, health care or comparable qualifications
  • Leadership experience in a comparable leadership position is a plus
  • Strength of communication and a professional approach to different internal and external stakeholders
  • assertiveness, strategic thinking and solution-oriented working methods
  • Experience in the healthcare or hospital environment with an understanding of clinical processes and processes
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss tariff system (TARDOC, amb.) Flat-rate, KVG, UVG, IV, MTK
  • Enjoy comprehensible preparation of complex tariff requirements and transfer them into practice
  • Good IT affinity, knowledge of SAP IS-H, IBIcare are a plus
  • Team play and a resilient, self-responsible personality

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
