Companies … Job Portal Installation of natural stone Installation of natural stone They supply, tolerate and assemble natural stone covers and rear walls according to plan. This also includes sinks, batteries, hobs, miters, cheeks and clean flat and hollow joints. After assembly, you impregnate and polish the work, perform service and warranty work and check the result on site. You fit well to this task if you have experience in stone processing, work exactly and enjoy the natural product. The most important in short Place of work Matzingen (TG) Operational area throughout Switzerland Pensum 100 %

Responsibilities

  • Supply, contracts and installation of granite covers and rear walls according to the plan
  • Installation of sinks, spouts, batteries and hobs
  • Adhesion of miters and cheeks
  • Preparation of flat and hollow joints
  • Impregnation of all work
  • Performance of polishing work
  • Execution of service and guarantee work
  • Final inspection and ensuring professional work

What you bring

  • Completed training in the professional field of stone processing
  • Some years of professional experience in the stone industry
  • Gluing of miters and cheeks – knowledge is a mandatory requirement
  • Exact and independent working methods as well as high customer orientation
  • Joy of craftsmanship and natural product
