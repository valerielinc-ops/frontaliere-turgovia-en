Back Internship Human Resources and Disposition – Permanent Employment Option Internship / Frauenfeld Start with HR! An internship that offers more: versatile tasks, real insights and an introduction to the exciting world of human resources and disposition. Do you not only want to watch, but actively participate – in dealing with people, in planning and organizing? And learn how modern recruiting really works? Then you are exactly right with us! In your internship, we accompany you step by step – from recruitment to planning and scheduling to administrative implementation. You not only get a comprehensive insight, but also learn how to keep an overview and think in everyday life.

What you should bring

  • Completed basic training (advantageous)
  • You are interested in HR topics, planning, organization and want to learn more about the recruiting and planning processes
  • Joy of organization, planning and dealing with people
  • You write and speak stylish German
  • Foreign language skills are a plus, but not a must

What we offer

  • Above average and fair remuneration during the internship
  • 5 weeks of holidays per year
  • Free parking directly at the workplace
  • Multifaceted internship with insights
  • Prospect of permanent employment after successful internship
  • Thorough training as part of a structured on-the-job training
  • Internship for 6 months or 12 months
  • A friendly and helpful team
