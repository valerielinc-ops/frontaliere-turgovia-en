About ALDI SUISSE AG

ALDI SUISSE is a Swiss retailer headquartered in Schwarzenbach (SG). It is part of the ALDI SÜD Group, which has locations in eleven countries around the world. Since entering the market in 2005, ALDI SUISSE has been continuously expanding its network of stores throughout Switzerland. The company currently operates 247 stores and employs around 4,000 people. The product range stands out for its top quality at the best price. On ALDI's shelves, you will find high-quality products from Switzerland, a wide range of fresh produce and the highest organic standard on the Swiss market. ALDI SUISSE also stands for fairness, both towards its customers and partners and towards its employees.
