Full description

You are a qualified nurse HF/FH and are looking for a new professional challenge in the seeking setting? From 1 January 2027 we are looking for a dedicated reinforcement for our External Psychiatric Service (EPD) in Romanshorn as

Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH External Psychiatric Service Romanshorn

50%

Are you looking for a meaningful task with responsibility and scope for design? In our External Psychiatric Service you work close to the people and are supported by an experienced, strong and multi-professional team. You like to work independently and accompany patients mindfully and at eye level in the sense of the recovery approach in their home environment.

You take over

  • Seeking support, counselling and treatment of mentally stressed people in their usual living environment
  • Creating target- and solution-oriented nursing diagnoses based on the recovery approach
  • Management of psychological crises and somatic side effects
  • Support, advice and guidance in dealing with social problems
  • Development and practice of an adapted and health-promoting daily structure
  • Coordination and coordination of necessary therapeutic, nursing and complementary measures
  • For employees with a part-time workload, there is the possibility of an additional, flexible work in the employee pool

You bring with you

  • Completed nursing training at tertiary level (HF, PsyKP, DN ll)
  • You have mandatory experience in psychiatric care
  • Working independently, high reliability
  • Empathy, social skills and resilience
  • Enjoy working in a multi-professional team
  • Due to the visiting activity, a separate vehicle registered in Switzerland is required

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau introduce themselves

The Outpatient Adult Psychiatry (AEP) together with the Psychiatric Clinic Münsterlingen (PKM) is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of the adult population in Thurgau. Every year, we treat more than 7,500 patients and offer specialized outpatient, outreach and daily clinical services in Münsterlingen, Weinfelden, Kreuzlingen, Frauenfeld and Romanshorn. An appreciative culture combined with an excellent education and training make the AEP as part of the Psychiatric Services Thurgau a highly attractive employer.
